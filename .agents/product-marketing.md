## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** "I stopped guessing what to study." Learners waste time on generic prep not aligned to the specific Gulf/international exam gating their career.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Foreign question banks aren't aligned to Gulf licensing exams (OMSB, Prometric, DHA).
- Generic tools don't adapt to the learner's weak areas.
- AI-only content is not clinically trustworthy for high-stakes medicine.
**What it costs them:** Failed exams delay or block a Gulf medical career; wasted study months; money on ill-fitting tools.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of failing a career-gating exam; doubt about whether prep is actually working; distrust of AI in medicine.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Foreign/global medical QBanks (UWorld, AMBOSS-style) — fall short: not Gulf-exam-aligned, not Oman-built, no local credibility.
**Secondary:** Generic study apps / flashcard tools — fall short: no medical-content governance, no exam-blueprint alignment.
**Indirect:** Local agencies producing generic social content (e.g. Media Solutions SPC, 145 OMR/mo) — fall short: marketing output, not a learning product; no outcome tie.
