## Purpose

Define who Bayan is for — precisely enough that a stranger could sort a lead in or out — and
turn that definition into named personas the whole team uses the same way.

---

## Scope

**In Scope:**
- The ICP boundary (who is in, who is out)
- The anti-persona (who Bayan will not serve)
- Named persona cards with job-to-be-done, VOC, triggers, objections
- Persona sign-off and versioning

**Out of Scope:**
- The segmentation matrix and fit scoring (Core 216)
- Messaging per persona (Proc 5)
- Channel/content planning (Proc 9)

## Procedure

### 1. Draw the ICP Boundary

**Frequency:** Process start, then annual
**Owner:** CEO (decision), Marketer (drafting)

**Steps:**

1.1. State the ICP in one sentence a stranger could apply:

> **Medical professionals and students preparing for a licensing or board exam** —
> doctors, residents, medical students, and nurses sitting Gulf/international exams.

1.2. State the anti-persona just as plainly:

> **NOT the general public, consumer-health learners, or patient education.**

1.3. **The boundary is binary.** A proposed audience is either inside or an anti-persona.
There is no "ICP-lite." Blurring the edge produces a product that serves no one sharply —
this is a CEO-owned red-line.

**Output:** A one-line in/out rule, CEO-signed

---

### 2. Collect Voice of Customer

**Frequency:** Continuous
**Owner:** Marketer (synthesis), Support Lead (source)

**Steps:**

2.1. Pull real language from support tickets, sales calls, and the EMPOWER/VOC notes. Record
the phrase as said, with its source.

2.2. **Never invent a quote.** A fabricated VOC line looks like evidence and is the opposite.
If no real quote exists for a persona, that persona stays a hypothesis.

2.3. Grounded anchors already captured:
- IMG self-funder: *"I stopped guessing what to study."*
- (collect the rest before promoting any persona to canonical)

**Output:** VOC quotes tagged by persona and source

---

### 3. Write the Persona Cards

**Frequency:** Quarterly or on new evidence
**Owner:** Marketer

**Steps:**

3.1. One card per persona, using form **F4-1 (Persona Card)**. Each card carries:

| Field | Example (IMG self-funder) |
|-------|---------------------------|
| Name + role | "Amir, IMG physician self-funder" |
| Motion | A (self-serve B2C) |
| Exam track | OMSB / DHA / SCFHS |
| Job-to-be-done (VOC) | "I stopped guessing what to study" |
| Trigger | Exam date booked |
| Primary objection | "Is this aligned to *my* exam?" |
| Anti-signals | General-interest, no exam date |

3.2. The four canonical personas (grounded in `product-marketing.html` + `cores-draft.md`):
IMG self-funder, Resident/student, Program director (B2B champion), Hospital/university
(financial buyer).

3.3. Clinical Reviewer checks each card for exam-track and career-stage realism (a "resident
sitting NCLEX-RN" is a clinical error, not a persona).

**Output:** Persona cards, Clinical-Reviewer-checked

---

### 4. Sign Off and Version

**Frequency:** On each card change
**Owner:** CEO (sign-off), Marketer (versioning)

**Steps:**

4.1. CEO signs each canonical persona. Until signed, a card is labelled **HYPOTHESIS** and
teams treat it as provisional.

4.2. Version persona cards. When a persona changes materially, bump the version and note what
the evidence was — personas drift as the market is understood, and the team needs to know
which version a campaign was built against.

4.3. Publish canonical personas where Proc 5 and Proc 9 consume them by name.

**Output:** Signed, versioned, published persona cards

## Edge Cases

**Case 1: A lucrative audience sits outside the ICP**
- **Cause:** Revenue temptation (e.g. a consumer-health course)
- **Solution:** Reject per the boundary. Log it as an anti-persona example so it stops
  recurring. Escalate to CEO only if a genuine strategy change is on the table.

**Case 2: A persona has strong demand but no VOC**
- **Cause:** Inferred from traffic, not heard
- **Solution:** Keep as HYPOTHESIS. Route Support to collect the language before it becomes
  canonical.

**Case 3: Two personas collapse into one in practice**
- **Cause:** Over-segmentation
- **Solution:** Merge. Fewer sharp personas beat many blurry ones.

**Case 4: Oman learner persona**
- **Cause:** Free-access nuance
- **Solution:** Model them as a normal segment with a free entitlement via
  grandfathering/institution — NOT a separate "Oman free" persona, and never described as
  "free in Oman" (Oman is not in the free-country geo set).
