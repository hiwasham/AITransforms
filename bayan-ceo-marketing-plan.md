## 4. Strategy — one sentence per pillar

1. **Website & content (lead):** publish clinician-reviewed pages targeting the
   low-difficulty question-bank keywords above — the direct revenue path.
2. **SEO (compound):** FAQ schema, trimmed metadata, BLUF style, faster mobile load —
   make those pages rank and get cited.

   Bayan; the weekly tracker proves movement.

which also lifted the Magister plan out of its earlier degraded compile state.
