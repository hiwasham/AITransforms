# Social Cadence Starter — X + Telegram

**One evidence-led clinical post per weekday, each tied to a real question or
blueprint topic, cross-posted to X (@Medresearch_om) and Telegram (BayanMedEd).**
This is a repeatable template plus 10 seed posts — nothing is scheduled or
published; social publishing is held for explicit confirmation.

## The weekday template

| Day | Post type | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Clinical pearl | One high-yield fact from a blueprint topic |
| Tue | Question of the day | Stem + options; answer + rationale in replies/next post |
| Wed | Drug monograph tip | One drug, one exam-relevant point |
| Thu | Exam/authority explainer | How one Gulf authority's exam works (DHA, SCFHS, OMSB…) |
| Fri | Acid-base / calculator | A calculation walk-through (ABG, anion gap, compensation) |

Rules: name the exam, cite the reasoning, never promise a pass, keep to one idea.

## 10 seed posts (2 weeks of Q-of-the-day + pearls)

1. **(Tue)** ABG shows pH 7.28, PaCO₂ 30, HCO₃⁻ 14. Primary disorder? → Metabolic
   acidosis with respiratory compensation. Check the anion gap next. #SMLE #OMSB
2. **(Mon)** Pearl: In suspected DKA, the anion gap tracks treatment response
   better than glucose. Blueprint: endocrine/acute medicine.
3. **(Wed)** Drug tip: Metformin is held before contrast in renal impairment —
   lactic-acidosis risk. Common Gulf-licensing pharmacology item.
4. **(Thu)** How the SMLE works: SCFHS-administered, Prometric-delivered, broad
   clinical scope. Prep to the blueprint, not to a topic you like.
5. **(Fri)** Winter's formula: expected PaCO₂ = 1.5×HCO₃⁻ + 8 (±2). If actual is
   higher, there's a concurrent respiratory acidosis.
6. **(Tue)** ECG: long PR, dropped beat, PR lengthens progressively. Diagnosis? →
   Mobitz I (Wenckebach). Usually benign. #MRCP
7. **(Mon)** Pearl: First-line for stable SVT is vagal manoeuvres, then adenosine.
   High-yield across licensing blueprints.
8. **(Thu)** OMSB is Oman's pathway under the Oman Medical Specialty Board — the
   home exam for candidates practising in Oman.
9. **(Wed)** Drug tip: ACE inhibitors + potassium-sparing diuretics → watch for
   hyperkalaemia. Frequent safety-item pairing.
10. **(Fri)** Anion gap = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻). >12 suggests a high-anion-gap
    metabolic acidosis — think MUDPILES.
