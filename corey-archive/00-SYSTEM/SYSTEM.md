# The System

Corey's whole playbook, assembled from the three sources of this archive. Every step links back to the source lesson, video, or resource.

## The model in one paragraph

Build a sellable AI offer in 7 days — no coding or capital required (group description). The offer is the **AI Assessment**: a fixed-scope audit where you map a business owner's workflows and show them where AI makes them money, saves time, or improves customer experience, for a flat fee (Day 1 lesson). Sell it, fulfill it, reinvest into retention offers (AI Concierge, second-brain builds), then compound via case studies and content.
