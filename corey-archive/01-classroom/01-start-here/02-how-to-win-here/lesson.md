# How to Win Here

This place works if you work it. Here's how.

**Rule 1:** Post like an operator. Wins, questions, and what you're building. No spam, no pitching your services to other members, no DM bait. We remove that stuff fast.

**Rule 2:** Comments count. The fastest way to get value here is to give it. Answer one question a day and watch what happens to your network.

**Rule 3: **Ship, then share. Every Sprint day ends with an action and a post. The members who post their progress finish. The ones who lurk usually don't.

**Quick note on levels: **Skool gives you points when people like your posts and comments. Higher levels will unlock perks as this community grows. You level up by being useful, not by hanging around.

One more thing. If you're stuck on anything, post it in the community with the word Stuck in the title. Me or another operator will get you moving.
