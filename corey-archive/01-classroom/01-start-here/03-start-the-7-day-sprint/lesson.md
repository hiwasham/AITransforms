# Start the 7-Day Sprint

The 7-Day Sprint is the fastest way to go from watching AI content to having a real client conversation on your calendar.

It's a condensed version of Month 1 inside [AI Operator Academy](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about), where members follow a 30-day plan to land their first paying AI services client. The Sprint takes the first stretch of that system and compresses it into 7 days.

Here's the model in one line: you offer a paid AI Assessment, a fixed-scope audit of a business's workflows that shows the owner exactly where AI saves them time and makes them money. Assessments first. Retainers later.

### What the 7 days look like:

**Day 1:** The model and your money math.

**Day 2:** How an assessment works end to end.

**Day 3: **Build your 10-person warm list.

**Day 4: **Send your first 5 outreach messages.

**Day 5:** Learn the discovery questions.

**Day 6: **Follow up and book the conversation.

**Day 7: **Graduate and plan your next 30 days.

Each day takes 15 to 30 minutes. One short lesson, one action, one post in the community so we can hold you accountable.

### **Two rules for the Sprint:**

**1) Do the action before you consume anything else.** Watching more content is how people hide from the work.

**2) Post every day.** Wins, stumbles, whatever. The post is part of the rep.
