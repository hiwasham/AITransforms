# Day 1: The Model and Your Money Math

### Objective: Understand the model you're going to run for the next 7 days.

Most people try to start an AI business by learning tools. Wrong order. You start by selling one simple offer, and that offer is the AI Assessment.

An AI Assessment is a fixed-scope audit of a business. You sit down with the owner, map their workflows, and show them exactly where AI makes them money, saves them time, or improves their customer experience. They pay a flat fee. Inside [AI Operator Academy](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about) we teach members to charge $999 for it.

Why assessments first:

It's easy to say yes to. A flat fee and a clear deliverable beats a vague monthly retainer when nobody knows you yet.

It requires zero code. The deliverable is clarity, not software.

It leads to bigger work. The assessment surfaces the projects. Retainers come later.

Now the money math. 4 assessments a month at $999 is roughly $4K a month, before a single retainer. That's the whole starting model. Simple enough to run this week, real enough to change your income.

### Today's action: 

Write your money math on one line. How many assessments a month, at what price, equals the monthly income you want to achieve selling AI services.
