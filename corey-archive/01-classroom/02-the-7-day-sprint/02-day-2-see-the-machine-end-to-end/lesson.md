# Day 2: See the Machine End to End

### Objective: Know every step of an assessment before you sell one.

Here's the entire machine, start to finish:

**Step 1:** Discovery call. 45 to 60 minutes with the owner. You ask questions and map how the business actually runs.

**Step 2: **AI-powered analysis. You feed your notes into AI and turn them into findings. Where time gets lost, where money leaks, what to fix first.

**Step 3: **The report. A short, clear document that ranks their opportunities by impact and effort. No 40-page decks.

**Step 4:** The review call. You walk them through the report, they see the value, and you hand them a path forward.

That's it. No coding, no complicated tech stack. Your job is to ask good questions, use AI to do the heavy analysis, and deliver clarity.

Notice what this means. The skill you need this week is not building agents. It's having a good conversation with a business owner. You can do that.

Inside [AI Operator Academy,](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about) members get the full playbook for each step, including the report templates and the review call structure. For this Sprint, you just need to understand the shape of the machine.
