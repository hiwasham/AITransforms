# Day 3: Build Your 10-Person Warm List

### Objective: List 10 people who already know you and own or run a business.

Cold outreach works, but it's slow when you have zero proof. Warm outreach is how you get real conversations this week.

Open your phone contacts, your LinkedIn connections, and your text threads. You're looking for:

- Business owners you know personally.

- Friends or family who run or manage a business.

- Past coworkers or clients who went out on their own.

- People from your gym, church, or neighborhood who own something local.

Write down 10 names. For each one, note their business and one workflow you'd bet is eating their time. Admin, follow-up, scheduling, content, invoicing. Every business has at least one.

Do not filter the list by whether they'd buy. You're not selling yet. You're starting conversations.

Inside [AI Operator Academy,](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about) members build a 30-person list and work it across 30 days with scripts for every stage. You're running the 10-person version to get moving this week.
