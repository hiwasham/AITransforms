# Day 4: Send Your First 5 Messages

### Objective: Send 5 warm outreach messages today.

Here's the script. Text or DM beats email if you have the option:

```
"Hey [name], quick question. I've been going deep on AI for business over the
last few months,and I'm doing a handful of AI assessments where I map out 
exactly where AI can save a business owner real time and money. I immediately 
thought of [their business]. Any interest in being one of my first? No charge,
I just want the reps."
```

Why this works:

It's honest. You're new and you say so. That reads as authentic, not desperate.

It's specific. An assessment with a clear outcome, not "can I pick your brain."

It's free, for now. Your first one or two are free in exchange for a testimonial and the experience. Then you flip to paid. That's exactly how we sequence it inside [AI Operator Academy](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about): free assessment, proof, then $999.

Send it to 5 people from your Day 3 list. Personalize the first line, keep the rest tight, hit send before you feel ready.

Some people will ignore you. Fine. Follow-up comes on Day 6 and that's where most of the yeses live. Some will ask what an AI assessment is. Perfect, that's a conversation, and tomorrow you'll learn exactly what to ask.

### Today's action: 

Send 5 messages. Then post Sprint Day 4 in the community with how many you sent and the best response so far, even if the best response is silence.
