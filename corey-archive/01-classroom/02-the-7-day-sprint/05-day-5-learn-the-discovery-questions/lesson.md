# Day 5: Learn the Discovery Questions

### Objective: Know the questions that turn a friendly chat into a real assessment conversation.

Every AI opportunity in a business falls into one of three buckets. Inside **[AI Operator Academy](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about)** we call them the Three Outcomes:

**Effectiveness:** make more money. Leads, follow-up, conversion, marketing.

**Efficiency: **save time. Admin, scheduling, reporting, repetitive work.

**Quality: **improve the product or the customer experience. Response times, consistency, communication.

Your discovery questions map straight to those buckets. Here are the 5 core ones:

**Question 1: **"Walk me through what a normal week looks like in the business."

**Question 2: **"What's the task you or your team redo over and over that makes you want to scream?"

**Question 3:** "Where do leads come from, and what happens in the first hour after one shows up?"

**Question 4: **"If I handed you back 10 hours a week, where would you spend them?"

**Question 5:** "What part of the customer experience do you know could be better?"

Ask, then be quiet and take notes. The owner will hand you the entire assessment if you let them talk. Your notes become the raw material AI turns into findings on Day 2's machine.

### Today's action: 

Pick one business you know well, yours or a friend's, and answer all 5 questions for it.
