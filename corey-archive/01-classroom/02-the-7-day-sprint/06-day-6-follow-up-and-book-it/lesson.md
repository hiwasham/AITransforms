# Day 6: Follow Up and Book It

### Objective: Follow up with everyone who hasn't replied, and push warm replies toward a booked time.

Here's a truth worth writing on your wall: most yeses show up after the follow-up, not the first message. One message is not outreach. It's a whisper.

The follow-up script:

```
"Hey [name], floating this back up. I've got 2 assessment slots left this month
 and I'd love for one to be yours. 45 minutes, and you'll walk away with a clear
picture of where AI can save you time even if we never talk again. Worth a look?"
```

Notice what it adds. Real scarcity, because you genuinely have limited slots. And a standalone payoff, because they get value even if nothing else ever happens.

For anyone who already replied with interest, stop chatting and offer times: "Awesome. I've got Tuesday at 2 or Thursday at 10. Which works better?"

Booked calls beat interested maybes. Push for the calendar every time.

Inside [AI Operator Academy](https://www.skool.com/aioperatoracademy/about) there's a full Follow-Up Formula with timing, scripts, and objection handling. This is the starter version, and it's enough to book your first conversation.
