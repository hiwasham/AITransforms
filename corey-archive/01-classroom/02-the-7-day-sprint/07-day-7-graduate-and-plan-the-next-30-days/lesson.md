# Day 7: Graduate and Plan the Next 30 Days

### Objective: Lock in what you built this week and decide what happens next.

Run your numbers from the week:

- 10 names listed

- 5 or more messages sent

- Follow-ups out

Conversations started, and maybe an assessment booked.

If you booked one: huge. Prep with the Day 5 questions, take ruthless notes, and deliver clarity. Then post about it. We want to see it.

If you didn't book one yet: you're not behind, you're early. The list and the reps compound. Keep sending 5 messages a week and the first conversation will come. Most members who stick with the loop book within a few weeks.
