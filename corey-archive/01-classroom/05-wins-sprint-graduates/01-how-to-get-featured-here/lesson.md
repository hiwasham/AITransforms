# How to Get Featured Here

This course is the proof shelf. Real people, real numbers.

### Two ways to get on it:

**Way 1:** Graduate the Sprint. Finish all 7 days and make your Sprint Graduate post with your numbers for the week. Every month we pull the new graduates into the Sprint Graduates lesson in this course.

**Way 2: **Post a win. Booked your first assessment? Got paid? Automated something that gave you your Saturday back? Post it in the community with the Win tag. Include the numbers. Wins with numbers get featured, vague wins don't.

Why this matters for you, not just for me: featured members get visibility, and visibility is what brings referrals, partners, and clients your way.

Go do the work, then come claim your spot.
