# Sam from Metro Detroit.

Sam from Metro Detroit.

Day job is Microsoft, Global Solutions Architect on the AI Business Solutions
side. 20 years in enterprise IT architecture before that, Walmart, Comerica,
AARP, that kind of work. So I have spent a long time watching big
companies try to actually adopt this stuff, not just buy it.

Building SamJak AI Consulting on the side. One client right now, a law firm,
AI strategy and implementation. Discovery call, then a report and
recommendations, then ongoing. That one honestly came through a recruiter, not
from anything I built.

Where I am headed is multi-location operators, 3 or more sites. Med spas,
dental groups, boutique fitness franchises, that kind of thing. The pattern I
keep seeing is that location three is where everything breaks. Nobody owns the
process across buildings, reporting gets built by hand every month, and the
whole thing runs on one GM who remembers how it works. That is a problem I know
how to fix and it is basically the same problem I solved at enterprise scale,
just smaller.

The assessment offer framework from Corey's videos is where my audit came from,
so that is what brought me here.

My biggest question: my one client came from a recruiter. Everything I built to
generate clients, content, lists, outreach, has produced nothing. For those of
you who got to client 2 and 3, what actually worked? I am not great at cold
outreach and I have maybe 15 hours a week, so I am trying to figure out where
to put them.
