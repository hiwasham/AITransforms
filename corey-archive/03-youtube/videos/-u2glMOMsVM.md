Key Points

Perplexity Computer completed most tasks 5 to 10 times faster than Manus — the Gmail and Calendar task took 60 seconds versus roughly 10 minutes.

Both tools pulled identical AI funding data, and both independently combined Anthropic's Series E and F rounds into the correct $16.5B total rather than just reporting the latest round.

Manus failed to format the Google Doc deliverable as a table despite the prompt explicitly requesting one — Perplexity nailed the formatting on the first try.

For the pricing comparison task, both tools independently recommended ClickUp's Business plan as the best value after scraping live data from five project management websites.

Manus produced a slightly better-looking website design in the first test, suggesting it may have an edge for visual or creative deliverables.

Perplexity Computer's $200/month Max plan is significantly more expensive than Manus's $20/month Pro plan, but the speed and output quality difference may justify the cost for power users.

Section Summaries

1. Multi-Source Research and Website Deployment
I gave both tools a single prompt to research the five most funded AI startups of 2025, compile the data, and deploy a live website with a shareable URL. Both nailed the accuracy — the funding figures matched TechCrunch exactly. Manus took five times longer but produced a slightly more polished website design.

2. Connected App Integration and Document Formatting
This test revealed the biggest gap between the two tools. Perplexity Computer read my Gmail, cross-referenced my Google Calendar, and produced a cleanly formatted Google Doc with a proper table in about 60 seconds. Manus completed the same task in 10 minutes but delivered a jumbled document without the table we requested.

3. Live Data Scraping and Visualization
Both tools scraped pricing from five project management websites, built comparison spreadsheets with source URLs, generated bar charts, and recommended ClickUp as the best value. This was the closest test — Manus actually finished slightly faster, and both deliverables were nearly identical in quality.
