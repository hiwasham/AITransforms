Key Points

• The AI assessment is a $999 paid 45-minute diagnostic that requires zero AI expertise to sell — you're just uncovering bottlenecks and recommending off-the-shelf tools, not building anything.

• 50 to 60 percent of assessment clients convert into a bigger implementation, which is why it should always be the first offer you sell.

• AI concierge retainers price at $1,000 to $2,000 a month for two 45-minute calls, which works out to $750 to $1,250 an hour because pricing is anchored to hours saved for the client, not hours worked.

• Never automate a broken process — a $3,500 process redesign that cuts a 25-step workflow to 10 steps should always come before automation.

• A wedding venue business paid $1,500 for a simple Zapier automation that eliminated a manual Asana onboarding task entirely, with zero behavior change required from the team.

• Knowledge systems, like a custom GPT trained on a business broker's listing docs, dropped inbound buyer emails from 500 to 10 and price at a $3,000 baseline because the value compounds over time.

• Full implementations should only be pitched after five to ten smaller projects, price at $5,000 to $10,000 plus, and typically carry a monthly retainer worth 10 percent of the build price.
