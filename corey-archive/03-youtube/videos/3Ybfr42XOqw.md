Key Points

Perplexity Computer runs 19 LLM agents in parallel in the cloud — no local machine required. You pick your lead model (Claude Opus, GPT-5, Sonnet) and it selects supporting models underneath.

Eliot built a full local SEO audit machine from just three prompts. It runs technical audits, Google Business Profile audits, and on-page analysis simultaneously — work that traditionally takes weeks.

The audit doesn't just report problems — it writes the deliverables (schema markup, page copy, meta titles) and can push changes directly to WordPress through a built-in connector.

Perplexity Computer's connector library includes LinkedIn (via Linkup API), WordPress, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and a rapid URL indexer — letting you chain entire workflows conversationally.

The LinkedIn lead machine Eliot built reads DMs, scores leads as hot or warm, and drafts replies — turning LinkedIn from a time sink into an intelligence dashboard you check in 2 minutes.

Treat AI like a smart intern, not a washing machine. If the output isn't right, give it more context, frameworks, and feedback rather than abandoning the tool.
