Summaries

1. Building a Zapier Agent from Scratch in Minutes

Tom demonstrates creating an agent with a simple natural language prompt: "Look for emails from or about Orchestrated Connecting in my Gmail. At the end of every week, send me a Slack message with a general summary and link me to them." Zapier parses this into a weekly trigger, Gmail search action, and Slack message step. The co-pilot fills out instructions and adds actions automatically. Connect your accounts through OAuth, set your preferred schedule, and the agent is ready to test. What would have taken significant setup time now happens in under five minutes with natural language input.

2. Agents vs. Traditional Zaps: When to Use Each

Zapier agents and traditional Zaps share the same underlying infrastructure—identical API routes, integrations, and capabilities. The difference is control and reliability. Use agents as your MVP: prove a workflow is possible and functional before investing time in a more granular build. Once validated, convert to a traditional Zap for precise control over each step, better error handling, and more predictable execution. Think of agents as rapid prototyping tools. If the agent accomplishes the task, you've proven the concept works within Zapier's platform. Then dial it in with a custom Zap.

3. Free Resource: Compare Automation Platforms Instantly

X-Ray built search.xray.tech—a free tool that indexes all triggers, actions, and searches available across Zapier, Make, N8N, Workato, Bardeen, and Power Automate. Search for any tool (Gmail, Slack, HubSpot) and instantly see what automations are possible on each platform. The top 100 SaaS tools have nearly identical support across platforms, but niche industry tools (legal, real estate, construction) often have better support on specific platforms.
