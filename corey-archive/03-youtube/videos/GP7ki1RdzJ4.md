Key Points

• The magic wand question ("if you could fix any one problem in your business, what would it be?") is the single highest-leverage question in the free assessment — it almost always surfaces email as the top pain point, which is why SaneBox is the most-prescribed tool.

• Pain points fall into three buckets that determine the prescription: high-frequency/high-friction gets an off-the-shelf tool, judgment-based tasks get Claude Cowork, and proprietary workflows get a custom Claude Skill.

• Pricing the AI Tools Assessment at $200 or $500 got people to buy but not to implement — raising it to $1,000 was the unlock that made clients actually take action, because they had a financial stake in the outcome.

• The AI Concierge retainer runs on two 45-minute monthly working sessions plus unlimited Voxer access with a 12-business-hour response guarantee, yet across 3 months and 5 clients only 4 messages ever came in outside calls.

• Four Claude skills — intake-to-project-files, AI Concierge call update, AI Concierge follow-up email, and an orchestrator that chains the last two — automate the entire backend, cutting admin time to under 5 minutes per client per week.

• The blended hourly rate across the current client roster is close to $1,250/hour, built by raising the price with every single yes: $1,200, then $1,500, $1,500, $1,800, and $2,000 a month with zero pushback.

• The fastest path to a first client requires no money or audience: text 10 people you already know offering a free 15-minute mini assessment — roughly 6 will take the meeting and 1-2 will convert to the paid $999 assessment.
