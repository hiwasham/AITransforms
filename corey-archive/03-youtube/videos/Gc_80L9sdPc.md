Key Points

 • The whole system is folders and markdown files. No code, no platform lock-in. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Hermes, Codex, anything
. • Delivery is a zip file: operations manual plus installer dot MD. The AI reads the installer and builds everything. Zero fulfillment work.
 • Jobs to be done framework: figure out the jobs in any business, make a folder per job with instructions, and you have a sellable system for any niche.
 • Every agent needs three things: memory, instructions, and tools. Folders and files give you all three. • Sales advice: use a slide deck on every call. By the fifth or sixth time it's dialed in and you can hand it to a hire.

Sections

THE SYSTEM – CMO HQ has four sections: HQ for identity, brands for per-brand intelligence and metrics, finance for real P&L reporting, shared for collaboration. All markdown.
THE DELIVERY MODEL – Zip file with an operations manual and installer dot MD. Customer hands the installer to any AI and it builds everything in one session. Sales page to zip file, done.
HOW TO BUILD YOUR OWN – Start with jobs to be done, build folders and instructions with Claude, package as a zip with an installer, sell from a sales page. The people who want to build it themselves are not your customers.
