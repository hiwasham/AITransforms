Key Points

Build a fully functional AI social media agent — daily scheduling, image generation, multi-platform formatting, email delivery — with one plain-English prompt in 15 minutes. No coding required.

Appy builds a real web app with a GUI, sign-up pages, user preferences, and Stripe integration — not just a backend automation.

AI content quality depends on your inputs. Kevin trains his agent on his website and a brand philosophy document. That's what separates thought leadership from AI slop.

The value isn't summarizing news — it's layering YOUR perspective on top. Kevin's agent finds stories, then applies his philosophy to create posts that build his brand.

Updating is conversational. Kevin went from LinkedIn-only to four platforms by typing one sentence. No node editing, no breaking things.

You can deploy and monetize agents with one click — sell them, enable your team, or build niche tools for specific industries.

Section Summaries

1. The Evolution of Automation Tools
Kevin walks through the history — from manual coding and APIs, to Zapier, to Make.com's visual nodes, to where we are now: AI agents built from plain English. I pull up an N8N automation to show just how different the build experience is.

2. Building and Testing the Agent Live
Kevin shows the exact prompt he used, including how he trained it on his website and brand docs. We watch Appy build the app in real time, then I sign up and test it myself — changing the niche to fitness. The agent finds a New York Post article about peptides and generates a complete post in seconds.

3. Apps, Not Just Automations
Appy doesn't just build a workflow — it builds a real web app with a UI, preferences panel, sign-up page, and Stripe integration. Kevin shows how updating is as simple as chatting with it, and we discuss deploying agents as tools, niche products, or standalone software.

4. Practical AI for Entrepreneurs
We zoom out on why this matters for non-developers. What would have cost $10-20K and weeks of dev time can now be built in 15 minutes. Kevin shares why he built this agent: consistent social media presence without sacrificing video production time.
