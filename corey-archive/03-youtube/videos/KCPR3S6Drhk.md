Key Points

• The full offer ladder is free mini assessment (15 minutes, one pain point, one tool) to $999 paid assessment (three to seven tools prescribed) to a $1,000–$2,000/month AI concierge retainer.

• You only need four AI concierge clients at $1,500/month plus four $999 paid assessments per month to hit $10K/month.

• Your only cost of goods is a $20/month Claude subscription, making this a roughly 98% net margin business with no team required.

• AI concierge fulfillment is just two 45-minute calls a month, which works out to $667–$1,333 an hour depending on what you charge.

• Send the exact text script to 10 people from your existing network and you'll typically get 6 responses, 3 mini assessments, and 1–2 paid clients.

• Never anchor AI concierge pricing to hours spent, anchor it to hours saved, and never price the offer below $1,000/month.

• Every "no" is a referral opportunity. Asking every contact for two referrals, whether they buy or not, is how you never run out of warm leads.
