 Key Points

• The pre-call intake form is a hard requirement — if a client hasn't filled it out before call one, reschedule. It surfaces time sinks, bottlenecks, and gives you one to three AI opportunities before you ever get on the clock.

• This is a done-WITH-you engagement, not done-for-you. You're on calls building Claude skills and workflows alongside the client — upselling to fully done-for-you is a separate add-on.

• The AOA framework (Audit, Optimize, Automate) keeps you from jumping straight to automation. Fix the process first, then hand it to Claude — automating a broken workflow just breaks things faster.

• Two Claude skills handle all post-call admin in 30 seconds: one fills out the Notion call log from the transcript, the other pulls action items and drafts a follow-up email to the client.

• When you're closing 100% of your sales calls, your price is too low. Corey raised from $1,200 to $1,500 to $2,000/month after every new client said yes without hesitation.

• At $1,500/month for two 45-minute calls, the effective hourly rate is $1,000/hour — and the Notion hub gives clients a running list of every skill and automation built, making that rate easy to justify at renewal.
