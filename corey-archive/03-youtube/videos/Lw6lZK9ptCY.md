Key Points

• I had Codex generate five AI service business ideas for non-technical founders, then combined two of them, appointment recovery and review growth, into a single bundled offer.

• I explicitly told Codex to apply the frameworks from Alex Hormozi's $100 Million Offers to make the pricing and value stack feel like a no-brainer instead of just another marketing pitch.

• Codex built a live, hosted two-page website on Codex Sites, complete with a working "book a consultation" button, in about three minutes, something that would have cost roughly $5,000 and three weeks a few years ago.

• The lead-gen research happened entirely inside Codex: it browsed Google Maps directly, found 10 local businesses with strong reviews but no website, and sorted them by proximity to my neighborhood so I could walk the route in order.

• The lowest-hanging-fruit leads are mom-and-pop service businesses with great Google reviews but no website and no way to book online, meaning every missed call or unanswered lead is money left on the table.

• You can run this entire workflow from your phone using Codex's remote feature, meaning you could build a business idea, an offer, a website, and a lead list while driving or on a walk.

• I didn't touch a keyboard once during this entire build, from idea to offer to live website to a sorted, verified lead list ready to walk in and pitch.
