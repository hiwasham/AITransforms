Key Points

• Mark's part-time AI consulting income surpassed the $500K ARR his 7-year, $8M-raised SaaS company had built up, and he shut the company down to go all in on AI training and implementation.

• His first public workshop charged $500 a head, was marketed only through Eventbrite and LinkedIn DMs, and pulled in 15 people with zero existing audience.

• His pricing framework: estimate the low, mid, and best-case value a client will realize from adopting what you teach, then charge roughly 20% of that number.

• His full offer ladder runs free artifact, then paid workshop or assessment ($15-25K), then a custom build ($25-50K over three to six weeks), then an optional monthly retainer.

• He deliberately avoids collecting maintenance retainers, since for him that signals he failed to actually train the client's team to run the system themselves.

• He turns down workshops with companies that are either too AI-sophisticated already or show no realistic path to a follow-up build, preferring to reinvest that time in higher-value clients.
