Summaries

1. The Research Task: Invoice Automation Tools Report

Both tools received identical instructions: research three free or low-cost AI tools that help solopreneurs automate invoice follow-up. Find the name, pricing, one key feature, and one real user review for each. Compile everything into a one-page PDF with a professional header, comparison table, and best-for recommendation. This tests research ability, formatting skills, and the capacity to synthesize findings into a deliverable business document.

2. Output Comparison: Nearly Identical Quality

Perplexity Computer recommended Zoho Invoice, Invoice Ninja, and Invoice Sherpa. Claude Cowork recommended Zoho Invoice, FreshBooks, and Invoice Sherpa—two of three tools matched. Both outputs included professional headers, clean comparison tables, and best-for recommendations at the bottom. The key difference: Perplexity included clickable source links in the footer for both the tool information and user reviews. Cowork listed source names (G2, Capterra, GetApp) but didn't provide direct links. Both followed instructions precisely.

3. The Verdict: Cowork Wins on Value

Across three exercises, Perplexity won round one, Cowork won round two, and round three was a tie. Perplexity Computer feels slightly better—maybe 5-10% improvement—because it accesses multiple AI models (not just Anthropic) and runs cloud-based so your computer can be off. But Cowork costs $20/month on any Claude paid plan. Perplexity Computer requires the $200/month Max plan. Is 5-10% better worth 10x the price? No. For most solopreneurs and small business owners, Claude Cowork delivers comparable results at a fraction of the cost. Save the $180/month difference and invest it elsewhere.
