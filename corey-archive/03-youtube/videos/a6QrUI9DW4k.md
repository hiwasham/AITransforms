Key Points

James builds a complete marketing funnel live — landing page, quiz, email sequence, and social content — in Claude Code in minutes. The V1 is better than what most businesses pay $10-15K for.

"Skills" are instruction files that encode expert marketing knowledge for AI. James created 10 covering email, SEO, copy, and repurposing — now a product with thousands of sales.

Anyone using these tools hits 80% quality instantly, outperforming most competitors. The last 20% — ping-ponging between models, testing, expert judgment — separates good from great.

Quiz funnels convert because they create micro commitments and sunk cost. After 7 questions, prospects are psychologically invested and will give their email to see results.

Claude Code automatically tailored each email to the gaps the quiz uncovered — personalized nurture, not generic drip.

One email sequence was repurposed into 2 LinkedIn carousels, 4 posts, 2 X threads, 7 tweets, 5 video scripts, and 4 SEO articles (2,000-2,800 words) — all routing back to the quiz.
