Key Points

• I landed 7 qualified leads and 1 paying client (about $1,000) from a single AI for Business meetup that took 90 minutes to set up and cost $0.

• The door-knocking script is simple: "I help local businesses save 5 to 10 hours a week using AI, I'm doing free 15 minute assessments this week, do you want one?" One guy knocked 30 doors and landed 5 meetings and 2 paying clients in a week.

• LinkedIn outreach math: send 20 DMs a day to business owners with 10-50 employees, expect 1-2 to turn into live conversations, 5-10 active conversations a week, 1-2 sales calls booked weekly, and a paying client within three weeks.

• Your first client is probably already in your phone. Text 10 people in your warm network offering a free 20-minute AI workflow audit, 6 will respond, 1-3 will book, and 1-2 will upsell into a paid assessment.

• A guy in AI Operator Academy did a free assessment for a friend who was so impressed he Venmo'd him $500 the next day without being asked.

• Partnering with agencies, coaches, accountants, and consultants for a 10% referral cut turns them into an unpaid sales team who already have the clients you want.

• Hosting free weekly AI office hours at a local coworking space builds credibility and warm leads with zero cost to the space and zero pitch required.
