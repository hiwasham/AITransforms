Key Points

• GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the new battleground — companies are scrambling to figure out how to get cited inside AI answers on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot, not just ranked on Google.

• The Letido app connects directly to Ahrefs data to show exactly which competitor pages are getting cited in AI answers for specific search queries, and where your brand is invisible.

• The app auto-generates full articles designed to get cited by AI — structured with numbered lists, comparison tables, and FAQ sections, because AI engines specifically favor that formatting when pulling answers.

• The workflow is fully hands-off: it researches the opportunity, writes the article, validates its own draft, lets you preview it live on a staging URL, and one-click publishes to your real website via GitHub.

• Once published, the app tracks daily whether your brand is mentioned, whether your page is cited, and your AI share of voice against competitors — giving you a trackable performance report over time.

• You can monetize this four ways: a paid AI visibility audit, a prioritized content gap report for self-implementation, standalone citation-ready content packs, or a full ongoing managed service billed monthly with a measurable ROI report.
