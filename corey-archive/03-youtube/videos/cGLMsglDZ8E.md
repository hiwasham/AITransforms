Key Points

Out of the box, Cowork has zero context about you — the entire onboarding exists to fix that by building three files (About Me, Brand Voice, Working Style) that Claude references every session so it never has to ask the same questions twice.

The one step most people miss: after generating global instructions, you have to manually paste them into Cowork settings — that single action is what makes every future session load with your context automatically.

Scheduled tasks only run when your computer is on and the Claude desktop app is open — so Cowork recommends running the morning briefing manually once to pre-approve Gmail and Calendar access before relying on automation.

The quarterly context review scheduled task is built into the free onboarding plugin and prompts you four times a year to update your context files — critical because your business changes faster than your setup files do.

The free plugin handles the full setup; the community-only version adds a self-assessment that audits your workflows, produces an impact-versus-effort matrix, and generates custom skill blueprints tailored to your specific business.
