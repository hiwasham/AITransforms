Key Points

• Hosting a local AI for small business meetup is the single best first move — Corey generated seven qualified leads in roughly two hours by partnering with a realtor, using her office, and following up every attendee within 24 hours.

• Door knocking is the fastest path to a paying client today. One person DMed Corey after knocking 30 doors — he got five appointments and two paying clients. The pitch is simple: a free 15-minute AI audit, no strings attached.

• Never sell in the first LinkedIn DM. Open with a genuine question about how they're using AI, have a real conversation, and let the sales ask come naturally after they show interest.

• Your warm network is the easiest starting point because trust is already there. Doing two to four free audits for friends or family members builds the experience and confidence to charge $999 for the same service.

• Agency and consultant partnerships are the highest long-term leverage on this list. One good referral partner can send 50 to 100 leads over the course of a year — essentially a sales team without payroll.

• AI office hours at a co-working space creates a double win: free exposure to business owners who need help, and a constant stream of real questions you can turn into social media content.
