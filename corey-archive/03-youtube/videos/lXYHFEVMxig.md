Key Points

• Dino turned a single $97 workshop into $65,734 in total pipeline value — a 44% close rate — without doing a single assessment before the money started coming in.

• He charges $5K/month for six months instead of a lump-sum $30K, because framing it monthly makes the number feel smaller even though the total is the same.

• He runs every assessment deliverable through NotebookLM (formerly Notebook LM) to auto-generate a 7-minute explainer video, so the client's spouse, coach, or business partner can get bought in without Dino having to be in the room.

• He customized Corey's assessment questionnaire template for the orthodontics niche by feeding Claude what he already knew about his industry's pain points.

• His sales pitch has no real close: he tells prospects they can take the assessment blueprint to any AI person they want, or hire him — and nearly everyone chooses him because trust and momentum are already built.
