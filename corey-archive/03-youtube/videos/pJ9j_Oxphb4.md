Summaries

1. The Four Levels Framework and Why It Matters

The AI mastery framework breaks down into four distinct levels, with 95% of entrepreneurs trapped at level 1. Level 1 is basic prompting—knowing how to talk to ChatGPT or Claude. Level 2 is workflow automation using tools like Make.com or Zapier. Level 3 is vibe coding, where you build custom tools using AI coding assistants. Level 4 is deploying autonomous agents that work independently. The critical insight: you don't need to reach level 4 to dominate your market. Getting to early level 3 puts you ahead of 99% of people and creates a massive competitive advantage. The gap between level 1 and level 3 is smaller than most think—it takes 90 days or less with consistent daily practice.

2. Mastering Level 1: Prompt Engineering That Actually Works

Basic competency at level 1 involves three steps: give the chatbot a specific role, define the exact task, and specify your desired output format. Instead of saying "write me a LinkedIn post," you'd say "You are an expert LinkedIn copywriter who specializes in scroll-stopping posts. Your task is to write a 100-word post on [topic]. Make it punchy with a strong CTA at the end." True mastery looks like having a reusable prompt template library, defaulting to AI instead of Google for answers, and being able to troubleshoot bad outputs by refining prompts rather than giving up. The simple role-task-output format is all you need to get ahead of 95% of people using AI.

3. Level 2 Through 3: Automation and Vibe Coding

Level 2 is where real leverage starts—using no-code automation tools like Make.com or Zapier to eliminate manual tasks. Competency means successfully automating 3-5 simple workflows like auto-responding to leads or syncing data between tools. Mastery looks like having multiple automations running simultaneously and leading with an "automation-first" mindset where you ask "can this be automated?" before doing any repetitive task. Level 3 is vibe coding, where you use AI assistants like Lovable, Replit, or Bolt to build custom tools, landing pages, or lightweight apps using natural language instead of traditional code. Start simple—build a landing page or internal tool. The key is building the muscle of creating custom solutions without needing to be a traditional developer.

4. The 90-Day Roadmap to Level 3

Month 1: Master prompting by practicing daily and building a prompt template library in Notion or Google Docs. Create reusable prompts for your most common tasks—from meal planning based on fridge contents to turning podcast transcripts into newsletter articles. Month 2: Automate one task per week using Make.com or Zapier. These don't need to save 20 hours each—the goal is building your automation muscle through repetition. Month 3: Build your first vibe-coded tool using a platform like Lovable. Start with something simple like a landing page for your personal brand. With just 15-20 minutes of daily practice following this roadmap, you'll develop three distinct high-value skillsets that put you ahead of 99% of people and make you incredibly valuable in the marketplace.
