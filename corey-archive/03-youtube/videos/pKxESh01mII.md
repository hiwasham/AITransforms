Key Points

• The AI Tools Assessment is a $999, 45-minute structured interview that prescribes 3-7 off-the-shelf tools to reclaim 5-10 hours a week, backed by a 100% money-back guarantee if you can't find at least 5 hours of opportunity.

• Fulfillment runs in four phases: discovery call, AI-assisted transcript analysis in Claude, a templated client report built in Claude Design, and a review call that ends with three closing questions.

• Every tool prescribed has to pull one of three levers to be worth recommending: effectiveness (make more money), efficiency (save time), or quality (improve the product or service).

• The effort vs impact matrix separates quick wins (off-the-shelf, low effort) from major projects (custom builds), and the major projects quadrant is where the upsell conversation starts.

• There are six upsell paths after the assessment: process redesign, simple automation builds, knowledge systems, custom workflows, retainers, and full implementation packages.

• Seven client acquisition methods require zero capital and zero audience: local AI meetups, door knocking, LinkedIn DMs, free mini-audits for your network, agency referral partners, AI office hours at co-working spaces, and posting your wins publicly.

• The AI Concierge retainer is two 45-minute monthly calls building Claude skills with a client, priced at $1,200-$2,000/month — an effective rate of roughly $1,000/hour.
