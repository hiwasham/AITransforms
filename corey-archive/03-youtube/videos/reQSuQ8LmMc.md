Key Points

• The whole AI Concierge system is four pieces: an intake form, two 45-minute monthly strategy calls, unlimited Voxer access, and a shared Notion Hub — nothing more complicated than that.

• Never automate a broken process. The AOA framework forces you to Audit the manual process, Optimize it down to its essential steps, then Automate it into a Claude skill — automating first just speeds up inefficiency.

• The intake form is a hard requirement before the first call — no form, no call. It surfaces the top three time-eating tasks and the one process the client would pay the most to never do again.

• Voxer access is high perceived value with low actual fulfillment — clients rarely use it, but knowing they can reach you anytime makes the retainer feel indispensable.

• The Notion Hub is the renewal weapon. It logs every call recording, summary, top three takeaways, action items, and every skill and context file built, so the client can see exactly what they're paying for.

• Never charge less than $1,000 a month for this offer — it's a high-touch, done-with-you service, and charging less tanks the perceived value in the client's mind.

• Raise your price by $250–$500 every time you close a new client. If nobody is saying no, your price is too low.
