In this episode I sit down with Nick Puru, an AI consultant who pulled in 4 million views on Instagram in 30 days and nearly 6,000 newsletter signups in 45 days using a short-form content system he runs entirely inside Claude Code. We walk through his exact setup — the CLAUDE.md "brain" file, his skills for writing scripts, reviewing scripts, and generating captions, his ICP file built around a persona named Patrick, and the foundations file that encodes everything he has learned about hooks, negativity bias, and algorithm behavior over two years of posting. By the end of the episode, you will know exactly how to turn Claude Code into a short-form content factory that sounds like you, speaks to your ICP, and feeds a real pipeline.

Key Points

• Nick's Claude Code setup converted 4 million Instagram views into roughly 6,000 newsletter signups in 45 days using a comment-triggered ManyChat funnel that hands off to a human appointment setter.

• The system is built on three skills — write script, review script, and create captions — orchestrated by a CLAUDE.md "brain" file that holds writing rules, key principles, and quality checklists.

• The ICP file matters as much as the brand voice file: Nick's "Patrick" persona (small business owners doing a few hundred thousand to $15M in revenue) shapes every hook, line, and CTA the system generates.

• To beat the AI-slop pattern (dashes, "it's not X, it's Y," overly formal structure), Nick layers in a humanizer skill from GitHub that rewrites drafts to sound natural.

• The right way to start is the same as onboarding a new hire: dump your SOPs, website, past analytics, and ICP into CLAUDE.md, then expand into specialized skills as you find gaps.

• Prompting is largely solved — the competitive edge now is context. Good context plus a clear prompt beats clever prompt engineering every time.
