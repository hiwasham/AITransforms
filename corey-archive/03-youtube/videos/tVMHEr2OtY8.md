Section Summaries

THE TRIPWIRE OFFER MODEL Alex and I walk through the core business concept: use HyperAgent to scan for local businesses with great Google reviews but weak or nonexistent websites, then have the agent build a full website mockup automatically. The website becomes a free giveaway that gets you on the phone with the business owner, and the revenue comes from upselling systems that actually drive their business forward.


SPEED TO QUOTE SYSTEMS THAT CLOSE DEALS We go deep on two examples, a landscaping company and an auto mechanic, where HyperAgent builds interactive speed to quote tools directly into the website. RUNNING AND MONETIZING YOUR AGENT TEAM Alex shows how he manages eight to ten agents concurrently on an Airtable Kanban board, with agents moving their own cards into a review column when work is done. We also cover three ways to monetize agents: setup fees plus retainers, exporting agent configs to a client's workspace, or the upcoming marketplace model where builders earn a markup on compute costs.
