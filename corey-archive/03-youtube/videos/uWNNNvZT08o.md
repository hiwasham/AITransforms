Key Points

• A context profile is a structured information package that gives AI the background knowledge it needs to perform tasks effectively—think of it like briefing documents for a new team member.

• Without a context profile, you're starting from scratch every time, getting generic outputs that don't sound like you or serve your specific audience.

• This system works for more than just content creation—use it for ICP profiles, SOPs, sales processes, or any recurring AI task.

• Claude projects (or custom GPTs) act as dedicated workspaces where you can store all your context files and custom instructions in one place.

• Quality examples beat quantity every time—five to ten of your best podcasts, posts, or documents will give AI a vivid understanding of your voice and style.

Summaries:

1. What Is a Context Profile and How Does It Work

A context profile is essentially a briefing document that contains everything AI needs to know about you, your business, and your audience. Instead of re-explaining yourself every time you open ChatGPT or Claude, you build this profile once and reference it forever. The two core documents I use are my brand voice & bio document (who I am, what I do, who I serve) and my ICP document (the exact demographics, pain points, and goals of my ideal client). These documents were generated by having Claude interview me—I didn't spend hours writing them manually. Once uploaded to a Claude project, every output I get is tailored to my voice and my audience without me having to remind the AI each time.

2. The Power of Real Examples in Your Context Profile

Documents and descriptions are helpful, but nothing beats real examples. I attached five full podcast transcripts so Claude understands how I speak out loud—my tone, cadence, and word choices. I also included my top ten best-performing LinkedIn posts and top ten X posts so it has concrete examples of how I write. This isn't about letting AI write everything for you. It's about getting 70-80% of the way there instantly, then editing with your own expertise. Quality over quantity matters here—don't dump every piece of content you've ever created. Choose your absolute best five to ten examples that showcase your style at its peak.

3. Project Instructions That Tie Everything Together

Having documents attached to a Claude project isn't enough—you need custom instructions that tell Claude when and how to use each file. My project instructions explicitly say: "Always reference the uploaded context files before creating any content. The brandprofile.json contains complete brand voice guidelines, the ICP document defines the target audience, the bio covers my background, and the podcast transcripts show tone and style." Without these instructions, Claude is guessing which documents to reference. With them, every output is informed by the full context profile. I generated these instructions using a simple prompt asking Claude to create concise project instructions based on all the files I'd uploaded.
