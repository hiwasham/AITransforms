Key Points

• The free mini assessment is a 15-minute call where you identify exactly one pain point and prescribe one tool or workflow fix, nothing more, so you protect your time and set up the paid upsell.

• Only run the free assessment for your first one to three prospects, its only job is generating testimonials and case studies that make the paid offer an easy yes.

• The paid assessment is the free assessment "on steroids": a 45-60 minute interview that surfaces 3-7 opportunities, scored on an effort-versus-impact matrix, and sold for $500-$2,000 (Corey's current price is $2,000).

• Document ROI in the client's own words: ask their hourly rate and hours lost to a bottleneck, then multiply, if SaneBox saves someone worth $250/hour four hours a week on email, that is $1,000 a week they can't argue with.

• AI Concierge is the real recurring-revenue engine: two 45-minute strategy calls a month plus unlimited Voxer access, priced $1,000-$2,000/month, which works out to roughly $750-$1,250 per hour of actual work.

• The AoA framework (Audit, Optimize, Automate) is how you run every Concierge call: audit the manual process step by step, cut the wasted steps, then rebuild it as a Claude skill inside Claude Cowork.

• Raise your price by $250 every time a prospect says yes, Corey used this exact tactic to go from $1,000/month clients to no longer taking anyone under $2,500/month.
