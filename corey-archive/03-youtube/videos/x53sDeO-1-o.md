Summaries

1. The LinkedIn Authority Flywheel: Turn One Podcast Into Seven Posts

Record podcasts with LinkedIn influencers who have 10K+ followers in your niche. Use Google Gemini to extract five to ten core themes—contrarian takes, frameworks, tactics, stories. Publish five to seven LinkedIn posts based on those themes. Tag the guest so they amplify your posts through comments, likes, or reposts. This gives you dual distribution to both audiences. Your audience and authority grow as people see you collaborating with thought leaders. Engagement signals guide which topics resonate and which guests to interview next. Growing authority attracts higher quality guests, creating better conversations and stronger content inputs. The flywheel gets easier with each rotation.

2. The YouTube Authority Flywheel: Same Strategy, Different Platform

The YouTube flywheel follows identical framework adapted for video. Record collaboration videos with YouTube creators who have 5K+ subscribers. Extract the best clips and themes using AI. Publish YouTube Shorts and long-form videos based on those clips. Get your collab partner to promote the content. Your subscriber count and channel authority grow. Use performance data to guide future collabs and content topics. Growing authority attracts bigger creators, leading to better collaborations. One collaboration generates multiple pieces of content that both parties amplify for mutual benefit.

3. The Minimum Viable Stack: Five Free Tools

The MVS philosophy: use the minimum tools needed to execute this strategy. Notion tracks every KPI and stores workflow templates. Riverside records all episodes in broadcast quality. Google Gemini analyzes transcripts and extracts themes, frameworks, and story angles. Claude polishes rough drafts—you feed it bullet points, it returns a 70-80% finished post, you edit to 100%. Google Sheets stores guest prospects. No paid subscriptions required. The real power isn't the tools themselves but the system that connects them.
