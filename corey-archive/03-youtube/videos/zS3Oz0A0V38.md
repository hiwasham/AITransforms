Key Points

The LLM Wiki skill ships built into Hermes out of the box — your job is to curate sources, the agent's job is to summarize, file, and retrieve.

Under the hood the wiki has three layers: read-only raw sources you provide, the agent-owned web of markdown files, and the schema and tags that make querying low-friction.

The differentiator versus OpenClaw is the self-improving learning loop — Hermes proactively creates skills, logs learnings, and runs recurring tasks that actually fire.

Deploying on a Hostinger VPS is cheaper than a Mac mini, one-click to install, always-on, and keeps your data private — KVM2 gives you headroom to run multiple agents.

MarkDownload is the cheat code for feeding the wiki — clip any tweet, article, or webpage into clean Markdown and drag-and-drop it into your Telegram chat with the bot.

Your knowledge base will be the dumbest it ever is on day one — keep feeding it and by day 90 it becomes a compounding personal asset you can query like a search engine.
