Key Points

• Host a free monthly "AI for Business" meetup at a local co-working space and you never have to cold email or post content again — in-person trust beats six months of DMs in 30 seconds.

• You get clients two ways from this: direct signups who become paying leads, and referrals from other attendees who send you people who never even come to the event.

• The offer stack is a front door and a house — the free meetup gets people in, then you sell a $999 AI Tools Assessment, then a $1,000–$2,000/month AI Concierge retainer to implement it.

• Selling two assessments and one concierge client from a single meetup can generate $3,000–$4,000 in one evening, and the math only gets better every month you keep hosting.

• The single biggest lever for turnout is partnering with a local Facebook group owner as co-host — trading your expertise for their existing audience turned my next event from 30 to a projected 50+ attendees.

• Teach first, pitch second: run a 15–20 minute no-jargon demo, then close with a 30–60 second soft CTA offering the paid assessment — never hard-sell the room itself.

• Lock a recurring monthly date and don't quit after event one — referrals and authority compound with every rep, the same way SEO compounds over months, not days.
