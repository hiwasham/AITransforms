06

The design decisions that make it work

Every choice in the prompt is load-bearing. These are the four that matter most.

PRINCIPLE 01

Answer first, ask second

Claude answers each question from the Master Prompt before asking you. This eliminates the blank-page problem — you're correcting, not composing. It also surfaces gaps in the Master Prompt itself.

PRINCIPLE 02

One question at a time

No question dumps. Each question gets its own turn, its own insight, its own confirmation. This is slower but produces far more specific answers — and prevents the "I'll answer the easy ones" failure mode.

PRINCIPLE 03

Benchmarks after every answer

Raw numbers mean nothing without context. Each answer is immediately compared to industry benchmarks (lead-to-qualified 13–20%, proposal-to-close 20–30%, etc.) so you know whether you're behind, average, or ahead.

PRINCIPLE 04

The output feeds the input

Discoveries are logged during the interview and become a formal amendment to the Master Prompt. The assessment doesn't just produce a report — it makes the AI permanently smarter about your business.
