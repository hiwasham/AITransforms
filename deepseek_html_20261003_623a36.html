04 Marketing-relevant reads

Three things the profile payload reveals that matter to the marketing work.

Signal 01
Per-domain weak areas

specialty_scores + weak_areas mean the app models each learner's weak spots. This feeds adaptive quizzing and a "Previously Wrong" pool — a concrete product feature to reference in messaging.

Signal 02
PayPal billing + Prometric bundle SKUs

subscription_* + paypal_email confirm PayPal billing. prometric_bundles shows a separate Prometric bundle SKU exists — an additional revenue lane beyond the standard subscription.

Signal 03
Geo / anti-fraud signal

ip_country_mismatch is a geo/anti-fraud flag on the account. Useful context for segmentation work — the app already distinguishes declared country from observed IP country.

05 Role & track

◆
Dual-role account

The test account is a nurse learner in the client area and a reviewer in the console — /api/messages returns userRole: "reviewer"; /api/reviewer-preferences returns reviewer_scope: "student".

This is why the review queue shows medical-school content while the dashboard shows nursing — two different role scopes on the same login.

⚠
Preview As · unverified

A "Preview As" / "Viewing as Nurse" toggle sits bottom-right in-app — a likely non-mutating way to see other role dashboards. Not clicked — see GAPS.md.

Student and Resident dashboards need a track switch, which is a write and therefore gated.
