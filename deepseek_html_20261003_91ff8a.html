Roadmap — Bayan Product Brain

From the 1-week pilot to a standing AI system for Bayan. Draft-first throughout: every phase reads Bayan's own approved facts and changes nothing without sign-off. Each phase earns the next — nothing past Phase 1 is committed.

Framing lock

The win is measured in time saved and drift eliminated, not revenue lift. Bayan is pre-revenue with no instrumented funnel, so any conversion claim would be unbacked. Keep the value story on the CEO's stated pain: his time.

§ How the phases stack

Four phases, in order of earn-out. Only Phase 1 is committed today; each later phase is contingent on the previous one landing. Two blocks at the bottom are deliberately out of the near roadmap — parked on purpose.
