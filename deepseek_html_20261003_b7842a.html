§ Executive recommendation

Lead with the denominator

The addressable base is not 1,300 — and its real size is unknown until a deduplicated audit.

The CEO brief reports 1,300 registered users, <1% conversion, $277 total revenue. Planning excerpts also report that accounts created before 2026-05-01 have permanent free access and that 13 countries receive humanitarian free access. If those rules are confirmed, those users should not be treated as ordinary missed purchase opportunities.

Keep grandfathered access intact unless the CEO explicitly decides otherwise. Do not include those accounts in the paying-opportunity denominator under current policy. Treat country eligibility the same way. "Inactive" is an engagement state; "wrong segment" is a hypothesis to check — not a reason to silently drop users.

Funnel instrumentation: the strategy excerpt says the funnel is uninstrumented, but the project's integration status lists PostHog as connected. Week 1 therefore checks existing PostHog / payment / product events first, and only builds a new track() layer + sink if an inventory shows it's needed. The supplied spec names the work and a 10-event completion criterion — but does not include the actual event schema.

Four weekly deliverable sets: (1) cohort + event audit, three landing-page drafts, count reconciliation · (2) draft activation and trial-to-paid concepts · (3) draft reactivation + low-tier proposal + grandfathering memo · (4) measured readout and CEO go/no-go. Deployment, payment changes, access changes, and outbound messages remain approval-gated.

02 Cohort model · two dimensions

Use two dimensions so the cohort math is valid. First: a mutually exclusive entitlement / eligibility group for a fixed account snapshot. Second: an engagement cross-tab over each group.

G + F + A + U = 1,300
only if the supplied total is confirmed against the export · report counts, shares, cutoff date, source, and rules
G
Grandfathered
Under the confirmed policy. Excluded from paid denominator unless the CEO explicitly decides otherwise.
F
Free-country
Eligible for humanitarian free access, excluding G by a documented precedence rule.
A
Addressable non-payer
Unique, non-paying, eligible location / entitlement, and a verified matching paid offer. This is the denominator.
U
Unresolved
Access, country, duplicate, payment, or offer-fit evidence missing or ambiguous. Excluded until resolved.
Engagement state — active / inactive / unknown, defined with an agreed activity window and event. Inactive is not an extra mutually exclusive group; G, F, and A can each include inactive accounts. "Wrong segment" remains a separate declared-intent / offer-fit attribute.

04 Measurement and target policy

⚠
Do not commit 3% / 5% / 7% as targets before Week 1

They are scenarios in the CEO-review material, not Bayan-measured rates and not validated by the cited SaaS benchmark. The live addressable denominator, baseline numerator, measurement window, price, and recurring-vs-one-time product mix are unknown. The annex shows the arithmetic both ways and defines the exact data gate for turning a scenario into a target.

◎
Primary metric

Unique addressable non-payers at the fixed cutoff who complete a first successful paid transaction within the agreed observation window, divided by fixed A. Show numerator and denominator.

For a recurring plan, report new MRR from active subscriptions separately; for an exam-window pass, report one-time receipts, not MRR. Adjust for refunds and churn only using verified matched-period data. Do not compare lifetime revenue to monthly revenue.
