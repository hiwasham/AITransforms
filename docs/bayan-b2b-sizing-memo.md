## 1. Exec verdict (sized)

The Gulf institutional prize, measured in **Bayan's own documented seat revenue**, is **small**: full-GCC TAM ≈ **$7.6M–$12.5M/yr at 100% penetration** (fantasy ceiling), serviceable SAM ≈ **$1.1M–$3.0M/yr**, and realistically obtainable by a 10 hr/week team in 12–24 months (SOM) ≈ **$5k–$86k/yr ARR** — with **~$0 year-1 cash** because Bayan currently gives free 1-year institutional trials. A single landed pilot nets roughly **$1,200–$7,200/yr gross** (20–120 seats × ~$60 blended), **minus the CEO's 20% commission**.

So on seat revenue alone, B2B does not pay for a dedicated near-term cash track. It is defensible as **priority #1 only if valued as a credibility / distribution / moat play** — anchor logos, bulk free-seat user acquisition that feeds the B2C funnel, co-marketing with named institutions, and a springboard to **negotiated site-license / CME contracts priced far above the per-seat list** (the list price is a floor, not a ceiling). The single number that flips the verdict from "strategic, small" to "material revenue" is **average negotiated contract value per institution** — if that is a flat site/CME deal in the tens of thousands rather than 40 seats × $60, the prize is 10–50× larger. That number is unknown today and is the thing to go get.

## 2. TAM / SAM / SOM (inputs, arithmetic, sources, confidence)

**Bayan documented institutional seat prices** (`product-archive/08-pricing-exam-catalog-and-access-model.md` L36-42; corroborated `research.md` L390-392): Physician **$99/seat/yr**, Nurse **$69/seat/yr**, Student **$35/seat/yr**. Terms: **min 20 seats, up to 100 trial seats, 1-year institutional trial, 50% off individual, invoice-based**; currently **free 1-yr trials to selected institutions**. (No prices invented.)

**Seat-pool inputs (GCC-wide installed base), low/base/high:**

| Segment | Price/seat/yr | Low | Base | High | Source / confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medical students | $35 | 45,000 | 60,000 | 75,000 | Scaled from GCC medical-college counts (§3); Saudi ~27–34 schools `[confirm]` |
| Nursing students / early-career | $69 | 40,000 | 55,000 | 70,000 | Saudi nurse workforce 243,336 (2024, verified) is upper bound; exam-active subset `[unverified]` |
| Residents / trainees | $99 | 30,000 | 37,000 | 45,000 | Anchored on **verified** Saudi SCFHS 25,880 trainees; GCC scale-up `[confirm]` |
| Pharmacy students | $35 | 8,000 | 12,000 | 16,000 | `[unverified]` scaled estimate |

**TAM = Σ (seats × price), 100% of installed GCC target seats:**
- Low: 123,000 seats → **$7,585,000/yr**
- Base: 164,000 seats → **$9,978,000/yr** (blended **$60.8/seat**)
- High: 206,000 seats → **$12,470,000/yr**

**SAM = TAM × exam-active fraction (~30% `[confirm]`) × institution-licensable fraction (~50% `[confirm]`):**
- Low: **$1,137,750/yr** · Base: **$1,496,700/yr** · High: **$1,870,500/yr**
- Looser sensitivity (40% × 60%): High ≈ **$2,992,800/yr**

**SOM = pilots landed × avg paid seats × ~$60 blended** (10 hr/week team, min-20-seat 1-yr pilots, 12–24 mo):
- Low: 3 pilots × 30 seats = 90 → **$5,400/yr ARR**
- Base: 6 pilots × 60 seats = 360 → **$21,600/yr ARR**
- High: 12 pilots × 120 seats = 1,440 → **$86,400/yr ARR**
- **Critical:** Bayan's free 1-yr trial means **year-1 cash from pilots ≈ $0**; ARR only lands at renewal (month 12–24), only for pilots that convert.

Confidence: seat *prices* = **confirmed (internal)**. Seat *counts* = **low confidence** (`[confirm]`/`[unverified]`); directional, not precise. TAM/SAM swing ±40% on seat-pool assumptions; SOM is the most robust conclusion (small under any assumption — tiny team, free pilots).

## 5. Unit economics — seat revenue vs effort to land one institution

- **Per pilot gross:** 20 seats × $60 = **$1,200/yr**; 40 = **$2,400**; 60 = **$3,600**; 120 = **$7,200/yr**.
- **Net of CEO's 20% commission** (`growth-engine-design.md` L62-63): a 40–60-seat pilot nets **$1,920–$2,880/yr**.
- **Sales motion is high-touch and slow:** CEO-endorsed B2B motion is relationship-first — *"visit the exhibition... make friendship first, then prepare a tailored proposal for each and personally approach them"* (`research.md` L219-222). Weeks of effort per account.
- **Payback:** free 1-yr trials → cash payback on selling effort is **>12 months at best**, only if the pilot converts at renewal. On seat revenue alone, the effort-to-revenue ratio is poor.
- **Where the economics *could* work:** (a) a pilot's free seats are a **bulk B2C acquisition channel** — convert even 10–20% of trial users to $69–$199/yr individual subs and the pilot is worth 3–10× the seat fee; (b) **negotiated flat site/CME contracts** above list; (c) **anchor logos** that lower B2C CAC. None captured in the seat-price model.

## 7. Recommendation (B2B is priority #1 — the prize + first moves)

**How big is the prize:** direct seat revenue is **modest** — SAM ~$1–3M/yr, obtainable SOM <$90k/yr ARR for a 10 hr/week team, ~$0 year-1 cash. If the CEO ranks B2B above B2C, the justification is **not** near-term seat cash; it must be **strategic**: anchor logos + clinician/institutional relationships (match the CEO's and Nasim's network and selling style), **bulk free-seat user acquisition feeding the B2C funnel**, and a path to **negotiated site-license/CME contracts** the per-seat list floors but does not cap. Framed that way, B2B-first is coherent — provided success is measured in **logos, converted trial-users, and one lighthouse contract**, not seat-fee revenue.

**First 1–2 moves (fits 10 hr/week, min-20-seat 1-yr pilots):**

1. **Convert the warm book first — do not prospect cold.** Pull the list of institutions **already on free 1-yr trials** (unknown #6) + the Oman/MedResearch Academy relationships, and run a **renewal/expansion conversation** on the two or three warmest. Only motion that produces revenue (or a real contract-value data point — unknown #1) inside 12 months with near-zero selling hours. Target: **one signed lighthouse contract** with a named Gulf institution.
2. **Instrument trial-seat → B2C conversion.** Before scaling pilots, track free institutional seats so you can prove the real payoff (B2C conversions + usage), per the funnel-instrumentation gate in `revenue-integration-strategy.md`. Turns the free-trial model from a cost into a measurable acquisition channel and produces the contract-value/conversion numbers that currently make the prize unknowable.

**The one piece of data that changes the answer:** average **negotiated contract value per institution** (unknown #1). If lighthouse deals land as flat site/CME licenses in the tens of thousands rather than ~$2k seat bundles, the prize is 10–50× the seat model and B2B-first is justified on revenue alone — go get that number from move #1.
