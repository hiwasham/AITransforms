## The interpretation sequence Bayan drills

1. Assess pH — acidaemia or alkalaemia.
2. Identify the primary disorder — respiratory or metabolic.
3. Check compensation — appropriate or a second disorder.
4. Calculate the anion gap and the delta ratio.
5. Tie it back to the clinical picture.
