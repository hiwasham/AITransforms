## What "Prometric exam questions" means

Most Gulf licensing exams are delivered through Prometric test centres. The exam
content is set by each country's health authority, so "Prometric questions" is
shorthand for authority-specific licensing questions sat at a Prometric centre.
Bayan maps its banks to each authority rather than offering one generic pool.
