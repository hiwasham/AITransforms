04 Access model

A user gets full access if any of the following is true. Priority order applies.

  1. Admin — internal staff account
  2. Reviewer — clinical/content reviewer
  3. Grandfathered — account created before 2026-05-01
  4. Institution — holds a paid seat
  5. Free country — matches humanitarian geo list
  6. Subscription active — paying consumer
Humanitarian access · 13 countries Full free access for users in: Yemen, Sudan, Syria, Libya, Afghanistan, Somalia, South Sudan, Myanmar, DR Congo, Central African Republic, Eritrea, Haiti, Palestine (Gaza / West Bank).
Common misunderstanding · correct it Oman is NOT in the free-country list. Free access for Omani users comes through grandfathering or institutional deals — not a blanket geo rule. Say "free for early Oman users / institutions," not "free in Oman."

07 Marketing takeaways

  • Prices are now known. Anchor tiers: Student $9.99, Nurse $19, Physician $29 monthly — all with a 30-day trial and ~43% annual savings. This closes the biggest [TBD] in the plan.
  • Institutional seat pricing is a B2B revenue lane. Minimum 20 seats, 1-year trial, routed through MedResearch Academy. A concrete Revenue-stage play for hospitals and training programs.
  • Humanitarian free-country access is authentic mission proof. 13 conflict and low-income countries get full free access — an on-brand integrity signal, not a marketing claim.
  • Per-exam counts back the "23 exams / 5,500+ questions" claim. The catalog provides auditable numbers — usable as concrete proof, not rounded marketing figures.
Brand rule · enforce it The site's own nurse-tier copy uses the banned terms "Prometric" and "NCLEX" (US-focused). Our copy must reframe everything to "Gulf Licensing Exam Preparation" — even when the source config doesn't follow the rule.
