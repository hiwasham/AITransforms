05 Marketing takeaways

Four concrete reads for the marketing work — the tooling is quotable proof, not a marketing claim.

01
Point-of-care tooling is a real product surface

22 evidence-based calculators across 8 specialties, 150+ drug monographs, an interaction checker, renal dosing, IV compatibility, and 20 emergency drug cards. This is concrete "clinical companion" proof for the physician tier — not a marketing claim.

02
Voice-enabled Virtual Patients is a demo-able hero feature

Already listed in the physician tier's config, and now confirmed live in the product. Usable as a hero feature — differentiated and easy to demo.

03
These are utility / retention features, not exam prep

They give a reason to open the app between study sessions — the EMPOWER Retention stage. Different job from the question bank, same brand.

04
Bank scale is quotable with the app's own numbers

"22", "150+", "20" — actual counts, not round marketing figures. Usable directly in copy per the standing verified-numbers rule.
