## Problem Statement

Hiwa wants to combine the Bayan marketing work (especially BayanLearning) and the
already-sent "Bayan Product Brain" proposal into a bigger offering: a "CEO Bayan AI
Brain." The prior design chose the narrow Product Brain wedge on purpose. This session
re-opens scope: how to go bigger without over-building, without stepping on Nasim's
marketing lane, and without pulling Hiwa off his AITransforms path into a bespoke Bayan
build.

## Demand Evidence

Split, and the split is the whole point:
- **Wedge (Product Brain): real.** CEO-acknowledged pain — *"this is taking a lot of my
  time,"* *"we have been running around cycles for missing Bayan logo."* Live drift across
  surfaces (question count appears as 8 different values; brand-entity split). Proposal
  already sent.
- **Platform (CEO AI Brain): a bet, not validated demand.** Hiwa's own answer (D2): *no
  evidence the CEO wants a platform — it's my bet on where AITransforms goes.* His stated
  #1 pain is time, not "give me a system to run." So the platform is a founder-vision
  thesis the wedge must test, not a validated want.

## Status Quo

Manual reconciliation of facts across sites/apps/brochures; the CEO or Nasim chases the
"official" number, then waits on the CEO to decide it. Cost = the CEO's scarcest resource
(time) + staff hours + brand risk at events.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

Target human: **Dr. Abdullah Al-Alawi, CEO** — runs 6 products with a small team, drained
by coordination, low-touch by preference (~2 calls/week). Narrowest wedge (unchanged):
the **$500 Product Brain pilot** — canonical approved facts + EN/AR ask-the-brain + drift
check. It is now framed as **Stage 0** of the CEO AI Brain program.

## Constraints

- Low-touch cadence (~2 calls + weekly report). Draft-first, reads public surfaces only.
- Must stay on Hiwa's AITransforms path; must not overlap Nasim's marketing lane — ideally
  empowers her.
- Bayan content locks: human-owned/clinician-reviewed, verified counts only (5,589 Qs · 55+
  countries), no pass/outcome guarantees, unknowns → `[confirm with team]`.

## Premises (all confirmed this session)

1. **Ship the narrow Product Brain wedge FIRST**; design the platform as the destination.
2. **A brain that writes marketing copy is in Nasim's lane** — hand those outputs to her,
   or carve marketing out. Unresolved, it damages the relationship the engagement depends on.
3. **The platform must REMOVE the CEO's touch**, never add operational burden — he is buying
   his time back.
4. **Stay the brain/infrastructure layer** — don't get pulled into Bayan's own product
   engineering.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Minimal — relabel + roadmap
Banner the existing proposal + roadmap as "CEO AI Brain — Phase 1." Zero new build, ships
now. Rejected as primary: vision-as-appendix reads like an afterthought, doesn't make the
platform feel inevitable.

### Approach B: Ideal — one brain, two surfaces
Architect a single canonical brain with two query surfaces (facts source-of-truth + ask/
draft assistant); ship facts first, assistant + facts-feed slot in with minimal rework —
both surfaces read the same canonical fact store through one defined query interface
(integration risk for engineering planning to confirm). This is the build spine, not the
sales frame.

### Approach C: Lateral — staged program pitch (CHOSEN)
Sell the whole "CEO Bayan AI Brain" now as a staged program: Stage 0 = the $500 Product
Brain (safe on-ramp), each later stage a small paid gate on the prior win. Lets Hiwa pitch
big while staying wedge-safe; each stage funds the next.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach C, built on Approach B's architecture.** The CEO AI Brain is the product; the
$500 Product Brain is Stage 0; B's one-brain-two-surfaces spine — a single canonical fact
store with one query interface both surfaces consume — is how each stage ships with minimal
rework (integration risk engineering planning must confirm). This is the only path that
satisfies "go big" (Hiwa's explicit choice) without breaking wedge discipline (P1) or
scaring a time-starved CEO (P3).

Provisional stage map (to be priced — see Open Questions):
- **Stage 0 — Product Brain** ($500): canonical facts + EN/AR ask + drift check. *Ships first.*
- **Stage 1 — CEO Assistant**: "what's our official X?" + drafts **internal/reference** text
  on demand from the canonical source (removes touch, P3). Anything **publishable** (marketing
  copy) routes to Nasim per P2 — the internal-vs-publishable boundary is an open item to
  confirm with her before Stage 1 is pitched.
- **Stage 2 — Facts-feed**: brain auto-populates marketing/app-store/brochures; **outputs
  route to Nasim** (she is the publisher — satisfies P2).
- **Deferred**: learner-facing study-tutor / RAG over the 5,589-Q bank (off-path, Bayan's
  own engineering — P4).

## Open Questions

1. **Per-stage pricing.** Stage 0 = $500 (set). Stages 1–2 unpriced. External research says
   $500 is already 5× below the market floor for a solo "quick win" — decide whether the
   program stays a deliberate loss-leader ladder or steps up (e.g. Stage 1 $1.5–3k, Stage 2
   retainer $500–1,500/mo). Hiwa owns this.
2. **Nasim's role in Stage 2.** "Route outputs to Nasim" is the P2 answer, but her role
   (reviewer? publisher? co-owner of the facts-feed?) needs her buy-in before Stage 2 is
   pitched. Depends on the alignment message going out first.
3. **How much of the program to reveal in the pitch now.** Overselling the vision before
   Stage 0 delivers can dent trust (the C con). Likely: name the destination, sell Stage 0.
4. **AITransforms recognition.** Confirm the CEO knows AITransforms as a distinct entity vs
   "Nasim's technical person" before leaning on the program framing.

## Success Criteria

- CEO approves Stage 0 (the $500 Product Brain pilot) — unchanged from the prior design.
- The pitch names the CEO AI Brain destination without committing Hiwa to build it before
  Stage 0 is paid and delivered.
- Nasim is aligned and sees Stage 2 as handing her the approved reference, not competing.
- Engagement recognized as AITransforms, not absorbed into the marketing work.
- CEO time cost during Stage 0 stays ~2 calls + weekly report.

## Distribution Plan

No software distribution at pitch stage. Stage 0 runs on Hiwa's existing gbrain stack; the
deliverable to the CEO is a document + a live query demo + the first drift report. Later
stages are delivered as gbrain-backed surfaces, not a shipped app.

## Dependencies

  `marketing-plans/bayan/nasim-alignment-message.md`.
