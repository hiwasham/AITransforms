Why a qualified practitioner may hesitate

Qualified practitioners may hesitate because the registration decision has five practical questions: eligibility, lodging, currency/payment, refund timing, and certificate value.

  • The page welcomes IFS-informed and IFS Institute Level I trained practitioners, but buyers may still wonder if their exact background qualifies.
  • The price includes lunch, but lodging is separate and booked through Ham Green House.
  • The workshop price is listed in USD, while lodging details are in GBP.
  • Refunds are available until 21 days before the workshop, minus a 3% processing fee. No refunds are available inside 20 days.
  • A certificate is available, and buyers may want to know how it applies to IFS-I certification.

Gentle follow-up draft: If you are IFS-informed or Level I trained and unsure whether this workshop fits your background, send us a short note about your training and group-facilitation experience. We can help you decide before you register.
