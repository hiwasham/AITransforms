# Level 2: Inbound Content Strategy Blueprint

**Blueprint Name:** Inbound Content Strategy
**Within Core Process:** Market to Lead
**Owner:** Hiwa (Content Creator)
**Audience:** Future content hires, freelance writers, translation reviewers
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-09

---

## Function

Create and publish problem-focused content (EN/FA/AR) that attracts ideal customers searching for AI transformation solutions, and converts a fraction of them into leads.

This blueprint covers the **Attract** and **Engage** stages of the core process.

---

## Detailed Workflow

### 1. Topic Research (Weekly, ~2 hours)

- Pull keyword ideas (AI CEO assistant, RAG architecture, business brain mapping, AI SOPs)
- Review which existing posts drove engagement/leads last week
- Mine real questions from leads, community, and sales conversations
- Pick 2-3 topics for the week; log in content calendar

**Quality gate:** Each chosen topic maps to a specific customer pain point and a specific service.

### 2. Content Creation (2-3x/week, ~3-4 hours each)

- Follow the Level 3 Guide (`02-guide-blog-creation.md`)
- Draft in English first (source language)
- Flag sections needing cultural adaptation for FA/AR

**Quality gate:** Technical accuracy verified; follows the Level 4 template.

### 3. Multi-Language Production (~1 hour per language)

- Translate EN → FA and EN → AR
- Keep technical terms in English where that's the norm
- Native-speaker review before publish (RTL rendering check)

**Quality gate:** RTL layout correct; no machine-translation awkwardness.

### 4. Publication & Distribution (~30 min)

- Publish all three language versions with correct `hreflang`
- SEO checklist complete (see Level 4)
- Syndicate: LinkedIn post, newsletter queue
- Mark "published" in content calendar

**Quality gate:** All three locales live; internal links added.

---

## Handoff Procedures

| From | To | What transfers |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Topic Research | Content Creation | Approved topic + target keyword + pain point |
| Content Creation | Multi-Language | Final EN draft + adaptation notes |
| Multi-Language | Publication | 3 reviewed language versions |
| Publication | Lead Capture Blueprint | Published page with working CTA/form |

---

## Service Level Agreements

- Topic research: completed every Monday
- Posts: 2-3 published per week
- Translation review: within 48h of EN draft completion
- A published post is never missing its FA/AR versions for more than 72h

---

## Resources Required

- Content calendar (Notion or Google Sheet)
- Keyword research (free tier initially)
- Translation review contacts (FA + AR native speakers)
- Access to `src/content/site.ts` for any on-site copy changes

---

## Common Issues & Escalation

- **Translation backlog** → publish EN, queue FA/AR, never block the EN release
- **No topics meet the quality gate** → repurpose a strong past post into a deeper guide
- **Technical claim can't be verified** → cut the claim or mark the post draft until confirmed

---

## Success Criteria

- 2-3 posts/week, all three languages
- Average post quality score >8/10 (Level 3 checklist)
- Organic traffic growth 10-20% MoM
- ~1 qualified lead per 500 visitors
