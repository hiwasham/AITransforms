Agentes Secretos and Casa Dividida are your books. Teachers are searching for them by name, at 390 and 40 searches a month, and finding someone else. Berto sits at #4 — which proves the pattern works when the page is right. Three of the four book-title searches are being handed to competitors.

You win the buying question and lose the two choosing questions. That is the wrong way around: by the time a teacher is ready to buy, they have already decided what to buy. You are absent from the conversation where they make that decision.

4. Speed is costing you teachers

Real visitors, 75th percentile, mobile: 4.2 seconds before main content appears. Desktop is 4.0s. A Lighthouse mobile run scores performance 53, with roughly 450ms recoverable from unused CSS and about 150ms each from CSS and JS minification.

That is measured from your actual traffic, not a lab. A teacher on a school connection deciding between two suppliers does not wait four seconds.

What was built from this

This audit was turned into eight work items. Seven are drafted and ready for review:

  • Spanish learning-books page — title, meta description, H1 and copy targeting "spanish language learning books" (2,900/mo), with verified internal links.
  • French learning-books page — the same treatment for a 720/mo gap.
  • Berto page rebuild — protect and extend the one search that already works.
  • Homepage discovery package — title, description, Open Graph, single-H1 hierarchy, Organization JSON-LD, plus llms.txt and llms-full.txt drafts.
  • Novice-learner guidance — level guidance and classroom examples for the AI question you are currently losing.
  • Graded-reader comparison guide — a direct answer to the question Claude answers without you.
  • Speed change set — the safe CSS/JS wins behind the 4.2s.
  • Teacher social calendar — four themes drawn from your own catalogue.
Nothing has been published. No live change was made to tprsbooks.com. Every item above is a draft waiting for a human at TPRS Books to read it and decide.

If you want the short version

Fix the homepage markup so search engines and AI can read the site properly. Build real pages for the four book searches you are losing — two of which are your own titles. Then answer the two choosing questions teachers are asking, once, well, where AI will find the answer.

That is the whole plan. It is finite and it is measurable — the same six searches can be re-run in ninety days and compared against this baseline.
