One funnel, three broken pages

I went looking for your free resources and found the front door locked, with a suggestion box standing where the key should be. Everything below is something you can open in a browser right now and see for yourself.

What I found

“Free Resources” is one of the main links in your top navigation. It points to /resources/. That page says exactly one thing to a teacher:

“Signup for access to all of our free resources for teachers of all languages! Thank you for connecting with TPRS, fill out the form and gain access to all our free resources.”

There is a form on that page. It just isn’t a signup form.

The only form on the Free Resources page is your testimonials form.
Its heading reads “Leave us a review”, and its fields are Name · Role · Product · Experience/Testimonial.

So the page instructs a teacher to “fill out the form and gain access to all our free resources,” and the form in front of them is asking for a product review.

That is the visible problem. The invisible one is worse, because it affects the pages people reach from search and from old links.

The rest of the free-resource family

Four different URLs promise free resources. Here is what each one actually serves:

URLVisible page contentForm on page
/resources/ 247 characters — the “fill out the form” copy Testimonials
/free/ 0 characters Testimonials
/free-resources-2/ 0 characters Testimonials
/freeresources/ 0 characters Testimonials

/free-resources/ redirects to /free-resources-2/ — which is one of the blank ones. So any link, bookmark, or search result pointing at the old address lands a teacher on an empty page with a review form on it.

The thing that proves it isn’t “just how the site looks”

I checked this against your homepage, because “no content” is the kind of claim that is usually my fault rather than the site’s:

Your site is fine. Your theme is fine. These four pages specifically are not — and they are the ones holding the free content, which is the most reliable first step a new teacher takes with you.

One small sitewide detail while I was in there: the testimonials form’s first field has no label. It renders the placeholder text Untitled (Required) to the person filling it out. Anyone who does decide to leave you a review is being asked to fill in a field called “Untitled.”

Why this is the funnel I’d fix first

  • It sits in your main navigation, so it is on every page of the site.
  • Free resources is where a teacher who is curious but not yet buying goes. Right now that road ends at a review form.
  • It is a small, contained fix. Nothing needs rebuilding — the pages need their real content, and the signup form needs to be the form on the page.
  • It is measurable. A working signup form on /resources/ gives you a number you do not currently have: how many teachers actually want the free material.
What I’d do, concretely. Put a real signup form on /resources/, point the three duplicate URLs at it so nothing lands on a blank page, and give the testimonials field a label. That is the whole job.
