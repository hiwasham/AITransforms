# 2. Set Up for Live Sessions & Office Hours

> Curriculum › Section 1: Getting Started › Lesson 2

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/curriculum-f7e760/sections/864454/lessons/3281821)

## Content

The live sessions are where the real magic happens – this is where you'll learn the core methodology, get your questions answered, and collaborate with fellow participants over the next 5 weeks.

## Add Sessions to Your Calendar

**Your action step:** Go to the [Calendar](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/calendar-83e687) space and click **"Add to calendar"** for each session you want to attend.

Good news: you're automatically RSVP'd to every session by default. This means you'll receive an email reminder 1 hour before each session starts, so you won't miss anything.

## How to Join Sessions

All sessions will be conducted via **Zoom**. The Zoom link will be provided in your calendar invite and also posted in the Live Sessions space.

It’s the same for every session as well as the Office Hours: [https://zoom.us/j/93642051746?pwd=22jcKNyk4neboBc0vcnz7b9F5pSqwb.1](https://zoom.us/j/93642051746?pwd=22jcKNyk4neboBc0vcnz7b9F5pSqwb.1)

## Technical Requirements

For the best experience:

- **Join from a laptop or desktop** using the [Zoom app](https://zoom.us/download) (mobile works, but isn't ideal)
- **Have your camera on** – we encourage active participation

- **Ensure a strong internet connection** – you'll want to fully engage without technical issues

## Session Preparation

We'll publish any pre-work or preparation materials with plenty of time before each session. Keep an eye on the [Announcements](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/announcements-4101a0) for updates.

## Session Recordings

Can't make a live session? No problem. All sessions will be recorded and made available in the curriculum just a few hours after the live session ends.

Remember: while recordings are available, the live experience – with real-time Q&A, group discussions, and immediate feedback – is where you'll get the most value from this program.

### Use of AI Meeting Agents

To protect participant privacy and foster open dialogue, we do *not* allow any external AI meeting agents, transcription tools, or note takers (like Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, etc.) to join our live sessions.

We will record all sessions ourselves and have a dedicated note-taker present. After each session, we’ll share the official recording and session notes with all participants.

This policy helps us ensure that anything shared during the sessions—especially sensitive details about your business—remains confidential and protected. We want everyone to feel safe discussing real challenges and insights without concern that their information will be distributed or stored by third-party AI tools.

If we detect any unauthorized AI note takers or bots in a session, we’ll remove them immediately.

Thank you for helping us maintain a secure and respectful learning environment.
