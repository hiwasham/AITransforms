## Content

To get the most out of this program, you'll need access to a capable LLM. Since all our demonstrations and examples will be done using Claude, we strongly recommend you use Claude as well.

## Why Claude?

Using the same AI tool as your instructors means:

- You can follow along with every demonstration
- The prompts and techniques we teach will work exactly as shown
- You'll get consistent results when practicing what you learn

## Get Claude Pro or Team

**You'll need a paid Claude account** to participate fully in this program. The free version has limitations that will prevent you from completing many of the exercises.

Choose between:

- **Claude Pro** ($20/month) - Perfect for individual use
- **Claude Team** ($25/month per user with 5 users minimum) - Better if you're planning to share with your team

→ **[Sign up for a paid Claude account](https://claude.ai/upgrade)**

## Download the Claude Desktop App

For the best experience, **download the Claude desktop app** rather than using it in your browser.
