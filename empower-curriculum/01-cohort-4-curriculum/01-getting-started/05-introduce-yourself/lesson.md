# 5. Introduce Yourself

> Curriculum › Section 1: Getting Started › Lesson 5

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/curriculum-f7e760/sections/864454/lessons/3281856)

## Content

Before we kick off, we’d love for you to introduce yourself to your fellow participants in the [Discussion](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449) space.

This program is designed to be collaborative. You’re not just learning from us—you’re learning from each other. Getting to know who’s here will make the experience richer for everyone.

→ **[Introduce Yourself](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449/)**

You can keep it casual, but here are five helpful things to include:

1. **Your location**: Where are you based in the world?
2. **Your role: **What do you do, and what’s your day-to-day like?
3. **Your business or organization: **What kind of company are you part of? What do you offer?
4. **Your current goals: **What are you hoping to improve or build during this program?
5. **Your interest in AI: **What drew you to this program? Have you already experimented with AI?
