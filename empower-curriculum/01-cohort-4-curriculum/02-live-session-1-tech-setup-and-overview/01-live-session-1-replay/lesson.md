## Summary

### EMPOWER Labs Cohort Hosting Logistics

Josh and Midori discussed the logistics of breakout rooms and session hosting for an upcoming cohort. They clarified that they are now hosting under EMPOWER Labs, a separate entity from Second Brain Enterprise, and agreed to mention Thiago as a co-creator but not as an active part of the program moving forward. They also reviewed the process for managing breakout rooms, welcoming participants, and using host tools to facilitate the session.

### Cohort Program Structure and Logistics

Josh and Midori discussed the structure and logistics of an upcoming cohort program. They reviewed the curriculum, calendar, and session details, including the use of Zoom links and event creation. Midori was briefed on her role in the program, which includes delivering the program introduction, circle overview, and breakout sessions, as well as facilitating discussions and collecting participant goals. They clarified that the program is 7 weeks long, with 12 sessions, and Josh noted some changes made to the schedule and documentation.

### Session Planning and Attendance Overview

Josh explained that he expects around 15 participants for the upcoming session, with about 15 people returning from the beta and 7 new sales. He noted that not everyone will attend live sessions, but all content will be recorded for later viewing. Josh also discussed the process of managing note-takers during the session, mentioning that he had to manually remove them up to 20 times in previous sessions due to a Zoom bug. The conversation then shifted to casual discussion about Josh's weekend with his father and Midori's experience dealing with a recent snowstorm.

### AI Business Transformation Program Kickoff

The EMPOWER Labs Cohort 4 kickoff meeting introduced the program to participants, with Midori and Josh explaining the structure and goals of the AI-focused business transformation program. The facilitators outlined the program's history of supporting over 100 companies and emphasized its focus on creating AI-powered operating systems. The session included introductions of the facilitation team, including Josh's background in digital agency work and Hayden's experience in scaling businesses, while participants shared their locations and weather conditions. The conversation ended with an overview of the program structure, tools, and master prompt concept, with the real work scheduled to begin after the session and continue through the next live session on Wednesday.

### EMPOWER Labs Program Overview

Josh introduced the 12-week EMPOWER Labs program, emphasizing its focus on practical business transformation rather than content lectures. He outlined the program's structure, including three live sessions this week and weekly sessions thereafter, with a recommendation to focus on one business aspect at a time for better results. Josh explained the use of AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT, introduced the master prompt framework, and described the program's interaction through Circle, a community platform where participants can engage 24/7 and access curriculum materials, exercises, and support.

### Program Learning Modes Overview

Midori outlined the three learning modes of the program: live sessions, hands-on exercises, and the Circle community platform. She emphasized the importance of active engagement and introduced the platform's features, including the curriculum section, discussion forums, and calendar for live sessions. Midori then led a breakout discussion for participants to introduce themselves and share their goals for the program. After the discussion, she instructed participants to reflect on patterns and goals that emerged during the breakout sessions.

### AI Beta Cohort Reflections

The meeting focused on discussing participants' goals and experiences from a previous beta cohort focused on AI processes. Participants shared their "one thing" they wanted to focus on, including operations documentation, product automation, and scaling processes. The group discussed using Claude as their LLM companion during the program, noting its strengths in understanding context and following complex instructions. Josh emphasized the importance of taking time to think deeply about business processes and encouraged participants to continue refining their approaches.

### Master Prompt Strategy for Claude

Josh discussed the importance of maintaining consistency across long conversations and highlighted Claude's strengths in strategic thinking and nuanced business applications. He recommended setting up a paid Claude account, especially a team account for easier collaboration, and introduced the concept of the master prompt as a foundational framework for transforming AI into a strategic business partner. Josh explained the five layers of context in the master prompt, including training data, prompt system information, user preferences, project knowledge, and chat inputs. He concluded by outlining steps for creating a clean setup of the master prompt in Claude, including setting up a new project titled "Master Prompt" and preparing for the next meeting on Wednesday.

### Cohort Project Setup in Claude

Josh explained the project setup for the cohort, focusing on a dedicated workspace for running prompts and evolving the master prompt. He demonstrated how to create a new project in Claude, run a master prompt assessment, and add the resulting artifact to the project. Josh also showed three options for adding artifacts to the project: directly adding as a Markdown file, downloading as a Markdown file and uploading manually, or downloading as a PDF. He emphasized the importance of keeping the project clean and focused, recommending leaving personal preferences blank during the cohort.

### Master Prompt Creation and Strategy

The meeting focused on explaining markdown files and discussing next steps for creating a master prompt. Josh explained the process of building a master prompt and encouraged participants to either start from scratch or use their previous versions, depending on their needs. Midori outlined the upcoming sessions, including a focus on the invisible structure of business and the EMPOWER operating system. Lee asked about updating the master prompt and received guidance from Josh on when to start fresh or build upon existing work. The session concluded with an open Q&A, where participants could seek clarity on any aspects of the process.

### AI Tools and Team Implementation

The discussion focused on AI tools and business processes, with Josh explaining that while Claude Enterprise is not currently in use, they offer EMPOWER for Teams to help companies scale their AI implementation. He emphasized that the cohort is about strategic planning rather than operational work, and described how Stage 3 software can help manage shared AI prompts while maintaining control over sensitive information. Nancy inquired about transitioning from individual Claude Pro accounts to a team version, and Josh clarified that while there are different account options, upgrading from individual to team accounts would not merge existing work.

### Stage 3 Prompt Management Discussion

The meeting focused on the use of Stage 3 for housing and sharing master prompts, with Josh recommending its use for efficiency and time-saving. Nancy expressed uncertainty about whether to consolidate her prompts into Stage 3 or continue using her current system, and Josh clarified that Stage 3 is primarily for storage and sharing, not for creating or editing prompts. Lee inquired about usage limits in Claude, and Josh explained that recent updates have increased token capacity and improved chat continuity. The session concluded with instructions for participants to create their first version of a master prompt and to prepare for the next meeting on Wednesday.
