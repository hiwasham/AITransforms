## Content

## **Purpose**

Create the first version of your Master Prompt, which will allow your LLM to provide you with more personalized advice.

---

## **Instructions**

1. Create a NEW Claude project titled “Master Prompt”
2. Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat in your project
3. Let Claude guide you through the questions. The more specific and honest your answers are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.
4. Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select “Copy to Project” to add the artifact to your project knowledge. (For a detailed explanation of artifacts in Claude, [click here](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/beginner-s-guide-to-claude/sections/748240/lessons/2837858).)

→ **[Access the Prompt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/130JlqKzDo6eQLeXve0yE9v54CSILlt1ahdnf4GSJp28/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.35bjhk15jx4w)**

**Time to complete: **20 - 30 minutes

## FAQs

### How do I use the same Claude account for different businesses?

We suggest creating different projects, one for each separate business, and creating separate Master Prompts for each business within these projects. This approach allows Claude to apply the relevant Master Prompt within each project, ensuring a clean state outside those projects.

### Should the Master Prompt be written in English or my native language?

**We recommend English, but your comfort and clarity matter most.**

**Why English works best:** Your AI will perform at its highest level when working in English. The business frameworks, strategic concepts, and industry knowledge that power the best AI advice are primarily developed in English. Since the entire SBE program is taught in English, keeping your Master Prompt in English also creates seamless integration with all course materials and ongoing AI interactions.

**When to use your native language:** If you find yourself struggling to express complex business details, industry nuances, or specific challenges clearly in English, switch to your native language for those sections. Poor or oversimplified business context will hurt the quality of advice you receive more than language barriers will.

**Our recommendation:** Start writing in English, but don't sacrifice clarity for language. If you get stuck on a question or feel like you're losing important details about your business, write that response in whatever language lets you be most precise and thorough.

**Remember**: The goal is to give your AI the clearest, most accurate picture of your business possible. Language is just the tool to get there.
