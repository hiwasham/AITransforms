## Content

Imagine a business as a journey that follows a clear path:

**1. Market to Lead:** We start by connecting with potential customers. This is where marketing happens – we're letting people know we exist and what we offer.

**2. Lead to Sale:** When someone shows interest, they become a lead. Our job now is to understand their needs and show how our product or service can help them. If we do this well, the lead becomes a customer.

**3. Sale to Delivery:** After a sale, we need to provide what we promised. This might mean shipping a product, implementing a service, or onboarding a new client.

**4. Delivery to Success:** The most important part - making sure our customers are successful with what we've provided. This means following up, providing support, and ensuring they're getting value.

## **Beyond the Basics: Creating a Virtuous Cycle**

In average businesses, the journey might end after delivery. But great businesses go beyond that and build a virtuous cycle that continuously builds on itself:

**5. Success to Lead:** When customers are successful, something amazing happens – they tell others about their experience. Happy customers become a source of new leads through:

- Referrals to friends and colleagues
- Testimonials that build trust
- Case studies that demonstrate value
- Repeat business and upsells

You need processes in your businesses that ensure the above happen consistently. These should link to KPIs and goals.

**6. Success to Market:** Customer success also feeds back into our marketing:

- Reviews and ratings boost our reputation
- Customer stories become powerful marketing content
- Real results provide data for our marketing claims
- Market insights help us refine our messaging

In our businesses, this is typically through a regular meeting that Success has with Marketing, and regular goals for both departments.

---

## **Why This Cycle Matters**

This isn't just a nice diagram – it's the difference between:

- Constantly hunting for new customers vs. having customers come to you
- Struggling with tight margins vs. enjoying profitable growth
- Working harder vs. working smarter

When your business processes form this virtuous cycle, each customer you serve makes it easier to find and serve the next one. Your business becomes more efficient, more profitable, and more enjoyable to run.

The most successful businesses aren't just good at each individual step – they're masterful at connecting these steps into a self-reinforcing cycle where success creates more success.

Through this program, we will be working with AI to help us develop processes for the customer lifecycle. We will start by visualizing what we are currently doing, as well as what we should be doing but aren’t actively doing. Our goal for Stage 1 is to have existing processes fully documented.

Then we will see how much of that AI can potentially automate.
