## Content

Now that you've created powerful SOPs with Claude's help, it's time to get them out of the AI and into a system your team will actually use every day.

The best SOP storage solution isn't necessarily the most advanced one—**it's the one your team will consistently access, update, and follow.**

That's why we recommend starting with tools your team already knows and uses, rather than forcing them to learn an entirely new platform.

The goal here is simple: **transform these SOPs from interesting documents into practical, living tools** that drive real improvements in how your business operates.

Here are 3 options to consider:

---

## 🗂️ Organizational Knowledge Management

**Examples**: [Notion](https://www.notion.so/), [Coda](https://coda.io/), [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/)

**Use when:**

- You already use one of these platforms for other business functions
- You want SOPs integrated with your broader business knowledge base
- Your team is comfortable with these tools
- You need rich formatting, databases, and cross-linking capabilities

*Select “Duplicate” in the upper right-hand corner to create a copy in your own Notion workspace. *

---

## ☁️ Shared Cloud Storage

**Examples**: Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, Box

**Use when:**

- You're just starting with SOP documentation
- Your team is already deeply embedded in Google or Microsoft ecosystems
- You want the simplest, most familiar solution
- You primarily need document storage with basic collaboration

## ⚙️ Specialized Software

**Examples**: [Process Street](https://www.process.st/), Stage 3 ERP (coming)

**Use when:**

- You need workflow automation and approval processes
- You want advanced analytics on process completion
- You have complex, multi-step processes requiring tracking
- You're ready to invest in process optimization and enforcement
