## **Purpose**

To assess your organization’s financial performance management process, gauge the owner involvement, and identify areas for improvement.

---

## 🚨 Note on Owner Involvement in Financial Oversight

Unlike other processes where we encourage delegation, financial oversight requires owner involvement for fiduciary responsibility, strategic decision-making, and business protection.

**The goal is *****efficient***** owner involvement, not elimination of owner involvement.**
