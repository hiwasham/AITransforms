# Exercise 8.1: Run the Workforce & Leadership Diagnostic

## Content

## **Purpose**

To conduct a comprehensive diagnostic to identify your biggest opportunities for organizational effectiveness, employee engagement, leadership development, and systematic delegation.

---

## **Instructions**:

1. Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat within your Master Prompt project.
2. Rename the chat “Workforce & Leadership (Diagnostic)” (so you can easily find it later).
3. Let Claude guide you through the process. The more specific and honest you are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.
4. Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select “Copy to Project” to add the artifact to your project knowledge.

*Note: This “diagnostic” is in a different format than the “assessment” prompts in previous exercises you have completed. This is to give you an example of how alternative formats can work, and also to illustrate that you will need to decide what parts of the resulting artifact are included in your Master Prompt.

As an alternative, you might decide you like the previous “assessment” prompt format and output better. If this is the case, this exercise can include you creating a new prompt of your own in the style of the “assessment” (including the scoring rubric, etc.)

Starting points for creating your own “assessment” prompt might include one of the prior assessments as an example, the PDF slides from this session, or your own insights. Don’t be afraid to have some FUN with this!
