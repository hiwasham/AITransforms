## **Preparation Before Starting**

Before continuing, collect these materials from previous sessions. You'll use them at specific points to get better AI advice.

### **📊 #1 – All Assessment & Diagnostic Results**

- **Why:** Shows AI your weakest processes objectively, allowing it to validate priority choices
- **When to use:** Phase 1 (Priority Assessment) - attach when discussing ratings

### **📋 #2 – All Core Process Level 1 SOPs**

- **Why:** AI can review your ACTUAL processes vs. giving generic advice and identify specific gaps
- **When to use:** Phase 3 (Implementation Planning) - attach the SOP for your CHOSEN process only

### **🗺️ #3 – All Blueprint Level 2 SOPs**

- **Why:** AI can optimize your real workflows instead of starting from scratch
- **When to use:** Phase 3 (Implementation Planning) - attach the Blueprint for your CHOSEN process only

### **📈 #4 – Any Implementation Plans from Previous Sessions**

- **Why:** Builds continuity, avoids repeating work, and references previous AI recommendations
- **When to use:** Phase 3 (Implementation Planning) - mention relevant plans for context
