# Exercise 9.3: Create Your Initial Implementation Plan

> Curriculum › Section 10: Live Session 9: Execution Systems › Lesson 4

## Content

## **Purpose**

To plan and prepare for the first process implementation in your business.

---

## **Instructions**

1. Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat within your Master Prompt project.
2. Rename the chat “Implementation” (so you can easily find it later).
3. Let Claude guide you through the process. The more specific and honest you are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.
4. Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select “Copy to Project” to add the artifact to your project knowledge.

→ **[Access the Prompt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/130JlqKzDo6eQLeXve0yE9v54CSILlt1ahdnf4GSJp28/edit?tab=t.8t6madvg18p#heading=h.2pvxifgoiw2i)**

**Time to complete:** 60-90 minutes

---

## When to Move Forward

You'll know you're ready to move forward with your plan when:

- You have a clear, defensible choice for first implementation with strong AI leverage potential
- Your AI Operator is selected, committed, and has a clear training path
- You have a specific, actionable 6-week plan with 5-day feedback cycles
- Your AI systems are set up to support implementation
- You're ready to begin pilot implementation immediately after Session 9

### **Time Management Tips for each Phase**

- **Phase 1:** Don't overthink ratings - go with gut instinct
- **Phase 2:** If you don't have an obvious AI Operator, that's okay - we'll address this in Session 9
- **Phase 3:** Focus on YOUR specific process, not perfect generic planning
- **Phase 4:** Keep prompts simple and practical for your AI Operator’s skill level
