## **Exercise 11.1: Clean Up and Generate Your AI Master Prompt**

**Purpose**

In this cohort, adding SOPs, guides, and blueprints to your project was intentional so Claude could learn your business deeply.

Now the goal is to clean and consolidate that content so your Master Prompt is high quality, not repetitive, and easier to maintain.

In this exercise, you will:

- Resolve conflicting information across files

- Remove duplicate or low-value repetition

- Keep the important SOP/guide content that improves output quality

- Generate a clean Master Prompt package you can activate in Stage 3

Target: keep your active Master Prompt as lean as possible (ideally around **80k tokens**), without removing important operating context.

**Time to complete:** 30-60 minutes

**Deadline:** Before Session 12

**Instructions**

**Step 1: Open Claude in Your Company MP Project**

- Go to [claude.ai](http://claude.ai/)

- Open your Company Master Prompt project

- Start a **NEW** chat

- Confirm the project name appears at the top

**Step 2: Run the Session 11.1 Prompt**

Copy and paste the Session 11.1 prompt:

→ **[Access the Prompt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/130JlqKzDo6eQLeXve0yE9v54CSILlt1ahdnf4GSJp28/edit?tab=t.e6h60txhezen)**

Claude will guide you one question at a time and help you:

- Find and resolve conflicts

- Remove redundancy

- Keep the right level of SOP/guide detail

- Produce final artifacts ready for Stage 3

**Step 3: Review the Artifacts Claude Produces**

By the end, you should have:

1. **Cleaned AI Master Prompt Source**

2. **Cleanup Change Log**

3. **Optional Supporting Reference Pack** (only if Claude identifies content that should be reference-only)

Quick review checklist:

- Core strategy, operating rules, and SOP intelligence are preserved

- Duplicate sections are removed

- Conflicts are resolved and documented

- Final content is clean enough to activate

**Next Steps After Exercise 11.1**

**Step 1: Add to Stage 3 (AI Master Prompts)**

1. Go to Stage 3 > **AI Master Prompts**

2. Create a New Master Prompt (top right button) and then click “Add Document” and add the cleaned file(s) Claude generated

3. Enable the file(s) you want included in your active AI Master Prompt

4. Click **Generate Master Prompt, **and click Set as Default.

**Step 2: Create a new project in Claude**

1. Create a new project called “Clean Master Prompt”
2. Add your file(s) as document sources
3. Use this project for future chats
