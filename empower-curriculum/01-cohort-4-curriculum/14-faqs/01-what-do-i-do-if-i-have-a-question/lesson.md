# What do I do if I have a question?

> Curriculum › Section 14: FAQs › Lesson 1

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/curriculum-f7e760/sections/872005/lessons/3304985)

## Content

## **1. Subject matter questions**

If you have questions about what’s being taught in EMPOWER Labs:

1. Use Circle’s search function to check if someone else has already asked the question, and the answer is available.
2. Post your question in the [Discussion](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449) space so all participants can benefit from it.

## **2. Technical & product support**

1. For technical support related to Circle (such as app concerns or login issues, etc.), please reach out to the Circle team at [support@circle.so](mailto:support@circle.so). To receive the quickest assistance, please make sure to contact them using the same email that is associated with your Circle profile.
2. For product support related to the  EMPOWER Labs itself, please email us at [support@empowerlabs.ai](mailto:support@empowerlabs.ai).

## **3. 1-on-1 concerns**

If you have a concern that requires personalized attention, please email [support@empowerlabs.ai](mailto:support@empowerlabs.ai).
