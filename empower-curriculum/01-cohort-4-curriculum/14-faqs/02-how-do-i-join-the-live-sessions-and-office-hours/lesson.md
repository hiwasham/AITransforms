## Content

## **1. Create your Zoom account**

We use Zoom to host the live sessions of the EMPOWER Labs.

If you don’t have a Zoom account yet, you can sign up for free by visiting the **[Zoom sign-up page](https://zoom.us/signup)** and entering your details.

## 2. Download the Zoom app

For the best experience, we suggest joining our live sessions via the Zoom desktop app. [You can download it here](https://zoom.us/download).

## 2. RSVP for the live sessions

All live sessions are posted in the [Calendar](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/calendar-83e687) space. Click RSVP to add them to your personal calendar. The starting time is displayed automatically in your time zone.

## **4. Join the live sessions**

To join the live sessions at the scheduled time, click “Join virtual event” on the live session page in Circle or click the Zoom link in the calendar invite.

The Zoom link for every live session is the same: [https://zoom.us/j/93642051746?pwd=22jcKNyk4neboBc0vcnz7b9F5pSqwb.1](https://zoom.us/j/93642051746?pwd=22jcKNyk4neboBc0vcnz7b9F5pSqwb.1)

We suggest joining from a quiet environment that allows you to have your camera switched on.
