## Summary

### Business Brain Knowledge Management Strategy

The group discussed the concept of a "business brain" and how to effectively manage and organize organizational knowledge. Hayden explained that a business brain requires deep, narrow snapshots of employee information, constant updates, and proactive agents to maintain accuracy and synchronization across platforms. Nancy shared her strategy for implementing this concept within her team, focusing on assessments and knowledge sharing. The discussion highlighted the importance of integrating various sources of information and maintaining a single source of truth within the organization.

### System Integration and Automation Updates

Hayden discussed the importance of teaching foundational skills and maintaining up-to-date systems, emphasizing the use of Stage 3 as a single source of truth to synchronize with Claude and other tools. Mike shared his progress on automating the proposal system using Claude, which has significantly improved efficiency and reduced manual errors. He anticipated saving 15 minutes per proposal, potentially saving managers 1-2 hours weekly. Mike also inquired about the integration of RAG and Slack with Salesforce, and Hayden suggested using both formalized SOPs and informal data calls to maintain up-to-date knowledge.

### Airtable Transition and AI Applications

Hayden explained that while he did not set up the Airtable instance, it was done by Empower Labs, and he is transitioning from Salesforce to Airtable due to licensing constraints. He discussed the diminishing necessity of RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) for most use cases, citing improvements in token windows and benchmarks, and suggested that better context management and cheaper tokens make RAG suitable primarily for sophisticated applications like chatbot creation. Nancy shared an example of an AI avatar's realistic and charming introduction, and Don inquired about bottom-up training, to which Hayden responded by mentioning their Teams program that trains teams on their roles and responsibilities.

### Employee-Driven AI Implementation Process

Hayden explained a process where employees create prompts to interview each other, generating artifacts that contribute to their role master prompts. This process helps surface commitments for process ownership, AI automation opportunities, and delegation possibilities. Hayden described how this bottom-up approach leads to the creation of personalized scorecards and KPIs, which employees review with their managers. The process culminates in a clarity board, which helps identify and prioritize AI implementation opportunities within the organization.

### Sales Management and Educational Updates

Hayden shared a screen recording of a sales management overview and committed to posting it in the correct forum, which Midori noted should be migrated to Cohort 4. Hayden also mentioned plans to publish educational videos on the EMPOWER Labs YouTube channel in the coming weeks. Pavlo, who was joining from Ukraine, shared his experience using NotebookLM to create Ukrainian presentations for his team, finding it a valuable resource for knowledge sharing and task management.

### SOP Download and Save Issues

Hayden and Pavlo discussed issues with downloading and saving SOPs to Stage 3, where Pavlo encountered problems with saving content and generating HTML. Hayden explained that SOPs are typically saved as Markdown text and suggested using the chatbot feature to create blueprints and guides, emphasizing the importance of enabling the master prompt toggle. Nancy suggested screen-sharing to better assist Pavlo, and Pavlo agreed to share his screen to demonstrate the issue further.

### SOP Management and AI Security

The meeting focused on troubleshooting issues with SOPs disappearing in Stage 3 and discussing file organization strategies. Hayden advised Pavlo to copy and paste content and suggested organizing files in Cloud Code with separate folders for processes and languages. Nancy inquired about managing multilingual SOPs, and Hayden recommended keeping versions in different folders. The group also discussed daily routines when working with AI, with Hayden sharing his experience of using Obsidian and Cloud Code for note-taking and task management. Mike raised concerns about AI security, and Hayden emphasized the importance of being cautious with permissions and using trusted marketplaces for skills. The conversation ended with Hayden clarifying that the beta cohort would have access to Stage 3 for as long as the API costs remain low.
