# Lesson 7: Understanding Privacy in Claude

> Beginner's Guide to Claude › Section 1: How to Use Claude › Lesson 7

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/beginner-s-guide-to-claude/sections/748240/lessons/2837873)

## Content

### **1. Claude is NOT private or confidential**

**Do not input**:

- Confidential business information
- Private client/customer data
- Personal health, financial, or legal records
- Trade secrets or regulated data (e.g. HIPAA, FERPA, GDPR-sensitive)

👉 **Rule of thumb**: If you wouldn’t share it publicly or with a third-party service provider, don’t paste it into Claude.

---

### **2. Claude may store and use your inputs**

- Claude’s models **may retain and use your inputs** to improve the service unless you're using a paid, API-based enterprise account with data controls.
- Anthropic says: *“We may use Content to provide, maintain, and improve the Services.”*
- Inputs may be reviewed by humans during evaluation or safety monitoring.

---

### **3. You’re responsible for the content you input**

- Users **must have the right** to input any content they submit to Claude.
- You **can’t input** copyrighted, proprietary, or illegal content without permission.
- No harmful, abusive, or deceptive content allowed (per Acceptable Use Policy).

---

### **4. No use for legal, medical, or safety-critical decisions**

- Claude is not a lawyer, doctor, or engineer — it **should not be relied on** for:
  - Legal advice
  - Medical decisions
  - Financial, regulatory, or life-critical systems
- It’s a tool for brainstorming, summarizing, or exploring—not for compliance-sensitive decision-making.

---

### **5. Compliance with laws and sensitive data rules**

- Anthropic’s Claude does **not guarantee compliance** with regulations like:
  - **GDPR**
  - **HIPAA**
  - **CCPA**
  - **SEC/FINRA, etc.**
- If your business is in a regulated industry, **don’t input regulated data** into Claude.
