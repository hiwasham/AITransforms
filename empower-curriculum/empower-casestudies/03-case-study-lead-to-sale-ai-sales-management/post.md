## The Starting Point

Acquira had a good problem: the 8X increase in B2B appointments from Case Study 2 meant the company suddenly needed to hire and train many salespeople quickly.

But sales management doesn't scale easily. Here's what the team faced:

### The Manual Process

- Salespeople manually typed CRM notes after calls (15 minutes each)
- Manual end-of-day reports (30 minutes each)
- Manager compiled reports daily (1+ hour)
- Manager reviewed random calls when time allowed (never enough)
- Weekly pipeline reviews and coaching

## Building the System with Claude

**The vision:** Let AI handle boring admin work so humans focus on selling and meaningful coaching.

### Working with Claude

Hayden and his team described what they wanted: "A system that automatically records sales calls, transcribes them, analyzes them against our sales methodology, scores objectively, identifies coaching opportunities, eliminates manual CRM entry, and generates all reports automatically."

Claude created Mermaid diagrams showing workflow, wrote technical specifications, and helped think through edge cases and integrations.

Acquira hired a developer for $20-30K to build what Claude had designed.

### The System

- **Bubbles** automatically joins and records every call
- **Instant transcription** in real-time
- **Salesforce integration** sends transcripts automatically
- **OpenAI API** analyzes transcripts using custom sales methodology prompt
- **Automated scoring** on multiple dimensions:
  - No-show vs. attended?
  - Did the rep ask for the sale?
  - Did they ask required qualification questions?
  - Talk-to-listen ratio
  - Did they explore pain points deeply?
  - Did they follow the methodology?
- **Slack notification** posts results to the sales team channel
- **End-of-day reports** received by the manager via Salesforce
- **Automatic flagging** alerts the manager of any low scored calls for review

**What salespeople do:** Make sure the call recorder is on. That's it. Everything else is automatic.

## The Results

### What Got Eliminated

- Manual CRM notes: 15 min/call saved
- End-of-day reports: 30 min/person/day saved
- Manager report compilation: 1+ hour/day saved
- Random call reviews: Replaced with targeted flagged reviews
- 9 Salesforce licenses: Down to 1 automation account

### What Got Enabled

- Can onboard 20+ salespeople monthly without adding managers
- Immediate quality feedback on every call
- Data-driven coaching: Managers only review flagged calls
- Consistent evaluation across all reps
- Faster, more confident hiring/firing decisions
- More accurate reporting (no human error)

## Conclusion: The Transformation is Just Beginning

These three case studies span approximately 18 months at Acquira. The results are substantial:

- **Case Study 1:** From break-even to $70,000+ monthly contribution margin
- **Case Study 2:** From $133 per seller call to $7 per call, with 10x more calls
- **Case Study 3:** Capability to scale from ~10 to 50+ sales reps without proportional management overhead

**Total impact:** Hundreds of thousands in annual profit improvement from these three implementations alone.

But perhaps more importantly, these cases demonstrate something about the nature of AI transformation:

- It's not about replacing people. It's about removing constraints that prevent people from creating more value.
- It's not about efficiency alone. It's about enabling entirely new possibilities.
- It's not about technology. It's about using technology to align operations with strategy and strengths.
