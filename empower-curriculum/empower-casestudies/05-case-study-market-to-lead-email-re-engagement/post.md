## The Business Context

Acquira operates a two-sided marketplace with both buyers and sellers of businesses. On the buyer side, the company works with prospective buyers in a B2C model.

The typical buyer is middle management in corporate looking to start their own business. These are professionals seeking to transition from corporate employment to business ownership.

Over the last five to six years, Acquira has primarily brought customers in through a combination of Meta and Adwords. During this time they have put together an email list close to 30,000 people.

Acquira's funnel optimization works through several stages:

Lead → Applicant → Super Applicant → Call Held → Closed

Sales come from both new leads and re-engaging existing contacts. The company maintains an email newsletter sent about twice a week to stay in touch with prospects in various segments: leads, applicants, and super applicants.

This segmentation allows Acquira to send targeted messages appropriate to each prospect's stage in the buying journey, moving them progressively toward enrollment.

## The Starting Point

Acquira had one full-time content manager being paid $80,000 annually handling:

- SEO blog content
- Email newsletters (twice weekly)
- Managing media buying vendors

**Key Metrics:**

- Email frequency: 2 newsletters per week (educational only)
- Cost: ~$80,000 annually
- Sales from re-engagement: 1-3 per month at $6,500
- Contribution margin: Break-even

## The Trigger

The content manager quit unexpectedly. Rather than immediately replace him, Hayden and his leadership team asked: "Who is the right person for each of these responsibilities?"

## Phase 1: VA + Claude

Instead of hiring another full-time employee, Acquira hired a virtual assistant and gave them the email newsletter duties. They used Claude AI for content generation at $50 per newsletter, three times per week.

**Result:** 70-80% cost reduction (from $6,500/month to ~$600/month) while increasing output 50%.

## Phase 2: Strategic Expansion with AI Texting

### The Strategic Context

Hayden, who had been serving as chairman, had to let the CEO go and step in as CEO himself. During quarterly planning, he realized the company had a massive opportunity: double down on the Acquira Accelerator product line with a goal to quadruple revenue.

The strategy: Get 50-60% increases across three conversion points—market to lead, lead to qualified, qualified to sale. This was highly scalable without new hires, and the company had strong NPS scores.

### The Implementation

A contractor who specialized in re-engagement came to Acquira. He'd run his own company for three years. His approach: text everyone in the database to re-engage them and set up calls on the closers' calendars.

Mid-implementation, Hayden realized the ad attribution was broken—the company was probably losing money. He paused all ads and went all-in on re-engagement. The result was their best month ever.

### The Tools

- **Woo Sender** enabled personalized texts at scale—thousands of messages that each felt one-to-one. "Hey John, how's your business search going?"
- **Rev Reply** managed one-to-one email conversations, handling back-and-forth until a human needed to step in.

**The Volume:** 15,000-20,000 texts per cycle and 30,000 emails.

The team had replaced someone expensive doing limited work with a VA and AI doing 50% more at 90% lower cost. Then they asked: How can we generate MORE revenue?

## Phase 3: Testing Direct Response Copy

A direct response copywriter approached Acquira: "You're only sending educational content. You're leaving money on the table."

The deal: $700 commission per sale from his emails.

His results: 10-20 calls scheduled per email. The educational emails? 1-2 calls.

Acquira changed the schedule:

- Monday, Wednesday, Friday: Educational
- Tuesday, Thursday: Direct response

The direct response emails spoke directly to pain points and converted exceptionally well. The company ran this for three months.

## Phase 4: Claude Outperforms the Expert

Hayden had been experimenting with Claude's Projects feature. He uploaded 25-30 of the copywriter's best emails to train Claude on his style.

Then Acquira surveyed the list: "What's your biggest pain point?" They got 50-100 responses and fed them to Claude for analysis.

**Top 4 pain points identified:** Financing, deal sourcing, timing, and due diligence.

Hayden told the VA: "Have Claude write emails about these pain points using the Project."

They kept the copywriter on for another month to run a direct comparison.

**Result:** Claude outperformed him 100% of the time—better opens, more calls scheduled, at essentially zero marginal cost versus his $5-6K monthly fee.

**Current state:** 5 emails per week. 3 educational (1-2 calls each), 2 direct response (10-20 calls each). The educational content keeps relationships warm and prevents the emails from feeling overly promotional—without it, subscribers would likely feel the company was constantly asking for something, leading to higher unsubscribe rates.

## The Complete Transformation

**Before AI (Early 2023):**

- Cost: $6,500/month
- Sales: 1-3/month at $6,500 each
- Revenue: $6,500-$19,500/month
- Profit: Break-even

**After AI (Late 2024):**

- Cost: $3,000/month
- Sales: 6-9/month at $12,500 each
- Revenue: $75,000-$112,500/month
- Profit: ~$70,000/month

Acquira went from break-even to $97,000 profitable while cutting costs in half.

## Key Success Factors

Let's look at what made this transformation work:

1. **The trigger was a problem, not a vision.** Hayden's first approach wasn't to use AI—it was finding the right person. The team tried three people before turning to AI.
2. **Claude 3.5 was the breakthrough.** When it launched in June 2024, the writing quality significantly improved.
3. **Train AI on best examples only.** The team gave Claude the top 25-30 emails, not average ones. It learned what actually worked.
4. **Survey the audience.** Those 50-100 responses gave Claude exactly what to write about. AI spots trends faster than humans.
5. **Mix education with sales.** The 3:2 ratio is critical. Pure sales gets unsubscribes. Educational content makes sales emails more effective.
6. **Respect frequency constraints.** Acquira texts every 60-90 days maximum. The list of 15,000 is split into 60 daily segments on a 60-day rotation. You can't just 10x volume without damaging relationships.
7. **Integrate everywhere.** The team added Woo Sender to the paid traffic funnel. When someone becomes a lead but doesn't complete the application, they get an automatic text. This improved lead-to-applicant conversion across all channels.
