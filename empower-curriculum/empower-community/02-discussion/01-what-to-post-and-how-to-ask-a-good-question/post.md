# What to post & how to ask a good question 🙋

> Discussion › Post 1 › by Ronnie Seguilla › 2026-01-07

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449/what-to-post-how-to-ask-a-good-question)

## Content

This is your place to **ask questions, share wins, and swap insights** as you move through this EMPOWER Labs cohort.

## **Here’s what you can post**

- **Questions** – about the material, implementation, or anything you’re stuck on
- **Reflections** – what’s resonating, shifting, or surprising you
- **Wins** – big or small progress moments worth celebrating
- **Tips & Tools** – anything you’ve discovered that might help others
- **Takeaways** – from the live sessions, exercises, or coaching calls

## **How to ask a great question**

The better your question, the better the support you’ll receive. Here’s a simple framework to get the most out of your post:

1. **Core process:** Which core process are you working on? (e.g. market to lead)
2. **Challenge:** Where exactly are you stuck or uncertain?
3. **What you’ve tried:** What have you already done or considered?
4. **What kind of input you want:** Do you want ideas, feedback, examples, or troubleshooting?

**Example:**

> “I’m creating my Master Prompt and trying to articulate my company’s core values. I’ve drafted 3 but they still feel generic. I’m looking for feedback or examples—how did you make yours feel truly unique?”

Feel free to be concise, vulnerable, or even messy. This is a beta experience—everything here is a work in progress.

We’re excited to learn with you ✨
