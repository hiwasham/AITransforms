# 🤘 unlock untapped potential

> Discussion › Post 4 › by Christian Zingg › 2026-01-23

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449/unlock-untapped-potential)

## Content

Hi everyone — I’m Christian, based in Munich, and I’m a Managing Partner at Creative Business Hacker (CBH), a management consultancy. Our claim is “Unlock Untapped Potential” — and we mean it in the most practical way possible: we don’t show up to decorate problems, we show up to change the reality of execution. Put bluntly: we’re not here to stay. We’re here to build momentum, make the system work better, and leave behind a setup that keeps delivering without us.

At my core, I’m an engineer. I’ve always loved fixing things — companies included. I’m drawn to complex situations where something important is stuck: decisions aren’t landing, teams are busy but outcomes don’t move, priorities blur, ownership is fuzzy, or the operating rhythm is missing. My default instinct is to debug: find the constraint, isolate the true cause, simplify, and turn it into a system people can actually run.

Day-to-day, my work sits right where strategy meets execution: product strategy, operating models, decision-making under pressure, and making transformation tangible. I design and facilitate workshops, build pragmatic templates and cadences, align stakeholders, and create structures that help teams move faster with fewer rework loops. I’m not interested in “activity.” I’m interested in measurable progress and better decisions.

What makes me a little dangerous (in a good way): curiosity. I’m relentlessly curious — almost annoyingly so — and I tend to pull threads until something clicks. That’s also why I’m deeply into bleeding-edge AI. Not because it’s trendy, but because it’s leverage. I like tools that compress time: faster synthesis, sharper preparation, cleaner decision support, and repeatable workflows that scale. I’ve been experimenting heavily with LLM-based workflows and systems that turn messy context into clear next steps — and I’m always trying to squeeze out one more level of clarity, speed, and impact.

In this program, my goal is to push that even further: to use AI as a serious operating tool, not a toy. I want workflows that reliably produce better thinking, better decisions, and better execution — and that help unlock the kind of hidden potential that’s usually buried under friction, complexity, and “we’ve always done it this way.”

That’s the game I like playing: find the untapped potential, remove the friction, and get the system to move.

🤘Christian
