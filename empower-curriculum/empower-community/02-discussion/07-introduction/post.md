## Content

I’m based in the **Pacific Northwest near Seattle**.

I operate in **both** an enterprise and founder capacity. By day, I’m a senior technology and product leader at a **Microsoft**, focused on large-scale AI and data platforms, AI-ready data foundations, and responsible AI adoption. In parallel, I’m a founder building AI-native digital products, where I design systems, architecture, and operating models hands-on.

I’m building a **health-focused technology platform** that combines a simple consumer-facing execution layer with a centralized intelligence and data layer. The intent is to keep user experience straightforward and accessible while concentrating personalization, reasoning, and evidence-based insights in a shared AI foundation that can scale across products.

My current focus is improving **execution speed, AI architecture decisions, and regulatory-safe design and operating as a solopreneur**. I’m working on refining agentic systems, clarifying what AI should versus shouldn’t do, and building defensible, scalable AI foundations without sacrificing trust, safety, or simplicity.

I’ve already experimented extensively with AI across multiple models, tools, and agentic patterns. What drew me to this program is the opportunity to deepen my **AI operating model as a solopreneur**, pressure-test my approach, and learn from others.
