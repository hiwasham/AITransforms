## Content

Hey everyone! 👋

I'm Tim, joining you from **Nürnberg, Germany**, where I work as a Management Consultant at Creative Business Hacker GmbH. (together with Christian)

My day-to-day is all about helping companies and their leadership tackle business, product, organizational, and people challenges. I'm a former startup founder (AdTech company called Mindmee), and now I focus on creating impact fast – we're not about those endless consulting mandates, but rather about building sustainable solutions that actually work. Think process optimization, system design, and getting organizations to run smoothly.

At CBH we are obsessed with Time-to-Impact. Our approach is hands-on and pragmatic – we dive into the real problems, build the systems and processes needed, and make sure our clients see results quickly without the typical consulting overhead.

Right now we're entering a scaling phase at CBH, and I want to build the foundation right from the start. My main focus is automating all the administrative stuff so we can dedicate our energy to what really matters – delivering value to our clients. I'm also keen on establishing processes that are AI-native from day one and getting certifications to open doors to larger clients.

I've been experimenting with AI quite a bit already. I wrote my entire thesis on AI adoption and future workplace skills – using AI for everything from the outline to literature research, data analysis, and writing. During my startup days, I curated tons of AI content and built people-focused learning materials. Right now, I'm playing around with NotebookLM to better leverage my personal knowledge base. What excites me most about this program is the focus on unlocking and untapping potential – finding those friction points and eliminating them. I'm here to take CBH to the next level with AI.

Looking forward to learning and growing with all of you!😁
