## Content

Hi all,

I'm based in Switzerland and lead a boutique consultancy with all aspects such a role brings with—mainly organising resources and coordinating strategy implementation. We sell Medtech regulatory affairs knowledge to manufacturers, importers and other B2B. We presume AI will transform our business into a hybrid artificial-human-offering where we will keep the creative, strategic tasks and AI will take over the repetitive work like writing reports, opinions, presentations, etc. On top, at least in Europe and Switzerland, the regulatory dynamic is constantly increasing. This makes knowledge management crucial—finding the up-to-date KN in no time. We must become more efficient by using AI. We work with the industry standard, an ISO 13485 quality management system, using Google Workspace and Gemini Pro. We prepare to take the next level with agentic AI in a RAG, using Gemini Enterprise or an on premise platform.

Working with an ISO QMS is already a lot of documentation (SOPs, etc.) but this is rigid and not living as an OS in the daily business. I want to lead the company to the next level by understanding on how to make the difference using AI.
