# (Delayed) Intro...

> Discussion › Post 20 › by Kristina › 2026-01-30

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-b92449/delayed-intro)

## Content

My name is Kristina and I live in Andorra, but our business is based in Germany.

I am an entrepreneur and shareholder of ms gartenreich. My role is not operational day-to-day execution, but strategic ownership and entrepreneurial support. I work closely with Jonas, who carries overall operational responsibility, by helping to structure the business, clarify decision frameworks, and challenge assumptions—especially where complexity, long-term risk, or structural weaknesses appear. My focus is on creating clarity, stability, and long-term viability rather than short-term optimization.

ms gartenreich develops, leases, and manages weekend and holiday home communities. The business is built on long-term lease contracts, recurring payments, regulatory constraints, and operational processes that must work reliably over many years.  
Today, the complexity does not come from growth alone, but from historically grown structures, contracts that are legally valid yet operationally difficult, and increasing regulatory and cost-related dynamics. This makes ms gartenreich a very real, non-theoretical environment to work on fundamental operating questions.

Our goal in this program is to build a clear and robust operating model for ms gartenreich—starting from first principles.  
Before introducing new tools, systems, or automation, we want to clearly define what *must* work at the core of the business for money to flow and risk to remain controlled. This includes clarifying contractual truth, sources of financial obligations, decision boundaries, and minimum operational standards.  
For me personally, the goal is to help create a structure that allows the business to be operable, understandable, and resilient—independent of individual people or historical workarounds.  
I am interested in AI as a structural and cognitive leverage, not as a productivity shortcut. What drew me to this program is the opportunity to use AI to support clarity: making implicit knowledge explicit, reducing ambiguity, and strengthening decision-making in a complex, contract-driven business.  
We see AI as a potential partner in building and maintaining a robust operating model—*after* the fundamentals are clearly defined.
