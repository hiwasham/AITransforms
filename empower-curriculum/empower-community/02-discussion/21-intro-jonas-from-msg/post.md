## Content

Hey, I’m Jonas, based in Düsseldorf, Germany, and since September last year I’ve been the “employed CEO” (it *feels* a lot like co-founder energy) of ms gartenreich GmbH (msg).  
  
I’m a trained business psychologist and worked with various startups and corporates in Germany.   
  
**Why we exist: **We believe that a root connection to what truly matters is becoming ever more important. So we create places where people can recharge, reconnect with themselves, with nature, and with the people they care about.

**What we do:** We develop, lease, and manage weekend and holiday home communities. We're not selling square meters. We're selling energy, clarity, and an escape that's 2 hours away instead of a flight. At least that’s our ambition and we work hard to make it reality.

**My role:** I carry overall operational responsibility. Right now my main mission is less “do more” and more make the business legible: laying down our operating system, defining clean processes, identifying dependencies, and rebuilding the structure around an *ideal* future state (instead of patching today’s chaos). That means I'm in the weeds daily, like stabilizing operations, building the team, creating systems where there were none, and figuring out what actually needs to work for this business to run reliably. I work closely with Kristina, who handles strategic ownership and helps me structure decisions, challenge assumptions, and think long-term when I'm tempted to just fix whatever is on fire.

**What we're here to do:** Build a clear, robust operating model from first principles. Before we automate anything, we need to understand what must work at the core – contractual truth, financial obligations, decision boundaries, operational standards. The complexity here isn't theoretical; it's historically grown structures and contracts that are legally valid but operationally messy.

**Why AI:** I see it as a thinking partner, not a shortcut. I already use it daily for strategy, process design, and pressure-testing my logic. I'm here to push that further – using AI to make implicit knowledge explicit and support better decisions in a complex, contract-driven business.
