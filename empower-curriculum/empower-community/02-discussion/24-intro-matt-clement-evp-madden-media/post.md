# Intro: Matt Clement, EVP @ Madden Media

## Content

Hi everyone,   
  
I’m Matt, and base out of Fort Worth, Texas, though that's more of the mailing address than a real anchor point—I'm on the road 100-130 nights a year for speaking and client work (think George Clooney in ‘Up in the Air’ but not quite as good looking, lol).

I'm EVP at Madden Media where I run the strategic consulting division, called Madden Shift. Day-to-day oscillates between strategic planning facilitation with DMO clients, business development for the agency, and thought leadership work—workshops, 20+ conference talks annually, and plenty of innovation work. My cognitive architecture (which includes a severe case of ADHD) makes me good at high-complexity strategic problems and innovation, terrible at operational maintenance…I can work 20 straight hours on strategy or a new framework…but expense reports for 30 minutes? Nope.   
  
My company, Madden Media, is a 340-person destination marketing agency serving destination marketing organizations across the US and Canada (DMOs for short…if you’ve ever seen an ad for ‘Visit California’ or ‘Visit Orlando’…that’s our business). My division does strategic consulting, filling the gap between aspirational master plans and actual execution using consulting frameworks like Play to Win, 5-C Analysis, McKinsey 7S, and a few of our own creation here at Madden. My workflow is heavily dependent on leveraging AI platforms effectively.

I've been using LLMs extensively for the past 2 years, primarily integrating Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, NotebookLM, and Claude into planning workflows. I'm here to sharpen execution, learn AI workflow design and prepare for what’s coming with agentic AI in the next year or so.  
  
Look forward to see y’all around!  
  
Matt
