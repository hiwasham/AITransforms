## **We Did 10 Times More With the Same Team. Here Is What Changed.**

Many companies use AI to make the founder faster.

That may improve personal productivity, but it does not necessarily make the business more scalable.

When the founder still provides all the context, makes every key decision, and designs every workflow, AI can create a new bottleneck instead of removing one.

In this video, Hayden explains how to move from **founder-centered AI** to an **AI-Assisted Team**—where your people and AI operate from the same playbook to execute faster, more consistently, and with greater independence.

### **You'll learn:**

- The difference between founder-centered AI and an AI-Assisted Team
- How to create a shared operating system—or "business brain"—that both your team and AI can use
- Why shared context is critical for faster and more consistent execution
- A practical five-step framework for building an AI-Assisted Team
- How AI can help team members lead high-impact initiatives without creating leadership bottlenecks

The goal isn't simply to make one person faster.

It's to build a business where your entire team can execute with greater speed, consistency, confidence, and independence.
