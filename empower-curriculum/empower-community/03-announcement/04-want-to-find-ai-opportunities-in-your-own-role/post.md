## **I Let AI Interview Me About My Job. It Found AI Opportunities I Was Missing.**

If someone asked you where AI fits in your role, what would you say?

Most people can't answer that—not because they don't understand AI, but because they've never mapped how they actually spend their time.

In this video, Hayden introduces the **Clarityboard**—a structured AI-powered interview that helps you document your role, identify where your time goes, and uncover AI opportunities your job description can't reveal.

### **In this video, you'll learn:**

🤖 Why traditional job descriptions no longer reflect how work actually happens

📋 What a Clarityboard is and why every role should have one

🧠 The seven-phase framework for mapping your role with AI

📊 How to identify tasks you can automate, AI-assist, delegate, or eliminate

🚀 How to turn your Clarityboard into an action plan with your manager

By the end of the process, you'll have a complete picture of your role—including your responsibilities, KPIs, meetings, processes, and a prioritized list of AI opportunities to help you work more effectively.
