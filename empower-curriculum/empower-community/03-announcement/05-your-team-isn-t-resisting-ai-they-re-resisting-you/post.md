# Your Team Isn’t Resisting AI. They’re Resisting You.

> Announcement › Post 5 › by Ronnie Seguilla › 2026-07-20

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/announcement/your-team-isn-t-resisting-ai-they-re-resisting-you)

## Content

Most AI rollouts don't fail during training—they fail two weeks later.

The team attends the training. They agree AI is valuable. Leadership invests in the tools. But a few weeks later, everyone is back to updating the CRM manually, writing emails from scratch, and following the same old workflows.

The problem isn't the AI.

It's the adoption system.

### In this video, you'll learn:

🤖 Why only about **20%** of employees become consistent AI users after a typical rollout

👥 The **four gaps** blocking AI adoption: leadership, time, early wins, and standards

🚀 The four changes leaders must make to turn AI into part of their team's everyday workflow

⚙️ How to build an AI adoption system that creates lasting behavior change—not just temporary excitement

One of the biggest takeaways is that successful AI adoption isn't about having better AI tools. It's about building a better system where leaders model AI use, teams have protected time to learn, early wins are celebrated, and clear standards are established.
