## Content

Most teams think meeting productivity ends with an AI-generated summary.

It doesn't.

The real challenge begins after the meeting—capturing decisions, assigning ownership, tracking action items, and making sure the work actually gets done.

The problem isn't taking notes.

**It's turning conversations into execution.**

### **In this video, you'll learn:**

📝 How to use ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex to summarize meetings, extract action items, and draft follow-up messages

⚙️ The difference between using AI as a one-off productivity tool versus building a repeatable meeting workflow

📂 Why every meeting needs a structured operating record—not just a transcript

🤖 How AI can automatically prepare meetings, capture decisions, identify risks, assign owners, and generate next-meeting preparation

✅ Why the most effective AI workflows always include human review before anything becomes official

One of the biggest takeaways is that AI doesn't create execution—**systems do**. The real value comes from giving AI output a permanent home, a review process, and a destination where work can be tracked until it's complete.
