# Your Team Hit Every Priority. Your Company Got Less Profitable.

> Announcement › Post 10 › by Ronnie › 2026-08-18

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/announcement/your-team-hit-every-priority-your-company-got-less-profitable)

## Content

Most teams think finishing every quarterly priority means they're making progress.

It doesn't.

A company can finish every quarterly priority and still become less profitable.

The problem isn't that your team isn't working hard.

**It's that they may be working on the wrong things.**

In this video, Hayden explains why that happens and how to fix it using Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) starting with the P&L, translating the company's most important problem into measurable Key Results, and using AI to keep results visible and flag problems early.

### **In this video, you'll learn:**

📊 How to work backward from the P&L to identify the operating cause behind a profitability problem

🎯 How to turn a company Objective into measurable Key Results with a baseline, target, owner, and date

⚙️ The difference between an Objective, Key Result, Project, and Task—and why most companies start with the work and attach it to strategy afterward

👥 How leadership and teams can create aligned OKRs while giving the team a role in choosing the road

🚦 How to use **Now, Later, and Parked** so everything isn't treated as a priority

🤖 How AI can pressure-test your strategy by identifying missing baselines, targets, owners, dates, and dependencies

One of the biggest takeaways is that **AI doesn't choose the strategy. It exposes gaps in the strategy.** AI can keep the system visible, but leadership still decides what matters.

## **Ready to Audit Your Team's Priorities?**

Use the **OKR Alignment Audit Prompt** to run the same test Hayden used with the leadership team.

Give it **one important P&L trend and your current priorities**.

It will help you identify:

- Which priorities address the real problem
- Which are outcomes and which are just projects or tasks
- What belongs in **Now, Later, or Parked**

You'll get the alignment audit first. Then, if you choose, it will help you build the company OKRs, align one team at a time, and create the weekly review register.

It won't invent the strategy, targets, or owners.

**Let AI find the gaps. You decide what matters.**
