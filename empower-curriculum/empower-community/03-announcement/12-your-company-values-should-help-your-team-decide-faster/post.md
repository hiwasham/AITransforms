# Your Company Values Should Help Your Team Decide Faster

> Announcement › Post 12 › by Ronnie › 2026-08-27

[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/announcement/your-company-values-are-useless-until-they-help-your-team-decide)

## Content

If two managers read the same company value and make two different decisions, the value isn’t helping. It’s just a word on the wall.

The problem isn’t necessarily that your company has the wrong values. Often, the words are there but the rule behind them is missing.

A value becomes useful when your team can use it to decide what to do next, without waiting for the owner.

## **💡 Key Takeaway**

A poster doesn’t change behavior. The real value of a company value is whether it helps your team make better decisions in real situations. Choose one value, connect it to a recent decision, and define what good behavior should look like next time.
