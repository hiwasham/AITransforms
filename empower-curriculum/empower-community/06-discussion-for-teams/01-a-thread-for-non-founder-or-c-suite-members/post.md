[Open on Circle](https://community.empowerlabs.ai/c/discussion-for-teams-18cf6a/a-thread-for-non-founder-or-c-suite-members)

## Content

Not everyone here runs the company, but many of us are still leading change within. @mention thought it might help to have one space for those figuring out how to put these ideas into motion from the inside.  
  
Peter noticed that most of the conversations in our cohort naturally lean toward the founder or executive perspective, which makes sense given the group and the work we’re doing. But he thought it might be helpful to have a spot for those approaching this work from a different seat.

This space is for those considering how to translate these ideas into action when you’re not the founder, owner, or in the C-suite—working to build or adapt an AI operating system within an existing structure, where you influence direction but don’t set it outright.

Post questions, ideas, and creative solutions that seem to work in this context, and hopefully, this can be a place where we help each other figure out how to make this effective within our seats on the bus.
