## The EMPOWER Framework: What Must Exist First

Before AI can transform your business, you need three things documented:

### A complete map of your processes

Not whatever happens to be documented. The 8 core processes across Customer Flow, Money Flow, and People Flow.

Infrastructure

### Documentation at four levels

Strategic overview, functional breakdown, step-by-step guides, and daily templates. Not just high-level or overly detailed.

Hierarchy

### Clarity on what matters

Not all processes affect value equally. You need to know which ones to fix first to maximize enterprise value.

Strategy
