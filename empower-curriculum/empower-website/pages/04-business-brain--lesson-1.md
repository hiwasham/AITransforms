# Create Your AI Business Advisor

Stop getting generic advice. In this session, we'll install your specific business context into AI to create a strategic partner that knows your company as well as you do.

## What is a Master Prompt?

Think of it like this: every time you ask ChatGPT or Claude a question, it starts from zero. It doesn't know your business model, your team structure, your current challenges, or your strategic goals. So you get generic advice.

A Master Prompt changes that. It's a comprehensive document that teaches AI about YOUR specific business. Once you have it, every conversation with AI becomes infinitely more useful.

Without Master Prompt

"Should I hire another salesperson or an operations person?"

"Here are the general pros and cons of each role. Salespeople generate revenue, while operations people improve efficiency..." [Generic 500-word Wikipedia answer]

With Master Prompt

"Should I hire another salesperson or an operations person?"

"Based on your current team of 8 people, your 80-85% close rate, and the delivery capacity constraints you mentioned in Section 3, I recommend an Operations hire first. Adding sales now would likely break your fulfillment..."

## Create Your Master Prompt

Time needed: 30 minutesOutput: Master Prompt v1.0

Instructions:

- Create a NEW Claude project titled "Master Prompt"

- Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat in your project

- Let Claude guide you through the questions. The more specific and honest your answers are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.

- Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select "Copy to Project" to add the artifact to your project knowledge.

Pro tip: Use voice-to-text if you prefer talking over typing. It's often faster to just "ramble" your answers and let Claude structure them.

System_Prompt.txt

Copy Prompt

Help me create a comprehensive master prompt that I can share with you to get better, more personalized assistance and advice. I'll answer questions across 6 key areas, and then you'll compile my responses into a polished master prompt document. Instructions: Ask me questions one by one from one section at a time Wait for my complete responses before moving to the next section Feel free to ask follow-up questions if my answers need clarification After I've answered all questions from all sections, create an artifact containing my complete master prompt Let's start with section 1: 1. Personal & Company Identity What's your name and role in the company? What's your company name and industry? How many employees do you have currently? What's your annual revenue range? (Under $1M, $1-5M, $5-25M, $25M+) 2. Offers & Ideal Customer Who is your ideal customer? What's the main problem you solve for customers and what outcome do they achieve? What products or services do you offer? (Include your main revenue drivers and approximate % of total revenue) What new products or services are you planning to offer in the future (if any)? 3. Current State & Challenges What are your top 3 business challenges right now? What's the most important decision you're facing in the next 3 months? Where do you feel stuck or uncertain in your business? 4. Competitive Landscape Who are your 2-3 main competitors? What's your key differentiator? (What do you do better than anyone else?) What competitive threats keep you up at night? 5. Company Culture & Values What's your company's mission? (Why do you exist?) What are your core values? (The 3-5 principles that guide behavior) What are your organization's greatest strengths? (Where have you succeeded where others failed?) What are your biggest organizational weaknesses or blind spots? 6. Team & KPIs Who do you report to and what are the main KPIs/metrics that you are responsible for? Who are your key team members and what are their primary responsibilities and KPIs?
