## From Assessment to Documentation to Augmentation

This is intensive transformation work delivered through 11 live 90-minute sessions. We move through three phases in a specific sequence: assess what should exist, document it four levels deep, then enable AI augmentation.

01

Phase 1: Foundation

### What we're building: The assessment and framework

We'll begin with a tech prep session and deep dives into the frameworks that define where your business sits and what it needs next.

1

#### Tech Setup & Master Prompt V1

Configure your tech stack (Claude Team & Circle), define specific cohort outcomes, and build the V1 of your 'Master Prompt' context engine. This is your initial context foundation - we'll build it out as we document processes in Phase 2.

2

#### The 5 Stages of Business Maturity

Where are you today? Stage 1 (Heroic Effort), Stage 2 (Team-Led), Stage 3 (Process-Driven), Stage 4 (AI-Augmented), or Stage 5 (AI-Operated)? Most businesses are stuck in Stage 1 or 2, want to be Stage 4, but can't skip Stage 3 (documentation). EMPOWER is the bridge.

3

#### The EMPOWER Framework

Master the 7-part EMPOWER framework to audit your business health and valuation potential. You will run deep-dive process assessments, generate instant SOPs, and identify specific gaps to reduce owner dependency. This is the assessment tool: which of your processes affect business value most? We use seven value drivers to prioritize what to document first.

02

Phase 2: Documentation Sprint

### What we're building: Documented processes four levels deep

This is where the real work happens. For each of the 7 EMPOWER dimensions, we map what processes should exist, document them four levels deep, build your Master Prompt with real business context, and create the foundation for AI augmentation.

4

#### Expansion Capability

We're documenting the '5Rs of Customer Excellence' - not just describing them. You'll use AI to accelerate the documentation work (not augmentation - that comes later). Output: Customer lifecycle documented four levels deep, added to Master Prompt.

5

#### Marketing & Brand Equity

Develop a 'Category of One' strategy to command pricing power. You will analyze unit economics (CAC vs LTV), leverage AI to optimize 4 acquisition channels (inbound, outbound, paid, partnerships), and use Claude as a fractional CRO to refine your unique value proposition.

6

#### Profitability Metrics

Master the 'Seven Financial Levers' to drive cash flow and enterprise value. You will use AI to model pricing strategies, optimize cost structures, and build a financial performance hierarchy that turns data hygiene into strategic foresight.

7

#### Offering Excellence

Optimize your offering and sales process using the 'Sales Spectrum' and 'Jobs to be Done' frameworks. You will leverage AI to automate CRM workflows, generate real-time coaching, and create hyper-personalized proposals to increase conversion rates.

8

#### Workforce & Leadership

Implement a workforce transformation framework covering leadership structure, delegation, and retention. You will use AI to analyze RACI models for workload distribution, facilitate performance management conversations, and run the 'AI Hiring Protocol' to generate job descriptions and oncologist schedules.

9

#### Execution Systems

Establish a dynamic execution framework with clear prioritization and systematic communication rhythms. You will use AI to optimize meeting cadences, define the 'AI Operator' role, and run an execution assessment to identify high-impact areas for automation.

10

#### Robust Market Position

Solidify your market position by leveraging AI for competitive analysis and product expansion strategies. You will run impact scoring models to prioritize new offerings, conduct cost-benefit analyses, and build a future-proofing roadmap to adapt to rapid technological shifts.

03

Phase 3: Launch

### What we're building: Your ongoing systems

We wrap with a final session focused on sustainability: integrating your new documentation with your existing rhythm and building your long-term AI roadmap.

11

#### Sustainable AI Integration

Now that processes are documented, we create your AI augmentation roadmap and integration plan: How to maintain your Master Prompt, how to integrate with established operating systems (if applicable), how to enable AI augmentation systematically, and your 12-month continuous improvement plan.

That's exactly why EMPOWER exists. Training doesn't stick when processes aren't documented. You learn skills with nothing substantial to apply them to.
