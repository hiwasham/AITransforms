## The Transformation Roadmap

A surgical intervention to remove bottlenecks and install an autonomous operating framework.

The Friction

Executive Misalignment & Strategic Chaos

Phase 0

### Onboarding & Change Management

- •Align leadership on success criteria and high-value drivers

- •Build Company 'North Star' Master Prompt v1 to anchor all future work

- •Design incentive structures and draft internal change comms

- •Finalize participant list and assign individuals to specialized sprints

- •Establish organizational 'Rules of Engagement' and protocols

The Outcome

Unified leadership foundation ready for deployment

The Friction

Scattered Knowledge & Undocumented tribal Expertise

Phase 1

### EMPOWER 101

- •Train team on Empower process taxonomy and documentation standards

- •Document department processes 4-levels deep using AI-acceleration

- •Build custom Role Master Prompts for every cohort participant

- •Run Org-vs-Role alignment checks to surface strategic gaps

- •Map process ownership and define 'License to Operate' protocols

The Outcome

Documented organizational brain and AI-ready context

The Friction

Disconnected AI Experiments & Gut-Feel Prioritization

Phase 2

### Strategic Alignment

- •Identify key value drivers across the business (revenue, speed, error reduction)

- •Use AI to estimate Enterprise Value (EV) and ROI impact ranges

- •Build a simple scoring model for each initiative based on complexity and risk

- •Run a workflow-automation analysis to map projects to specific owners

- •Build a 'Now / Later / Park' AI project portfolio to reduce organizational noise

The Outcome

Prioritized AI project portfolio with clear ROI metrics

The Friction

Stale Acquisition Channels & High-Friction Sales

Phase 3

### Growth Engine

- •Map core growth lanes (new customers, upsell, reactivation, channel partners)

- •Assign lane owners and define primary KPIs for each growth category

- •Use AI to analyze current funnels and surface highest-leverage bottlenecks

- •Design and ship live growth workflows for account discovery and scoring

- •Capture prompts and checklists into a reusable organizational Growth Playbook

The Outcome

Scalable revenue workflows running with AI

The Friction

Fuzzy ROI & Fragmented Idea Scoping

Phase 4

### Operations Core

- •Build the 'Operational Spine' to turn ideas into Amazon-style project memos

- •Use AI to model EV/ROI estimates and prioritize the project portfolio

- •Design AI-assisted project management and task-orchestration flows

- •Rapid-prototype internal tools and workflows using low-code or AI-assisted code

- •Establish mid-sprint review cadences to tighten scope and build ownership

The Outcome

Surgical 'Operational Spine' with rapid-prototyping speed

The Friction

High Hiring Friction & Talent Knowledge Leaks

Phase 5

### People System

- •Use the AI Role Design Protocol to turn tribal knowledge into explicit scorecards

- •Install AI-Accelerated Topgrading for high-fidelity screening and deep-dive interviews

- •Generate 30-day strategic onboarding plans tied directly to documented workflows

- •Implement the ClarityBoard framework to connect role design to performance management

- •Build a library of role scorecards and onboarding playbooks for high-impact roles

The Outcome

Self-sustaining talent system ready for systematic scale
