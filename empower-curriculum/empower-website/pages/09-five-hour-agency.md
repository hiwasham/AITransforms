THE STRATEGIC OUTREACH ARCHITECT V6

Input Required

COPY PROMPT

MASTER_PROMPT_v6.0.sh

Act as a Senior Growth Strategist and Master of Outbound Psychology. Your objective is to transform raw prospect data into a hyper-personalized outreach sequence that positions the sender as a peer, not a solicitor. INPUT DATA REQUIRED: - My Ideal Client Profile (ICP) & Core Offer: [Insert Your ICP and Core Offer Data Here] - Prospect Digital Footprint (LinkedIn Bio, Recent Posts, Podcast Transcripts): [Paste Prospect Data Here] STEP 1: COGNITIVE ANALYSIS 1. SEARCH FOR "STRATEGIC ANCHORS": Identify 3 non-obvious details from the prospect's data (a specific phrase they used, a contrarian opinion they hold, or a recent strategic shift in their company). 2. THE PAIN BRIDGE: Map my Core Offer directly to a specific risk they are currently facing based on their role or company stage. STEP 2: ARCHITECT THE SEQUENCE Generate a 3-step personalized outreach sequence: MESSAGE 1 (The Peer Observation): - Goal: Low-friction connection. - Structure: Start with the Strategic Anchor. Connect it to a high-level industry trend. 3 sentences max. - Call to Action: A "Yes/No" question about their internal roadmap. No links. MESSAGE 2 (The Case-Study Parallel): - Goal: Social proof + value. - Structure: "I noticed [Strategic Anchor] and it reminded me of [Similar Client Outcome]." Explain the "Unseen Risk" of their current path in 2 sentences. MESSAGE 3 (The Strategic Gift): - Goal: Reciprocity. - Structure: Offer a "1-Page Audit" or "2-Minute Personalized Loom" based on their specific [Pain Bridge]. TONE CONSTRAINTS: - No generic praise ("Love what you're doing"). - Zero exclamation marks. - High-status, consultative, and slightly skeptical. - Write as if you are a busy founder messaging another busy founder.

Input Architecture Protocol:

First, paste a detailed summary of your ICP and Core Offer—the AI needs to know the 'Transformation' you provide before it looks at the lead. Then, provide the prospect's LinkedIn 'About' section or their last 3 social posts. High-fidelity outreach happens when the AI bridges the lead's specific current pain with your unique 'Value Pivot'.

THE EXECUTIVE NARRATOR PROTOCOL V6

Input Required

COPY PROMPT

MASTER_PROMPT_v6.0.sh

Act as a Fractional CMO and Strategic Data Analyst. Your goal is to move from "Reporting on Data" to "Prescribing Strategy" by transforming raw metrics into an executive narrative. RAW METRICS: [Paste CSV or Metrics Table Here] CONTEXT: [Describe Major events, years, or market conditions this month] DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL: 1. THE "SO WHAT?" FILTER: For every major metric shift (up or down), identify the bottom-line business impact in 1-2 sentences. Explain it to a CEO who cares about "Pipeline Velocity," not "CTR." 2. THE WINNER/LOSER AUDIT: Identify the single top-performing variable and the biggest "Budget Leak." Hypothesize the cause based on the provided context. 3. THE STRATEGIC FORECAST: Provide 3 concrete "Pivots" for next month. (Stop doing X, Start doing Y, Double down on Z). 4. THE RETENTION BRIDGE: Draft a personal message to the client highlighting a specific "Board-Level Win" they can use to demonstrate their department's value. GOAL: Prove ROI through narrative clarity and strategic foresight, not spreadsheet volume.

Input Architecture Protocol:

Paste your raw CSV metrics, but crucially add a 'Context Overlay': describe 2 major events that happened this month (e.g., 'a competitor launched a sale' or 'we changed our lead magnet'). AI can analyze numbers, but it needs your market observations to move from 'what happened' to 'what to do next'.

Input Required

COPY PROMPT

Act as a Skeptical Fractional CFO. Analyze my financial data to identify "Profit Leaks" and "Margin Expansion" opportunities. FINANCIAL DATA: [Paste P&L, Expense List, and Revenue by Service/Segment Data Here] ANALYSIS PROTOCOL: 1.
