Why Run It

## AI works better when the customer path is clear first.

Most teams feel pressure to automate, but their customer paths already have messy handoffs, unclear ownership, repeated manual work, lost context, or reporting gaps. The audit helps you see what to fix first so AI improves the lifecycle instead of speeding up a broken process.

### Find the friction that matters

Pinpoint where a customer path slows down, loses context, repeats work, or creates an inconsistent customer experience.

### Know what to fix before AI

Separate real AI opportunities from ownership, documentation, data, review, or handoff problems that should be solved first.

### Leave with a practical report

Get a generated audit report with the diagnosis, readiness gaps, priority order, quick wins, and one recommended next step.

## Know what your free AI audit report will show.

The audit is designed to answer a simple question: if you spend time on this, what do you get out of it? You get a practical diagnosis of the customer path you chose, plus the improvement opportunities and next steps that matter most.

### Lifecycle success definition

A plain-English view of what the selected customer path should achieve when it is working well.

### Current workflow snapshot

A start-to-finish summary of how the lifecycle begins, moves, hands off, slows down, and reaches an outcome.

### Bottlenecks and risk signals

A grounded diagnosis of delays, rework, unclear ownership, context loss, quality issues, and visibility gaps.

### AI opportunity areas

Specific places AI could responsibly improve speed, quality, consistency, visibility, or decision-making.

### Process fixes before automation

Non-AI improvements in ownership, documentation, data, review, or workflow that may need to happen first.

### Priority order and next step

A practical sequence of quick wins, higher-leverage opportunities, readiness gaps, and the next move to make.

Questions Before You Start

## Clear expectations before you begin.

The audit is meant to be practical, focused, and useful even if you are still unsure where AI belongs in the business.

### What do I get at the end?

You get a generated AI Audit report with a workflow snapshot, bottlenecks, risk signals, AI opportunities, process fixes, readiness gaps, a priority order, and a recommended next step.

### Which customer path should I choose?

Start with the path creating the most friction now. That might be lead generation, sales, onboarding, delivery, retention, referrals, or another customer path you want to improve.

### Can I run more than one audit?

Yes. Each report focuses on one customer path at a time so the diagnosis stays specific. You can run another audit later for a different lifecycle path.

### Do I need to know where AI should go?

No. The audit is diagnostic. It helps you see whether AI is actually useful, or whether process, ownership, data, or documentation should be fixed first.

### Is this a generic business audit?

No. The audit stays anchored to the selected customer lifecycle instead of becoming a broad department, role, or software inventory exercise.

### Is this a sales call?

No. The first step is the guided audit and generated report. Afterward, the report can point toward the most practical next path if you want deeper support.
