High-resolution logo and wordmark assets for the EMPOWER Labs global identity system.

### Clear Space

Ensure a "safe zone" around the logo that is at least 10% of the total width. Avoid placing text or other icons too close to the mark.

### Background Rules

Always prioritize legibility. Use the White background version for light documents and the Dark version for presentations or dark-themed marketing.

### Logo Integrity

Do not skew, stretch, or rotate the logo. Use only the approved Power Blue and Slate colors defined in the official style guide.
