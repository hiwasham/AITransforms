# EMPOWER Labs – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 23, 2025

EMPOWER Labs (1001358394 Ontario Inc.) (“EMPOWER Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you access or use our websites, applications, tools (including Stage 3), training programs, cohort programs, team programs, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy applies globally and is intended to comply with applicable data protection and privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), UK GDPR, Canadian privacy laws, and applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope and Relationship to Other Agreements

This Privacy Policy applies to all users, participants, customers, and visitors (“you”).

This Privacy Policy supplements, and does not replace:

- EMPOWER Labs Terms of Service

- Any applicable Master Services Agreement (“MSA”) and Order Form

- Any program-specific participation terms

In the event of a conflict, the applicable agreement governing your use of the Services controls.

## 2. Our Role (Controller vs. Processor)

EMPOWER Labs generally acts as a data controller with respect to personal information collected through our websites, cohort programs, and individual participation in the Services.

In certain team-based or enterprise engagements governed by an MSA or similar agreement, EMPOWER Labs may act as a service provider or data processor with respect to business data submitted on behalf of a customer. In such cases, processing is performed in accordance with the applicable agreement and customer instructions.

## 3. Information We Collect

### a. Information You Provide Directly

You may provide personal or business-related information, including:

- Name, email address, phone number

- Company name, role, and organizational details

- Billing and payment information (processed by third-party payment processors)

- Account credentials

- Applications, assessments, surveys, and forms

- Content submitted through the Services, including:

- business process documentation

- operational workflows

- SOPs and internal frameworks

- master prompts and prompt amendments

- information relating to customers, employees, contractors, vendors, or internal operations that you choose to input

- Communications with us

You acknowledge that some information you submit may be business-sensitive or confidential, and you are solely responsible for the content you choose to provide.

### b. Information Collected Automatically

- IP address

- Device identifiers

- Browser and operating system information

- Usage data and logs

- Session data and interaction metrics

- Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 7)

### c. Information from Third Parties

- Payment confirmations and limited billing data from payment processors

- Platform usage data from third-party platforms

- Analytics data (where consented)

## 4. Sensitive Information

You should not submit sensitive personal information through the Services unless strictly necessary and appropriate. This includes, without limitation:

- government-issued identification numbers

- financial account numbers in free-text fields

- health or biometric data

- information about minors

If you choose to submit such information, you do so at your own risk. EMPOWER Labs reserves the right to delete sensitive information where appropriate and disclaims liability for user-submitted sensitive data to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Services

- Deliver cohort programs, team programs, and tools

- Enable and manage access to Stage 3 and related platforms

- Process payments and invoices

- Communicate with users

- Improve, test, and develop Services

- Monitor performance, security, and usage

- Enforce our agreements

- Comply with legal obligations

- Protect the rights, property, and safety of EMPOWER Labs and others

Stage 3 and related tools may store and process information you submit, including:

- internal business processes

- operational assessments

- workflows, SOPs, and blueprints

- master prompts and related inputs

- information relating to your customers, employees, or contractors

Live or interactive sessions associated with the Services may be recorded. Recordings may include your name, voice, image, communications, or submissions.

You acknowledge that:

- EMPOWER Labs does not control or verify the accuracy or legality of user-submitted content;

- you are responsible for ensuring you have the right to submit any information you provide;

- you remain responsible for compliance with applicable laws relating to such information.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, EMPOWER Labs disclaims liability for user-submitted business or personal data.

## 7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the Services.

### Cookie Categories

- Strictly Necessary Cookies – essential for functionality and security

- Analytics Cookies – used to understand usage and improve Services (consent required)

- Marketing / Performance Cookies – used for marketing and performance analysis (consent required)

You may manage cookie preferences via the cookie banner or the “Cookie Preferences” link in the footer. Consent is stored for up to twelve (12) months unless withdrawn earlier.

To operate the Services, EMPOWER Labs uses internal systems and third-party service providers.

Information may be:

- processed through workflow automation tools;

- shared with collaboration, documentation, accounting, analytics, marketing, or communication platforms;

We may engage subprocessors from time to time. All such parties are granted access only as necessary and are subject to contractual confidentiality, data protection, and security obligations.
