*\#* 📦*Business Process Framework*

*This document outlines the strategic and operational process architecture of the business, organized into three tiers: \*\*Strategic Planning\*\*, \*\*Customer Lifecycle\*\*, and \*\*Enabling Processes\*\*.  It shows who in the organization is Responsible, Accountable and Consulted in each process, and if there is no one there, or it says X or Future, it means we plan on updating it in the future.*

*\#\# 🧠 STRATEGIC PLANNING* 

*Strategic Planning defines the company's direction, resource allocation, and long-term positioning.*

***Environmental Scanning**: Monitoring external factors including market trends, competitive landscape, regulatory changes, and emerging technologies to identify opportunities and threats*

***Vision, Mission & Values Definition**: Establishing and refining the organization's long-term vision, core mission, and fundamental values that guide decision-making and culture*

***Customer & Market Strategy**: Defining target customer segments, market positioning, and competitive differentiation strategies to drive sustainable growth*

***Brand Strategy**: Developing and maintaining brand identity, messaging, and positioning to create consistent market presence and customer perception*

***Strategy Formulation**: Translating vision and market analysis into specific strategic initiatives, priorities, and resource allocation decisions*

***Innovation Strategy**: Planning and prioritizing new product/service development, technology adoption, and process improvements to maintain competitive advantage*

***New Product/Service Strategy**: Systematic approach to identifying, developing, and launching new offerings that align with market needs and business objectives*

***Goal Setting & Strategic Initiative Planning**: Converting strategic direction into specific, measurable objectives and coordinated action plans with clear ownership and timelines*

***Strategy Communication & Alignment**: Ensuring strategic priorities and objectives are clearly communicated and understood across all levels of the organization*

*\#\# 🔁 CORE VALUE CHAIN: The Customer Lifecycle*

*These represent the \*\*customer lifecycle\*\* from initial awareness to advocacy and re-engagement.*

*\#\#\# 🎯 1\. MARKET TO LEAD*    
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Generate interest and turn strangers into MQLs*    
*\*\*Buyer Stage:\*\* Awareness → Interest*  

*\- ✅ Market Research & ICP Definition*    
*\- ✅ Positioning, Messaging & Channel Strategy*    
*\- ✅ Content Development & Campaigns*    
*\- ✅ Paid / Owned / Earned Media*    
*\- ✅ SEO & CRO*    
*\- ✅ Brand Monitoring & Sentiment Tracking*    
*\- ✅ Marketing Automation & Lead Capture*    
*\- ✅ Attribution & ROI Analytics*  

*\*\*🔄 Output →\*\* Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)*

*\#\#\# 🤝 2\. LEAD TO SALE*   
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Convert MQLs into paying customers*    
*\*\*Buyer Stage:\*\* Interest → Evaluation → Purchase*  

*\- ✅ Lead Scoring & Qualification*    
*\- ✅ Owned Media Content Marketing*    
*\- ✅ Owned Media Direct Outreach*    
*\- ✅ SDR / Setter Process*    
*\- ✅ Sales Enablement & Collateral*    
*\- ✅ Discovery & Demo Calls*    
*\- ✅ Objection Handling & Follow-up*    
*\- ✅ Quoting & Proposal Management*    
*\- ✅ CRM Pipeline Reviews & Forecasting*    
*\- ✅ Sales Team Coaching*  

*\*\*🔄 Output →\*\* Signed Contract / Closed-Won Deal*

*\#\#\# 🛫 3\. SALE TO DELIVERY*   
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Seamlessly onboard clients and prepare for delivery*    
*\*\*Buyer Stage:\*\* Purchase → Onboarding*  

*\- ✅ Contract Finalization & Internal Handoff*    
*\- ✅ Welcome Sequences / Client Onboarding*    
*\- ✅ Scheduling & Delivery Planning*    
*\- ✅ Success Metric Definition*    
*\- ✅ Asset / Info Collection*    
*\- ✅ Journey Mapping & Team Assignment*  

*\*\*🔄 Output →\*\* Delivery Kickoff \+ Fully Onboarded Client*

*\#\#\# 🧩 4\. DELIVERY TO SUCCESS*    
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Deliver value and build long-term satisfaction*    
*\*\*Buyer Stage:\*\* Onboarding → Value Realization*  

*\- ✅ Fulfillment / Service Execution*    
*\- ✅ KPI Tracking & Reporting*    
*\- ✅ QA / Approvals*    
*\- ✅ Customer Success Management*    
*\- ✅ Feedback Collection (NPS, CSAT)*    
*\- ✅ Issue Resolution*    
*\- ✅ Retention Strategies*  

*\*\*🔄 Output →\*\* Measured Success \+ Retained Client*

*\#\#\# 🔁 5\. SUCCESS TO LEAD*     
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Convert satisfied customers into growth engines*    
*\*\*Buyer Stage:\*\* Value Realization → Advocacy*  

*\- ✅ Case Study / Testimonial Gathering*    
*\- ✅ Review & Referral Program*    
*\- ✅ Cross-Sell / Upsell Opportunities*    
*\- ✅ Customer Community Engagement*    
*\- ✅ Ambassador & Affiliate Strategy*    
*\- ✅ Co-Marketing & Advocate Activation*  

*\*\*🔄 Output →\*\* New Referrals \+ Expansion Revenue*

*\#\#\# 🔄 6\. SUCCESS TO MARKET*    
*\*\*Goal:\*\* Feed advocacy back into the top of the funnel*  

*\- Past clients refer others → re-enter \*\*Lead to Sale\*\**   
*\- Upsold clients re-enter \*\*Sale to Delivery\*\**    
*\- Reviewers generate organic MQLs → re-enter \*\*Market to Lead\*\**  

*\#\# 🛠 ENABLING PROCESSES*

*These processes provide \*\*infrastructure, insight, and capacity\*\* to support the Core and Strategic functions.*

***Order to Cash**: Managing the financial flow from customer orders to revenue collection*  
***Supply Chain & Asset Management**: Sourcing, storing, and maintaining the physical resources needed to deliver value*  
***Information Technology & Knowledge Management**: IT operations, security, system maintenance, and organizing data and insights to support decision-making across the business*  
***Operational Scheduling & Deployment**: Scheduling and deploying operational resources including workforce, equipment, machinery, vehicles, and tools to ensure efficient service delivery and project execution*  
***Financial Performance Management**: Tracking, analyzing, and optimizing financial outcomes*  
***Sustainability & Environmental Management**: Minimizing negative impacts and creating positive change*  
***Vendor/Supplier Management**: Managing vendor relationships and expenditures*  
***Hire to Retire**: Building and supporting your team through recruitment, development, and offboarding*  
***Plan to Report**: Setting goals and measuring progress through KPIs and reporting*  
***Partnerships Management**: Developing and maintaining strategic relationships with other organizations*  
***Risk/Crisis Management**: Identifying, mitigating, and responding to potential threats*  
***Continuous Improvement**: Systematically enhancing processes across all areas of the business*  
***Quality Management**: Establishing standards, implementing control systems, and continuously monitoring to ensure consistent delivery of products/services that meet or exceed customer expectations across all business functions*  
***Legal & Compliance**: Managing regulatory requirements, contract administration, intellectual property protection, and ensuring adherence to applicable laws and industry standards across all business operations*

We're evaluating how effectively you move customers from 'yes' to 'ready for work to begin' \- including contract handling, onboarding, information gathering, expectation setting, and planning.*

*\#\#\# Scoring Framework*  
*Each question uses a scale of 7-5-3-1-0, where:*  
*\- \*\*7 points \= Excellent systems and processes in place\*\**  
*\- \*\*5 points \= Good systems that need minor improvements\*\**  
*\- \*\*3 points \= Basic systems exist but need significant improvement\*\**  
*\- \*\*1 point \= Minimal or ad-hoc approach\*\**  
*\- \*\*0 points \= No systems exist or unknown\*\**

*For each question, you'll also evaluate owner involvement using the RACI framework:*  
*\- \*\*Not at all / Informed (one-way communication only) \= 5 points\*\**  
*\- \*\*Consulted (provides input but isn't directly involved) \= 3 points\*\**  
*\- \*\*Accountable (has final approval/oversight but doesn't do the work) \= 1 point\*\**  
*\- \*\*Responsible (actively does the work) \= 0 points\*\**

*Higher scores on owner involvement indicate better delegation and less owner-dependent processes.*

*Always ask one question at a time (consisting of the process evaluation and the owner's involvement) and await my answer before moving on to the next.*

**Purpose**: To define how you differentiate yourself in the market so you can communicate effectively why customers should choose you over competitors. 

**Instructions**: 

1. Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat within your Master Prompt project.  
2. Rename the chat “Market to Lead (UVP)” (so you can easily find it later).  
3. Let Claude guide you through the process. The more specific and honest you are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.  
4. Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select “Copy to Project” to add the artifact to your project knowledge. 

**Time to complete:** 15-20 minutes 

---

## Prompt

*I need to develop a compelling Unique Value Proposition for my business. You already know my business and ideal customer profile from my Master Prompt.* 

*I want you to guide me through a structured conversation with these phases:*

***\# Phase 1: Problem Refinement***

*\#\# Ask me 3 questions \- ONE AT A TIME. For each question:*

*1\. First answer the question yourself based on what you know about my business*

*2\. Present your answer and ask me to confirm, edit, or completely change it*

*3\. Wait for my response before moving to the next question*

*\#\# The 3 questions should cover:*

*1\. The specific problem my ideal customers face*

*2\. What it costs them NOT to solve this problem*

*3\. How they currently try (and fail) to solve it*

*When helpful, create 5 options, rank them by priority yourself with explanations for your ranking, then ask me: "Which of these resonates most with you, or would you like to see more options or hybrid combinations of 2-3 of these?"*

***\# Phase 2: Competitive Differentiation***

*\#\# Ask me 3 questions \- ONE AT A TIME \- using the same approach:*

*1\. Answer each question based on my Master Prompt knowledge first*

*2\. Present your answer for my confirmation/editing*

*3\. Wait for my response before proceeding*

*\#\# The 3 questions should cover:*

*1\. What I do that competitors can't or won't do*

*2\. My unique methodology, process, or advantage*  

*3\. How I deliver better outcomes (faster, more likely success, less effort)*

*When presenting differentiation options, create 5 possibilities, rank them by competitive strength with explanations, then ask: "Which differentiation angle is strongest for your market, or should I create hybrid versions combining elements from multiple options?"*

***\# Phase 3: UVP Creation***

*\#\# Ask me 2 final questions using the same approach:*

*1\. What specific, measurable outcome customers get from my differentiators*

*2\. What's the primary alternative customers consider instead of working with me*

*Then create 5 different UVP versions using this framework:*

*"For \[target customer\] who \[problem\], we are the only \[category\] that \[key differentiator\] so you can \[specific outcome\] without \[common alternative drawback\]."*

*Rank these 5 UVP versions by market impact and explain your reasoning, then ask: "Which UVP version has the strongest market appeal for your business, or would you like me to create hybrid versions combining the best elements from multiple options?"*

***\# Advanced UVP Refinement (Optional):***

*If you want to mix and match elements from different UVP versions, you can specify exactly which components you prefer. For example:*

*\- "I like the target customer from \#3, the problem from \#5, the category from \#2, the differentiator from \#5, and the specific outcome from \#3"*

*\- Then ask for 4-5 ways to express this hybrid combination more compellingly, ranked by effectiveness*

*\- Finally, if you like 80% of a version but want to refine one specific part, ask for 3 alternative options for just that component*

***\# Phase 4: Master Prompt Integration***

*After I select my preferred UVP, create a separate artifact titled "UVP \- \[Today’s Date\]" that includes:*

*\- My refined UVP*

*\- Key differentiators* 

*\- Competitive positioning guidance*

*\- Instructions for AI to reference this UVP in business discussions*

***\# IMPORTANT:*** 

*\- Always answer your own questions first based on my Master Prompt*

*\- When offering options, rank them yourself with clear explanations for your priority order*

*\- Always ask if I want to see more options or hybrid combinations*

*\- Wait for my response before moving to the next question*

*\- CREATE A SEPARATE ARTIFACT as the output \- don't just include it in our conversation*

*Let's start with Phase 1\. What specific problem do my ideal customers face? Answer this yourself first based on what you know about my business, then ask for my input.*

**Purpose**: To understand the performance of your marketing channels with the goal of optimizing your customer acquisition strategy.

## Prompt

*I need to understand my marketing channel performance and unit economics to help optimize my customer acquisition strategy. You already know my business from my Master Prompt, so guide me through this systematically.*

*I want you to guide me through a structured conversation with these phases:*

***Phase 1: Product Foundation** \- Ask me 3 questions \- ONE AT A TIME. For each question:*

1. *First answer the question yourself based on what you know about my business*  
2. *Present your answer and ask me to confirm, edit, or completely change it*  
3. *Wait for my response before moving to the next question*

*The 3 questions should include:*

1. *What is your primary revenue-generating product/service?*  
2. *What is the annual revenue from this product/service?*  
3. *What is a brief description of what this product/service delivers?*

*Here’s an example format you can use:*

***\[“Let's start with Phase 1\. What is your primary revenue-generating product or service?***

*Based on what I know about your business from your Master Prompt, I believe your primary revenue generator is \[answer based on my business\], generating approximately $\[estimate\] annually. This product/service delivers \[brief description of value\].*

*Is this correct, or would you like to edit or refine this product/service assessment?”\]*

***Phase 2: Paid Advertising Channel Analysis** \- For each paid channel, ask me ONE AT A TIME:*

1. *First tell me what this channel is and provide a specific example of how it would work for my business*  
2. *Present your assessment of whether I likely use this channel based on my business model*  
3. *If I use it, ask for the specific metrics, and ask me questions about how documented my current processes are, scoring them as 0 (non-existent) 1, 3, 5, 7*  
4. ***If I don't use it, ask: "Would you be open to testing this channel? What's holding you back – budget, time, or uncertainty about ROI?"***

*Start with the channels most relevant to my business model, then cover others.*

*Based on my business, prioritize and explain these channels with MY industry-specific examples:*

* ***Meta (Facebook/Instagram Ads)**: "For your \[specific business type\], this would typically be used to \[specific example based on my business \- e.g., 'target business owners interested in acquisition education' or 'reach homeowners needing plumbing services'\]."*  
* ***Google Ads**: "For your business, this would mean \[specific search terms and use cases for my industry\]."*  
* ***LinkedIn Ads**: "Given your B2B focus, LinkedIn would be used to \[specific targeting and messaging for my business type\]."*  
* ***YouTube Ads**: "For your business, YouTube ads would \[specific content and targeting strategy\]."*  
* ***Other Paid Channels**: Only ask about channels relevant to my business model.*

***Phase 3: Organic/Content Channel Analysis** \- For each organic channel, follow the same approach:*

1. *Explain what the channel is and provide a specific example of how my business would use it*  
2. *Assess if I likely use it based on my business model and target audience*  
3. *If I use it, get % of customers and % that are "warm" (existing followers/subscribers)*  
4. ***If I don't use it, ask:"Would you be interested in exploring this channel? What's prevented you from using it so far?"***  
5. ***Ask about past content performance**: What content has worked best for driving customers from this channel?*

*Start with channels most relevant to my business:*

* ***YouTube Organic**: "For your business, YouTube content would typically focus on \[specific content topics relevant to my industry and audience\]."*  
* ***LinkedIn Organic**: "Given your target audience, LinkedIn content would be \[specific professional content strategy for my business\]."*  
* ***Google SEO**: "For your business, organic search would target keywords like \[specific examples based on my services/products\]."*  
* ***Email Marketing**: "Your email strategy would likely involve \[specific nurturing sequences and content for my customer journey\]."*  
* ***Other Organic Channels**: Only discuss channels that make sense for my business model.*

***For each channel I use, ask these content performance questions:***

* *What specific pieces of content (topics, formats, headlines) have generated the most leads/customers?*  
* *What content topics get the highest engagement from your audience?*  
* *What content formats work best for you (long-form, short-form, video, written, etc.)?*  
* *Which content pieces do your customers reference when they buy from you?*  
* *What content topics do your competitors create that you don't?*

***Phase 4: Direct Outreach & Referrals** \- Ask about:*

1. ***Email Marketing**: Explain difference between nurturing existing list vs. cold outreach*  
2. ***Text/SMS Marketing**: Explain compliance and warm vs. cold texting*  
3. ***Referral Programs**: Explain difference between customer referrals vs. partner referrals*

***Phase 5: Unit Economics Education & Assessment \-** This is the most critical section. I need you to teach me these concepts and test my understanding ONE STEP AT A TIME:*

***Step 1: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Education** \- Explain: "CAC is the VARIABLE cost to acquire one new customer. It's calculated as (Variable Marketing Costs ONLY) ÷ (Number of New Customers Acquired). This includes ad spend and other costs that scale with volume. DO NOT include fixed costs like salaries or software subscriptions \- those are used for breakeven analysis. Different channels have different CACs \- for example, Google Ads might cost $200 per customer, LinkedIn might be $800, while referrals might only cost $50."*

*Ask me: "Based on this explanation, if you spent $3,000 in VARIABLE marketing costs last month (ads \+ commissions) and acquired 25 new customers, what would your CAC be?"*

***WAIT FOR MY ANSWER AND CONFIRM UNDERSTANDING BEFORE PROCEEDING.***

***Step 2: Get My Blended CAC** \- After confirming I understand the calculation, ask ONLY: "What do you estimate your overall blended CAC (across all channels) to be?"*

***WAIT FOR MY ANSWER BEFORE PROCEEDING.***

***Step 3: Get My Primary Channel CAC** \- Ask ONLY: "What do you estimate your CAC is for your primary marketing channel?"*

***WAIT FOR MY ANSWER BEFORE PROCEEDING.***

***Step 4: CAC Tracking Assessment** Ask ONLY: "Do you currently track CAC by individual channel? (Yes/No/Somewhat)"*

***WAIT FOR MY ANSWER BEFORE PROCEEDING TO 90-DAY VALUE EDUCATION***

***Step 5: 90-Day Customer Value Education** \- Explain: "90-Day Customer Value is calculated as: (Revenue from customer in first 90 days) \- (Cost of goods sold) \- (Cost of sales like commissions). This is your true gross profit after all variable costs. We subtract commissions HERE, not in the CAC calculation. This shows your actual profit per customer and helps with cash flow planning. For your business specifically, this would include \[specific revenue streams and upsells relevant to my business model\]."*

*Ask me: "If a customer pays you $1,000, your cost of goods sold is $300, and you pay $100 in sales commissions, what's your 90-day customer value?"*

***WAIT FOR MY ANSWER AND CONFIRM UNDERSTANDING BEFORE PROCEEDING***

***Step 6: Get My Actual 90-Day Value** \- Ask ONLY: "What's the gross profit you typically make from a customer in their first 90 days?"*
