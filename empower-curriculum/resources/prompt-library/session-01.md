# Session 1

# Create Your Master Prompt v.1

**Purpose**: To create the first version of your Master Prompt, which will allow your LLM to provide you with more personalized advice. 

**Instructions**: 

1. Create a NEW Claude project titled “Master Prompt”   
2. Copy and paste the prompt into a NEW chat in your project   
3. Let Claude guide you through the questions. The more specific and honest you are, the more tailored and effective the guidance will be.  
4. Claude will create an artifact as the output. Select “Copy to Project” to add the artifact to your project knowledge. 

**Time to complete:** 20 \- 30 minutes 

**Tip**: If you prefer to speak your answers instead of writing them, use a voice-to-text tool such as [Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai/). 

---

## Prompt

*Help me create a comprehensive master prompt that I can share with you to get better, more personalized assistance and advice.*

*I'll answer questions across 6 key areas, and then you'll compile my responses into a polished master prompt document.*

*Instructions:*

* *Ask me questions one by one from one section at a time*  
* *Wait for my complete responses before moving to the next section*  
* *Feel free to ask follow-up questions if my answers need clarification*  
* *After I've answered all questions from all sections, create an artifact containing my complete master prompt*

*Let's start with section 1:*

## *1\. Personal & Company Identity*

1. *What's your name and role in the company?*  
2. *What's your company name and industry?*  
3. *How many employees do you have currently?*  
4. *What's your annual revenue range? (Under $1M, $1-5M, $5-25M, $25M+)*

## *2\. Offers & Ideal Customer*

1. *Who is your ideal customer?*   
2. *What's the main problem you solve for customers and what outcome do they achieve?*  
3. *What products or services do you offer? (Include your main revenue drivers and approximate % of total revenue)*  
4. *What new products or services are you planning to offer in the future (if any)?*

## 3\. Current State & Challenges

1. *What are your top 3 business challenges right now?*  
2. *What's the most important decision you're facing in the next 3 months?*  
3. *Where do you feel stuck or uncertain in your business?*

## *4\. Competitive Landscape*

1. *Who are your 2-3 main competitors?*  
2. *What's your key differentiator? (What do you do better than anyone else?)*  
3. *What competitive threats keep you up at night?*

## *5\. Company Culture & Values*

1. *What's your company's mission? (Why do you exist?)*  
2. *What are your core values? (The 3-5 principles that guide behavior)*  
3. *What are your organization's greatest strengths? (Where have you succeeded where others failed?)*  
4. *What are your biggest organizational weaknesses or blind spots?*

## *6\. Team & KPIs*

1. *Who do you report to and what are the main KPIs/metrics that you are responsible for?*   
2. *Who are your key team members and what are their primary responsibilities and KPIs?*
