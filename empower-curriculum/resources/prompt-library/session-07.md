## **The Prompt**

You are the global expert in assessing and improving sales process performance and team effectiveness.

I need to conduct a comprehensive sales diagnostic for my business to identify my biggest opportunities for conversion improvement, process optimization, sales team effectiveness, and owner independence. You already know my business from my Master Prompt.

Guide me through a structured assessment in these phases:

# **Phase 1: Sales Foundation & Current Performance**

Ask me 6 questions, one at a time. For each:

1. Answer it yourself based on what you know from my Master Prompt  
2. Present your answer and ask me to confirm, edit, or replace it  
3. Provide immediate insight about what the answer reveals about sales health and how it compares to industry benchmarks  
4. Wait for my response before moving on  
5. If you discover something new, add it to a list for the Master Prompt amendment in Phase 6

**Questions:**

1. **Sales Team Structure & Capacity**: Who handles lead qualification, demos/discovery calls, proposal development, and closing? Record actual names, roles, and capacity. What's the maximum number of deals per month your team can handle?  
2. **Sales Process Flow**: What are the specific steps in your current sales process from lead to close? Record the actual sequence and typical timeframes for each step. What's your average sales cycle length from first contact to close?  
3. **Conversion Rates by Stage**: What are your conversion rates for:  
   * Lead to qualified opportunity  
   * Qualified opportunity to proposal/demo  
   * Proposal to close  
   * Overall lead-to-close rate  
4. After answering, compare these to industry benchmarks (Lead to qualified: 13-20%, Qualified to proposal: 50-70%, Proposal to close: 20-30%, Overall lead-to-close: 2-5% for most B2B).  
5. **Pipeline Health & Forecasting**: What's your current number of active opportunities, total pipeline value, average deal size, and win rate over the last 12 months? How accurate are your sales forecasts, and what's your forecasting process? After answering, tell me what this reveals about pipeline predictability and sales forecasting accuracy.  
6. **Lead Sources & Qualification**: What are your primary lead sources and what percentage comes from each? How do you qualify leads \- do you have a formal scoring system and clear qualification criteria? What percentage of leads actually qualify? After answering, assess qualification effectiveness.  
7. **Owner Sales Involvement**: Rate your personal involvement (1-5 scale) in lead qualification, discovery calls, demos, proposals, objection handling, closing, and pricing decisions. How many hours per week do you spend on sales, and what percentage of deals are you personally involved in? After answering, tell me what this level of involvement indicates about scalability and delegation opportunities.

# **Phase 2: Sales Process Effectiveness & Documentation**

Ask me 6 questions, one at a time, following the same 5-step structure:

**Questions:**

1. **Sales Process Documentation & Scripts**: Do you have documented sales scripts for opening calls, discovery questions, presentations, closing, and follow-up? Are these consistently used? Rate the quality and effectiveness. Also, how well-documented is your overall sales process \- do you have playbooks, stage definitions, and advancement criteria? After answering, tell me that companies with documented scripts typically see 30% higher conversion rates.  
2. **Discovery & Needs Assessment**: Do you have a structured discovery process with specific questioning frameworks? What key questions do you always ask prospects? How do you uncover budget and identify decision-makers? How systematic is your needs assessment? After answering, tell me how thorough discovery correlates with closing rates.  
3. **Objection Handling & Sales Methodology**: What are your top 3 most common objections, and do you have documented responses? What objection is hardest to handle? Do you have a systematic sales methodology that your team follows? After answering, inform me that top performers typically overcome 60-80% of objections with documented approaches.  
4. **Proposal Development & Pricing**: How long does it take to create proposals \- do you use templates? What percentage of proposals result in sales? Is your pricing systematized with clear guidelines, or is it ad-hoc? After answering, tell me that best-in-class proposal-to-close rates are 40-60%, and proposals taking \>3 days to create indicate process inefficiency.  
5. **CRM Implementation & Pipeline Management**: What CRM do you use, and how consistently is data entered? How do you track pipeline health \- is it automated or manual? What sales reports do you generate and how often? After answering, assess CRM sophistication and data reliability.  
6. **Sales Performance Measurement**: How do you measure individual and team sales performance? What specific metrics do you track, and how often do you review performance? Do you have systematic performance analytics that identify optimization opportunities? After answering, tell me what this reveals about your ability to improve sales effectiveness.

# **Phase 3: Technology, Training & Team Development**

Ask me 5 questions, one at a time, following the same structure:

**Questions:**

1. **Sales Technology Stack & Integration**: Beyond your CRM, what other sales tools do you use (email automation, proposal generation, scheduling, analytics)? How well do they integrate? What percentage of your sales process is manual vs. automated? After answering, tell me what technology gaps might be limiting sales performance.  
2. **Sales Training & Development**: Do you have formal sales training for new team members? How is your sales process taught? What sales skills need the most improvement? Is training ongoing or just onboarding? After answering, inform me that companies with formal sales training see 50% higher net sales per employee.  
3. **Offer Strategy & Performance**: What different offers, packages, or pricing tiers do you have? Which offers perform best and worst? Do you track offer performance systematically or is it informal? After answering, assess offer optimization opportunities.  
4. **Promotions, Guarantees & Risk Reversal**: How often do you run promotions, and what types (discounts, bonuses, limited-time, bundles)? What guarantees or risk reversal do you offer? What's the promotional lift vs. standard pricing, and what's your actual refund rate? After answering, tell me how this impacts close rates and brand positioning.  
5. **Automation & Process Efficiency**: What sales tasks do you wish were automated? What data do you wish you could track better? Where are the biggest manual bottlenecks in your sales process? After answering, tell me which automation opportunities would have the highest ROI impact.

# **Phase 4: Competitive Position & Strategic Context**

Ask me 4 questions, one at a time, following the same structure:

**Questions:**

1. **Competitive Sales Comparison**: How do your main competitors sell, and what's different about their approach? What sales advantages do they have vs. what advantages do you have? How does your pricing compare? After answering, tell me what this reveals about differentiation opportunities.  
2. **Objection Patterns & Value Proposition**: How often do you face price objections specifically? What makes prospects choose you vs. competitors? Is your value proposition clearly articulated in your sales process? After answering, tell me what this indicates about value proposition clarity and pricing power.  
3. **Sales Capacity & Scaling Constraints**: What limits your ability to handle more sales volume \- team capacity, delivery bandwidth, or other constraints? At what point would you need to hire additional salespeople? After answering, tell me what this reveals about scaling readiness.  
4. **Customer Concentration & Revenue Mix**: What percentage of your sales revenue comes from your top 1 and top 3 customers? How dependent are you on repeat business vs. new customer acquisition? What's your customer lifetime value and acquisition cost? After answering, tell me what this indicates about sales diversification and risk.

   * 7 points: Fully integrated with automated workflows and seamless data flow
