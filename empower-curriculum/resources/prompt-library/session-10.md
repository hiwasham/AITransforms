## Prompt 1

*Generate a Level 1 Core Process SOP, including the visual process diagram, for the Product/Service Design process.*

*You are an expert in product development, service design, and innovation management. You understand what separates high-performing product teams from reactive, chaotic development processes. You know how to build scalable product systems that identify market opportunities, design solutions that customers love, and continuously optimize offerings while minimizing owner dependency.*

*I need you to create a comprehensive process map for my Product/Service Design system \- from identifying market opportunities and customer needs through the entire product lifecycle until sunset or major pivots. You already know my business from my Master Prompt.*

*The diagram should begin with market opportunity identification or customer problem discovery, flow through research, design, development, testing, launch, and optimization processes, and end with product lifecycle management decisions (enhancement, pivot, or sunset). Do not include the business strategy processes that create product priorities (that happens before) or the business impact after launch (that happens after) \- this should start when we identify a market opportunity and end when we've made lifecycle decisions about the product.*

*Ask me the following key questions, one at a time, before continuing. Answer each question as you currently understand it, but wait for me to confirm or edit before continuing:*

***What business are we looking at today?** In case I have multiple businesses.*

***What's your primary product development model?** (Internal product team, external agencies, founder-led design, customer co-creation, hybrid approach, etc. \- This determines who is Responsible vs Accountable for research, design, development, and testing)*

***Do you have dedicated product roles** (Product Manager, UX Designer, Developer, Product Marketing), or are product responsibilities shared across other roles? (This determines realistic RACI assignments \- if no product roles, responsibilities get distributed to operations managers, founders, customer success, etc.)*

***What's your current team size and product complexity?** (Single product vs multiple product lines, simple service vs complex platform \- This affects whether the CEO/Owner is directly involved in all product decisions or delegates to product managers)*

***Do you have technical development capabilities in-house or do you rely on external partners?** (This determines if development, testing, and iteration can be done internally or requires external coordination)*

***What's your typical product development timeline and budget range?** (Quick iterations vs major releases, bootstrap vs funded \- This affects the depth of research, testing, and validation processes)*

*The diagram should cover the key product/service design processes like:*

***Market Research & Discovery**: Opportunity identification through customer feedback analysis, competitor gap analysis, market trend monitoring, customer interview systematization, pain point validation, market size estimation, persona development with AI assistance, and jobs-to-be-done framework application.*

***Concept Development & Ideation**: Solution brainstorming with AI collaboration, concept prioritization using customer value scoring, technical feasibility assessment, business model validation, pricing strategy development, go-to-market fit evaluation, and concept documentation with clear success criteria.*

***Design & Prototyping**: User experience design with AI-powered personalization, service blueprint creation, minimum viable product (MVP) definition, prototype development using no-code/low-code tools, user flow optimization, accessibility compliance, and design system establishment.*

***Testing & Validation**: Customer validation through systematic testing, A/B testing framework implementation, user acceptance testing protocols, performance benchmarking, feedback collection automation, iteration planning based on data, and success criteria validation.*

***Launch Preparation & Go-to-Market**: Marketing asset creation with AI assistance, sales enablement and training, customer support documentation, pricing finalization and testing, distribution channel setup, launch timeline orchestration, and success metrics dashboard creation.*

***Post-Launch Optimization**: Performance monitoring and analytics, customer feedback analysis automation, feature usage tracking, conversion optimization, customer success correlation analysis, competitive response monitoring, and continuous improvement prioritization.*

***Lifecycle Management & Iteration**: Product roadmap development based on customer data, feature prioritization using AI insights, enhancement vs pivot decision frameworks, resource allocation optimization, sunset planning for underperforming features, portfolio optimization strategies, and strategic innovation pipeline management.*

*These should be the processes that turn market opportunities into successful products/services that customers love and that drive sustainable business growth throughout their lifecycle.*

*I want to see the basic steps in the process color coded based on the high-level product activities:*

***Market Research & Discovery (Purple)** \- Opportunity identification through customer validation and market analysis*

***Concept Development & Ideation (Blue)** \- Solution design and business model validation*

***Design & Prototyping (Green)** \- User experience design and MVP development*

***Testing & Validation (Orange)** \- Customer testing, feedback collection, and iteration planning*

***Launch Preparation & Go-to-Market (Teal)** \- Marketing, sales enablement, and launch execution*

***Post-Launch Optimization (Red)** \- Performance monitoring, optimization, and continuous improvement*

***Lifecycle Management & Iteration (Gray)** \- Strategic decisions, roadmap planning, and portfolio management*

*Each sub-item should be a separate arrow-connected block. This should be done in HTML/CSS as a visual process flow.  For each item, also show who is Responsible, Accountable, and Consulted based on what you know about my company and the answers to the questions above. Use these guidelines for RACI assignments:*

*Include RACI format: "R: Person | A: Person | C: Person |" and ensure you include the pipe character "|".*
