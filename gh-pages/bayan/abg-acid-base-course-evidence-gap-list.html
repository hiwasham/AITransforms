03 Claims intentionally excluded

What this page will not say

Each of the following is unsupported by the verified source set and must not appear in the published copy.

  • No accreditation, CPD, licensing, or professional-recognition claim.
  • No promise of passing an exam or improving scores.
  • No claim that the course makes learners independently competent to interpret ABGs or manage acid-base disorders.
  • No claim that the course is designed for a specific profession or exam.
  • No claim about self-paced access, videos, live teaching, duration, assessment, grading, prerequisites, or access period.
🚫

These exclusions are not stylistic preferences. Each one would create a factual claim about competence, recognition, or exam outcome that no verified source currently supports.

⛔ Publication gate

Do not publish until the course owner confirms the five gap areas

The current draft is suitable for editorial review and fact confirmation only. Intended learners and post-course outcomes are the critical blockers — without them, the answer page cannot accurately tell a reader who the course is for or what it enables them to do.

  • Certificate Exact title, issuer wording, QR verification, assessment requirement, accreditation status, eligibility conditions.
  • Clinical cases Case topics, whether interactive or graded, confirmation that “10 cases” is current.
  • Intended learners Target roles and training stages, plus prerequisites.
  • Format Self-paced or instructor-led, components, estimated time, access period, assessment and completion criteria.
  • Post-course capability Approved, observable learning outcomes — no substitutes.
📝

Next step: send this list to the course owner. Once the five areas are confirmed, the answer-page draft can be updated and moved to publication review.
