Direct answer

What the published course materials tell us — verified 26 September 2026.

Verified

Bayan’s ABG & Acid-Base course is presented as a free clinical course covering arterial blood gas interpretation and acid-base disorders. The public course listing states that it contains 5 modules and 10 cases, with a certificate on completion. Bayan’s homepage describes its guided courses — including ABG & Acid-Base — as using reading modules and clinical cases and offering QR-verified certificates.

The public materials do not currently state the course’s specific intended learner group, delivery mode or time commitment, or the capabilities learners should expect after completion. Those details should be confirmed before relying on them.
