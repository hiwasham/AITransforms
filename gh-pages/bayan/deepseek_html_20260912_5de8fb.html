Reflect — the real lesson

Look at your own business. Which of these nine is weakest right now? That's almost certainly where your growth is leaking.

The second lesson: they don't work in isolation. A great SEO team can't fix bad pricing. Great ads can't fix a leaky funnel. Great sales can't fix a product nobody keeps. Diagnose before you invest.
