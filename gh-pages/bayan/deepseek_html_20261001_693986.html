Non-negotiable

Guardrails for a medical brand.

Every aggressive tactic runs inside these. Break one and the moat becomes a liability.

01

No pass / outcome guarantee. Ever.

Cap at access- or effort-based refunds.

02

No fake scarcity.

No countdown timers around a licensing career.

03

Never bare "AI-powered."

Always: AI assists with drafting; humans own what reaches learners.

04

AI coach never invents clinical facts.

Cites reviewed content only.

05

Localize prices far below Empower's US anchors.

$9,998 cohort / $999 mo is the shape, not the number. Copy the ladder, not the price.

06

Don't over-paywall.

Let candidates feel the clinical rigor before the wall.

07

Keep brands and claims separate.

MedResearch Academy stays a separate brand. Don't cite SQUH / OMSB / SQU as the founder's employer. Segment by exam path, not "speaks to everyone."
