# Managing Your Master Prompts

Now that you've created your first Master Prompt, here's how to manage and build upon it.

## Your Master Prompts Hub

All your Master Prompts are stored in the **[Master Prompts](/ai/master-prompts)** section, accessible from the sidebar under **AI > Master Prompts**.

From here you can:

- **Create multiple Master Prompts** for different businesses or contexts
- **Edit and refine** your existing prompts as your business evolves
- **Attach Knowledge Documents** to enhance your prompts with additional context
- **Manage versions** to track changes over time

## Knowledge Documents

As you complete exercises throughout this curriculum, any documents generated (like your Organization Resources or the 4-Level Framework) are automatically saved as **Knowledge Documents**.

These files live alongside your Master Prompts and can be attached to any prompt to give your AI even more context about your business.

## Next Steps

1. Visit your **[Master Prompts](/ai/master-prompts)** to review what you've created
2. Continue with the curriculum to add more Knowledge Documents
3. Remember to update your Master Prompt as your business changes
