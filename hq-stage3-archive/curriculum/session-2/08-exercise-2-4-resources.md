# Exercise 2.4: Add Your Resources to Your Master Prompt

## Purpose

To add information about your available resources to your Master Prompt so your AI can take these constraints into consideration when providing advice.

---

## Instructions

1. Click the **"Identify Your Resources"** button below to open the Sessions Coach
2. The AI will guide you through questions about your organization's resources across five categories:
    - **Capital:** Cash, credit lines, investment funds
    - **Physical:** Equipment, facilities, inventory, vehicles
    - **Intellectual:** Patents, copyrights, trade secrets, customer lists
    - **Brand:** Reputation, trust, customer relationships
    - **People:** Key employees with their skills, knowledge, and relationships
3. Answer each question as specifically and honestly as you can - the more detail you provide, the more tailored the guidance will be
4. Once complete, the AI will compile your responses into a Resources document and offer to save it to your account
5. Your Resources document will be saved to **Knowledge Documents** where you can add it to any of your Master Prompts

[Identify Your Resources](#magic-button)

**Time to complete:** 10-15 minutes
