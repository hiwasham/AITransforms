# Exercise 2.5: Working with Your Knowledge Documents

## Purpose

Now that you've created Knowledge Documents through the exercises, here's how to put them to work by adding them to your Master Prompts.

---

## Instructions

1. From the sidebar, navigate to **Administration > [Master Prompts](/ai/master-prompts)**
2. Click on the Master Prompt you want to enhance
3. In the **Knowledge Documents** section you'll see a table listing all available documents with a toggle switch next to each one
4. Use the toggle switches to turn on the documents you want included in this Master Prompt. You can activate as many as you need - each one gives the AI more context about your business
5. Click the **"Regenerate Prompt"** button in the top-right corner to update the Master Prompt with your selected documents
6. Wait a few seconds for the regeneration to finish. Once complete, you'll see your updated Master Prompt content in the preview box at the bottom of the page. From now on, whenever you use this Master Prompt in your chats, all of this information will be automatically included as context for the AI

Each Master Prompt can use a different set of Knowledge Documents. For example, you might have one prompt focused on operations that uses your Customer Lifecycle and RACI documents, and another focused on strategy that uses your Three Layers and Resources documents.
