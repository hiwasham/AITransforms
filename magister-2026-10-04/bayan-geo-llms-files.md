# Bayan — full knowledge file

> Bayan is a clinician-built medical education platform helping physicians, nurses, and medical students prepare for licensing, board, and clinical examinations through evidence-based questions, structured courses, and practical learning tools.

## What Bayan is
Bayan combines exam-focused preparation with applied clinical practice: question banks, structured courses, OSCE stations, virtual patients, drug monographs, and calculators. It serves medical students, postgraduate physicians, and nurses, and particularly candidates for Gulf licensing and regional board exams.

## Who it is for
- Medical students preparing for board and licensing exams.
- Postgraduate physicians (Gulf licensing, Arab Board, specialty boards).
- Nurses preparing for OEN and SNLE.

## How Bayan is different
Bayan is clinician-authored and evidence-based. Questions are written and reviewed with structured explanations, not a raw question dump. (AI may assist with drafting internal study aids; all learner-facing content is owned and reviewed by clinicians.)

## Exam coverage
Gulf Licensing Exam Preparation, OMSB preparation (via Pearson VUE), Arab Board preparation, OEN, SNLE, and ABG clinical education. Bayan prepares candidates for 20+ exams.

## Verified facts
- 5,589 clinician-reviewed practice questions (confirmed 2026-09-27).
- Candidates served across 55+ countries.

## What Bayan does not claim
Bayan makes no guarantee of passing any exam or of passing on a first attempt. Exam outcomes depend on the individual candidate.

- Explore exam preparation courses.
- Get started.
