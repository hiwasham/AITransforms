## Website & Content

**Readiness:** 73/100

Bayan has strong educational depth across 60 discovered pages, but slow mobile loading and broad homepage messaging may weaken sign-ups.

**Top findings**

- The homepage tries to speak to everyone — Physicians, nurses, students, residents, and many exam candidates share one opening message, making the right path harder to spot.

- Mobile pages load slower than they should — The mobile score is 71/100. Redirects add about 780ms, while unused website code adds about 600ms before visitors can fully engage.

- Calls to action are too general — The main action found is “Get started,” which does not tell an ABIM, OEN, USMLE, or Prometric learner what happens next.

- Content review is incomplete — The site has 60 discovered pages, but only 10 were fetched, leaving many exam articles, calculators, and learning pages unchecked.

**Top opportunities**

- Create clear paths for each learner group — Give nursing, medical student, residency, and Gulf licensing visitors dedicated starting points with exam-specific plans and one clear next action.

- Make articles and tools lead to a purchase decision — Link each exam article and clinical calculator to the most relevant sample quiz, course, or pricing page.
