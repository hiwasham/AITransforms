Bayan Growth & Revenue Engine

The full pipeline for a growth engine — mapped onto the EMPOWER Business Process Framework. The goal is more revenue from the existing user base, built inside the framework you already have.

Product: Bayan Learning Prepared: 2026-09-26 Status: design · not authorized Mode: builder / ship-fast

01 Three findings

What the recon of the framework revealed — and why it changes what to build first.

Finding 1 · The revenue is in the empty back half

You built the front of the funnel. The money hides in the processes left at zero cores.

The customer lifecycle is a closed loop: Market → Lead → Sale → Delivery → Success → Lead. The first two are populated. But Sale-to-Delivery (onboarding), Delivery-to-Success (retention), and Success-to-Lead (referral) are all empty.

For a SaaS with thousands of free users and near-zero paid conversion, the money is not in more lead-gen — it's in activation → trial → paid → retention. That IS the two processes you left at zero cores. Build those first.

Finding 2 · Wrong motion for the flagship

Lead-to-Sale is a B2B agency motion. The flagship revenue is B2C self-serve.

The four cores in Lead-to-Sale — CRM enrichment, proposals, MQL→SQL routing, bulk cold outreach — are how you sell a service to an institution with a salesperson. But Bayan Learning sells subscriptions to individual candidates who self-serve inside the product. Nobody sends them a proposal.

These cores aren't wrong — they just belong to a second, institutional motion. Split the engine into two lanes.

Finding 3 · Nothing is Bayan yet

The strategy cores are blank templates. You already have the content to fill them.

All 8 strategy cores are generic seed templates. But research.md already contains the ICP, segmentation, competitors, positioning, pricing, and the founder-credibility spine.

The improvement isn't to invent content — it's to pour the research you already have into the scaffolds and delete the agency defaults.

04 The measurement gate

Do this before trusting any KPI. Every core's target is un-instrumented today.

⚠
Prerequisite · build first

Funnel instrumentation — before any KPI target

Bayan has zero funnel data — no MRR, installs, trials, conversion, or retention. So the first Enabling core to build is funnel instrumentation: analytics + attribution + a baseline snapshot.

Until it exists, every KPI target is marked [TBD — funnel data]. This gates any revenue projection.

05 Where the revenue comes from

Priority order — especially focused on more revenue from what already exists.

Instrument the funnel (proc 22).
You can't grow what you can't see. Baseline snapshot first.
Build Sale-to-Delivery + Delivery-to-Success (proc 15, 16).
Activation + trial → paid + retention. Converting the existing free base is the fastest MRR with zero new ad spend.
Founder's Offer to a slice of the free base.
Generates the first real conversion + price-sensitivity data.
Institutional pilots (Motion B, re-scoped proc 14).
Bigger deals — this is where the CEO's 20% commission pays out and where the raise ladder gets earned.
