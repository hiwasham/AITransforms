02 What B2B is measured in · NOT seat cash

⚠
Do not score B2B on seat fees

From bayan-b2b-sizing-memo.md: seat revenue is small — SOM ~$5–86k/yr ARR, ~$0 year-1 cash because trials are free. So the seat fee is not the metric.

Score B2B on these four things instead — in order of near-term value:

Metric 01
Logos
Named Gulf institutions on signed pilots. Value is credibility — a logo that lowers B2C CAC by lending social proof.
Metric 02
Trial-seat → B2C conversions
Free institutional users who convert to paid individual subs. Tracked on the shared funnel.
Metric 03
One lighthouse contract
A single named institution, ideally a negotiated site / CME deal. This is what turns a warm-book motion into a reference.
Metric 04
The verdict-flipping number
Average negotiated contract value per institution (sizing memo unknown #1). Go get it from move #1 below — one real number flips the whole B2B verdict.

03 First moves · warm book only, no cold prospecting

✕
The CS04 lesson · Acquira B2B outreach
Cold outreach replied at 0.2%.
It took 3 months of heavy enrichment — owner personal contact via Clay / Wiza waterfalls — to reach 1.5% — a 10× lift. Cold B2B is brutal. For a 10 hr/week track, cold prospecting is the wrong motion.

So the motion is the warm book — institutions already on free 1-year trials + the Oman / MedResearch Academy relationships.

#1
Pull the list of institutions already on free trials (sizing unknown #6) + warm Oman relationships.
One list: institution, seats, trial start, warmth.
#2
Run a renewal / expansion conversation on the 2–3 warmest.
For each: a contract-value data point (unknown #1) OR a renewal commitment.
#3
Land one lighthouse — a named Gulf institution willing to be referenced.
One signed pilot / contract + permission to name them.
No cold list-building, no paid tools, no outreach spend in this phase.
If a warm talk surfaces a real contract-value number in the tens of thousands, that flips the whole B2B verdict — escalate to Hiwa / CEO immediately.
