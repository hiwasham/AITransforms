**Status:** Internal operating doc for Hiwa + Nasim — **NOT a CEO deliverable.** Authorizes no spend, no outreach, no entitlement change. Built on `bayan-b2b-sizing-memo.md` (the sizing) and the EMPOWER Lead-to-Sale method (Session 7).
**Prepared:** 2026-10-02 · **Horizon:** runs in parallel with the B2C conversion trial, B2C-weighted.
**Rule:** B2C conversion is CEO priority #1; B2B runs alongside as a lighter warm-book track. This is "both, B2C-weighted," not "B2B above B2C."

---

## 1. The frame: one system, two owners

B2C and B2B are the **two halves of the same EMPOWER Lead-to-Sale stage** — the 7.1 assessment is a single diagnostic covering *conversion + process + sales-team + owner-independence*. So this is not two builds; it is **one instrumented funnel, two people running the two halves.**

| Half | Owner | What they run | Measured in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversion | Hiwa | B2C funnel: segmentation, instrumentation, paywall, trial→paid SOP | trial→paid rate, MRR |
| Relationships | Nasim | B2B warm-book: renewal/expansion talks, one lighthouse contract | logos, trial→B2C conversions, 1 contract |
| **Shared instrument** | both | ONE funnel; free institutional trial-seats are tracked as a B2C acquisition channel | trial-seat → B2C conversion % |

**Why one funnel:** a pilot's free seats are only worth the effort if we can prove what they convert to. Instrument the free seats as a bulk B2C channel (per the sizing memo §5) and the same PostHog layer measures both motions.

---

## 2. What B2B is measured in (NOT seat cash)

From `bayan-b2b-sizing-memo.md`: seat revenue is small (SOM ~$5–86k/yr ARR, ~$0 year-1 cash because trials are free). So **do not score B2B on seat fees.** Score it on:

1. **Logos** — named Gulf institutions on signed pilots (credibility / B2C CAC-lowering).
2. **Trial-seat → B2C conversions** — free institutional users who convert to paid individual subs.
3. **One lighthouse contract** — a single named institution, ideally a negotiated site/CME deal.
4. **The verdict-flipping number** — *average negotiated contract value per institution* (sizing memo unknown #1). Go get it from move #1 below.

## 3. Nasim's first moves — warm book only, do NOT prospect cold

**The CS04 lesson (Acquira B2B outreach):** cold outreach replied at **0.2%**; it took 3 months of heavy enrichment (owner personal contact via Clay/Wiza waterfalls) to reach **1.5% — a 10× lift**. Cold B2B is brutal. For a 10 hr/week track, cold prospecting is the wrong motion.

**So:** work the warm book — institutions **already on free 1-year trials** + the Oman / MedResearch Academy relationships.

| # | Move | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull the list of institutions already on free trials (sizing unknown #6) + warm Oman relationships | One list: institution, seats, trial start, warmth |
| 2 | Run a renewal/expansion conversation on the 2–3 warmest | For each: a contract-value data point (unknown #1) OR a renewal commitment |
| 3 | Land **one lighthouse** — a named Gulf institution willing to be referenced | One signed pilot/contract + permission to name them |

No cold list-building, no paid tools, no outreach spend in this phase. If a warm talk surfaces a real contract-value number in the tens of thousands, that flips the whole B2B verdict — escalate to Hiwa/CEO immediately.

## 4. The Lead-to-Sale SOP (EMPOWER 7.2) — Nasim's operating doc

Don't run the warm-book motion from memory. Document it once as a repeatable SOP so it improves every cycle and survives Nasim stepping away (the owner-independence half of the 7.1 assessment).

**Level 1 — process map (the steps):**
`warm list pulled → warmth-ranked → renewal/expansion call booked → tailored one-pager sent → contract-value captured → signed OR nurtured`

**Level 2 — blueprint per step (what "good" looks like):**

| Step | Input | Owner action | Output / tracked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank warmth | trial list + relationships | score by usage + relationship | ranked shortlist |
| Book the call | shortlist | relationship-first ask (CEO's style: "friendship first, then proposal") | call booked |
| Tailored one-pager | call notes | per-institution proposal (not a generic deck) | proposal sent |
| Capture contract value | proposal reply | log the real number (unknown #1) | data point recorded |
| Close or nurture | reply | sign, or add to a light nurture | logo OR next-touch date |

This SOP is the internal tool, not a CEO deliverable. It is also the thing that turns one lucky deal into a repeatable motion.
