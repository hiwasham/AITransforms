# Roadmap — Bayan Product Brain

From the 1-week pilot to a standing AI system for Bayan. Draft-first throughout:
every phase reads Bayan's own approved facts and changes nothing without sign-off.
Each phase earns the next; nothing past Phase 1 is committed.

**Framing lock:** the win is measured in *time saved* and *drift eliminated*, not
revenue lift — Bayan is pre-revenue with no instrumented funnel, so any conversion
claim would be unbacked. Keep the value story on the CEO's stated pain: his time.

---

## Phase 1 — The Pilot (now · ~1 week · $500 one-time)

The wedge. Three pieces:

1. **Product Brain** — one structured, versioned, CEO-approved record per product:
   figures, pricing, positioning, taglines, brand entity, live / in-development status.
2. **Ask-the-brain** — plain-language query, EN + AR: "what's our official question
   count?" → the one approved answer.
3. **Drift check** — automated pass over the live corporate site, product sites, and
   app listings; produces a drift report listing every surface that disagrees with the
   approved brain.

**Maps to AITransforms services:** Business Brain Mapping + RAG & Knowledge Architecture.
**Deliverable proof:** the first drift report, on day one.
**CEO time cost:** ~2 calls + one approval pass + one weekly written report.

---

## Phase 2 — Drift-guard retainer (after the win · $200/mo optional)

Operationalize Phase 1. The brain stays current; a scheduled drift report runs before
each exhibition, launch, or brochure. The mismatch gets caught before buyers see it —
never discovered at the booth again.

**Maps to:** the standing-system half of Productization. Lowest-touch continuation,
calibrated to the CEO's stated cadence (~2 calls/week, a few messages).

---

## Phase 3 — AI CEO Assistant

The brain grows a voice. Beyond answering "what's official?", it *drafts* consistent
copy on demand from the approved facts — a product blurb, an app-store description, a
one-line answer for a partner — always sourced from the canonical record.

**Maps to:** Local AI Agents & AI CEO Assistant (AITransforms service 4).
**Why it's later:** it needs the approved brain underneath it. Phase 1 builds that floor.
*(Scope note: this is the Bayan-engagement assistant, distinct from the "do-not-build"
CEO-assistant demo guard on the AITransforms marketing website — different thing.)*

---

## Phase 4 — Facts-feed integration

Close the loop at the source. The approved brain auto-populates marketing copy, app-store
listings, and brochures, so surfaces are born consistent instead of being reconciled after
the fact. This directly hands Nasim's marketing work the approved reference — the brain
stops being a checker and becomes the supply.

**Maps to:** Productization, full loop (detect → prevent).
**Lane note:** this *empowers* the marketing lane; it does not replace it.

---

## Deferred — Approach B (revisit only after the pilot wins)

Explicitly **out** of the near roadmap. Parked on purpose:

- **AI study-tutor / RAG over the 5,589-question bank** — learner-facing, differentiates
  vs UWorld. Deferred because it overlaps Bayan's own engineering, takes weeks not days,
  and doesn't touch the CEO's time-pain. This is a big build, not a wedge.
- **Clinical-ML resource backlog** (AI-for-Medicine material, medical-QA repos) — belongs
  to Approach B, not the source-of-truth wedge.

Pulling any of this into the near phases would dilute the wedge and drag toward the big
build we chose not to lead with.

---

## Bayan-side, NOT AITransforms-owned (kept separate to respect lanes)

These are Bayan product/marketing decisions the brain could *feed*, but they belong to the
CEO + the marketing lane, not to this engagement. Listed for context only:

- **Phase 0 for Bayan:** instrument install/usage events, and *lock one question count*
  across every surface — which is literally the drift problem the Product Brain solves.
- **Two CEO-gated decisions parked** (grandfathering sunset; a mid-tier single-exam-pass
  SKU) — both need live data pulls this engagement doesn't touch.

---
