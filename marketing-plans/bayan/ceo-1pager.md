*Prepared for Dr. Abdullah Al-Alawi · from Hiwa (AITransforms) & Nasim*

## The problem, in one line

The same Bayan fact shows up differently across your sites, apps, and brochures. Your
headline question count alone appears as several different numbers, and the brand entity
reads two different ways on your two main sites. The content is strong — there's just no
single approved place that says *"this is the official version,"* so it drifts, and
reconciling it keeps costing you time.

## The first win (Week 1)

**One approved source of truth for Bayan.** A single record of every product's numbers,
pricing, positioning, and brand — that anyone can ask in plain language (English or
Arabic), plus an automatic check that flags any live page that disagrees with it.

- You approve the official numbers once. After that, no one has to ask you again.
- It reads your public pages only and changes nothing without your sign-off.
- Deliverable at the end of the week: the approved brain, a working "ask it anything"
  demo, and a first report showing exactly where your surfaces contradict each other today.
- Your time cost: about two short calls and one approval. That's it.

## Where it goes (only after the first win earns it)

The Week-1 win is stage one of a **Bayan AI brain** that grows in small, low-risk steps:

1. **Source of truth** (Week 1) — the approved facts + the drift check.
2. **CEO assistant** — ask "what's our official X?" and get consistent draft text on demand,
   always from the approved record.
3. **Feeds your surfaces** — the approved facts flow into marketing, app listings, and
   brochures, so they stay consistent by default. This hands Nasim's marketing an approved
   reference to work from.

Each stage is small and proves itself before the next. Nothing big to commit to up front.
