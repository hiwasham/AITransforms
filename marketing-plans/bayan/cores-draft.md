## 215 — ICP & Persona
**Primary ICP:** physicians, residents, medical students, and nurses preparing for
Gulf/international licensing + board exams. **NOT the general public** (CEO + Nasim explicit).

**Persona A — "The IMG self-funder" (flagship revenue, Motion A):** a doctor/nurse
targeting a Gulf Prometric or licensing exam (SCFHS, DHA, OMSB, QCHP, NHRA), paying out of
pocket, exam-date-driven, anxious about *what* to study, comfortable studying in English.
Core job-to-be-done (VOC): *"I stopped guessing what to study."*

**Persona B — "The institutional buyer" (Motion B):** a hospital / university / training-board
L&D lead who licenses seats for cohorts of residents or nurses. Relationship-led sale.

**Segment by exam track:** Doctors → OMSB / DHA / MOH / SCFHS / QCHP / NHRA; Residents →
Arab Board / OMSB / MRCP / ABIM; Nurses → OEN / SNLE / DHA / Prometric / NCLEX-RN.
