Executive Summary

The finding

Comparable tech conferences — Web Summit, TechCrunch Disrupt, CES — demonstrate that well-run multi-channel campaigns scale from hundreds to tens of thousands of attendees.

A documented case study (a nonprofit virtual conference) achieved a 40% attendee increase using the same channel mix recommended here.

The method

Conservative, sourced conversion benchmarks were applied to each channel. Projected engagement across the five channels totals 560 estimated attendees — a 12% buffer above the 500-person target.

Every figure below is traceable to a named source; no channel is projected on reach alone.

On the margin: the 12% buffer relies on benchmark averages, not guarantees. Recommendation 7 defines a contingency lever to activate if any channel underperforms its benchmark by more than ~20%.

Recommendations

7 actions
01

Prioritize email as the highest-yield channel

It's projected to deliver the largest single share of registrations (180). Build the segmented list now — past attendees, industry contacts, partner lists — and start the save-the-date → registration-open → speaker spotlight → early-bird → last-chance sequence at least 90 days out, at a cadence of 5–8 emails/month per Bloomreach's ROI benchmark.

180 attendees90 days out5–8 emails/mo
02

Lock the branded hashtag early and verify uniqueness

Check across LinkedIn, Twitter/X and Instagram before any public campaign launch, then run the 90-day phased content plan: foundation → amplification → urgency → 48-hour blitz → post-event follow-up.

Pre-launch3 platforms5 phases
03

Treat community outreach as a distinct workstream, not an afterthought

Assign someone to directly post and cross-promote in the 5 identified community platforms — TechMeetups, Meetup.com groups, DevITJobs, TechMeetups.io, and the GitHub Awesome-Tech-Communities Slack/Discord groups — rather than relying on passive discovery.

120 attendeesNamed owner5 platforms
04

List the event on all three confirmed platforms immediately

Eventbrite, Meetup and Luma. This is a low-effort, low-cost channel — Luma is free — that compounds with SEO and organic search over time.

70 attendeesLow costCompounding
05

Secure 5–10 micro-influencer/speaker partners early

Target the 5,000–50,000 follower range and provide pre-made shareable assets rather than relying on organic goodwill. This channel has the lowest projected volume (40) but the highest credibility and trust value per registration.

40 attendeesHighest trust5–10 partners
06

Instrument tracking now

Install website retargeting pixels, use unique UTM parameters per channel, and track the stated KPI benchmarks weekly — email open/CTR/conversion; social registration conversion 3–5%, engagement 4–6%, share rate 2–3%; hashtag reach — so underperforming channels can be reallocated budget and effort before the final 30-day urgency window.

UTM per channelRetargeting pixelsWeekly review
07

Build in a buffer plan

Since the 560 projection has only a 12% margin over the 500 target and relies on benchmark averages (not guarantees), have a contingency lever ready — e.g. a paid social/search ad budget increase, or an extended early-bird deadline — to activate if mid-campaign tracking shows any channel underperforming its benchmark by more than ~20%.
