01 HIGH Your CSP silently blocks every GA4 and Google Ads conversion event ▾
What happens

The site sends analytics and conversion beacons to Google's real endpoints, but the connect-src directive doesn't allow them. The browser refuses every request. Confirmed on both the public landing page and inside the authenticated app.

Endpoints being blocked
region1.analytics.google.com/g/collectGA4 page_view, scroll, form_start
region1.analytics.google.com/measurement/conversionGoogle Ads conversion
stats.g.doubleclick.net/g/collectGA4 signals / remarketing
ad.doubleclick.net/ccm/s/collectAds conversion linker (AW-529849606)
Business impact. GA4 and Google Ads receive nothing from the web app. You're paying for clicks and optimizing bids against empty conversion data. GA property G-YNPH01TKYD, Ads account AW-529849606.
The fix · one line of config
connect-src 'self' https://*.supabase.co https://api.openai.com
  https://*.analytics.google.com https://*.google-analytics.com
  https://*.g.doubleclick.net https://*.doubleclick.net
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov https://static.cloudflareinsights.com

Verify in GA4 Realtime that page_view and conversion events arrive. Est. 30 min including deploy + verify.

02 HIGH Two authenticated queries return HTTP 400 on primary screens ▾
2a · Dashboard quiz history (quiz_sessions)

The learner sees "Recent Sessions — No sessions yet" and "0 Questions Today" — while the same dashboard's aggregate panel shows "10 Total Questions, 40% Accuracy." Their own history is invisible because the query dies.

2b · OSCE stations (osce_stations)

Two problems: the query 400s, and it filters education_level=eq.undergraduate for a postgraduate account — wrong scope. The page falls back to partial content instead of the real station list.

Likely root cause (hypothesis — not confirmed). PostgREST 400s on explicit column lists usually mean the client is selecting columns the schema no longer has. Candidates: session_type / exam_mode on quiz_sessions; marking_criteria_count / scenario_preview on osce_stations. Your team can confirm instantly from Supabase logs.
Business impact

Learners see a dashboard that says they've done nothing → demotivating, looks broken, undercuts the retention/streak mechanic that drives subscription renewals.

The fix

Align the client select lists with the live schema (or add the missing columns), and pass the correct education_level for the user's track. Est. 1–3 hours depending on cause.

03 MED Nursing data bleeds into an Internal-Medicine board-prep profile ▾
Evidence · By Specialty breakdown
Critical Care 50% Neurology 0% Nephrology 0% Adult Health 100% Mental Health 100% Principles of Care 100% Internal Medicine 0%

"Adult Health", "Mental Health" and "Principles of Care" are NCLEX/nursing categories — they shouldn't appear inside an IM board-prep dashboard. Historical answers from one track are being counted under another track's taxonomy.

Also: those 100% figures come from a single question each (tiny sample) and are shown with no sample-size context — the learner sees "100%" next to "40% overall" with no way to reconcile them.
The fix

Scope specialty stats to the active program's taxonomy, and suppress or annotate percentages below a minimum n (e.g. show "1/1" instead of "100%").
