02 The diagnostic

Captured verbatim. The app leads with a real clinical case — the first screen a new learner sees.

bayan.edu.om/onboarding
Welcome to Bayan
"Let's see what you know"
Question 1
1 / 5
A 58-year-old man presents to the emergency department with sudden onset crushing chest pain radiating to his left arm for the past 30 minutes. He is diaphoretic and anxious. His BP is 150/90 mmHg, HR 100 bpm. ECG shows ST elevation in leads II, III, and aVF.
Which of the following is the most appropriate initial management?
A Oral metoprolol
B Aspirin 325 mg + activate catheterization lab
C Thrombolytic therapy
D Echocardiography
✓
Guideline answer

Option B — Aspirin 325 mg + primary PCI activation. This is the guideline-correct answer for an inferior STEMI presentation with ST elevation in leads II, III, and aVF. The app grades the diagnostic to seed the learner's starting level.

04 Marketing takeaways

Four concrete reads for the marketing work — the onboarding flow is a strong hook.

01
Diagnostic-first onboarding is a strong hook

The app leads with a real clinical case, not a signup wall. "Let's see what you know" positions Bayan as an assessment engine from second one — the EMPOWER Lead → Activate step done right.

02
The starting level is data-driven

The diagnostic seeds the learner's level — reinforcing the adaptive-engine story covered in docs 07 and 08. This is a concrete product feature, not a marketing claim.

03
Low-friction entry

No long form before value. The case + instant answer-check is the first dopamine hit. Usable as an onboarding proof point in any pitch about time-to-value.

04
Clinician-grade content from the first screen

The opening diagnostic is guideline-correct STEMI management — consistent with the human-reviewed-content brand rule. The brand promise is demonstrated before it is claimed.
