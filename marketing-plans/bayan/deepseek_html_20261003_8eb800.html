01 Brief 1 · Grandfathering sunset

01
The decision

What to do with free / comped accounts created before the paywall.

Accounts created before the paywall (pre-2026-05-01) that still have free access today. Two costs: a revenue leak (engaged lifers seated for free) and a poisoned Phase 0 baseline (free lifers look like active users but can never convert).

Why now

Every day these accounts stay unsegmented, the trial→paid and activation rates read artificially low. Segmenting them out is a Phase 0 requirement; the sunset decision is what makes the segmentation meaningful.

★
Recommendation · C, then revisit

Bayan is pre-traction — the handful of engaged early users are worth more as advocates and testimonials than as reclaimed seats.

Reclaim only dormant lifers now (near-zero goodwill cost), and hold the engaged-lifer decision until Phase 0 shows what a converted user is actually worth. If the engaged-lifer count is trivially small, C ≈ A and you skip the risk entirely.

Guardrails
  • No migration without sign-off. Pull + propose only.
  • Announce before any change. Never touch an account unannounced.
  • Frame as access / effort tiers, never punitive.
  • Honor the medical-brand trust bar — no outcome guarantees, no pressure.
Reversibility
Partially reversible — message matters more than mechanism

Re-comping an account is trivial. But a botched public announcement damages trust once and doesn't un-send. So the message matters more than the mechanism — draft it before touching a single account.

02 Brief 2 · Mid-tier price

02
The decision

Price and shape of the single-exam / exam-window pass SKU.

A one-time single-exam pass — tier = single_exam already exists in the analytics spec. It captures the IMG who won't commit to a recurring sub but will pay once for their exam run-up. It's the Phase 1 packaging gap: today the ladder jumps straight from free to recurring with nothing for the commitment-averse.

Why now

A lower-friction first purchase that later upsells into the sub or the cohort. Fill the packaging gap before paid acquisition begins so that the first paid push lands on a complete ladder.

# Option Upside Risk
A Aggressive entry (low price) Max volume, easy yes Cannibalizes the recurring sub
B Anchored mid (~2–3× the monthly sub) Recommended Reads as "commit to your exam," protects the sub Fewer buyers if mispriced
C Two SKUs (single-exam vs all-access window) More choice More complexity, splits the message
★
Recommendation · B, single SKU

Price at roughly 2.5× the monthly sub, window = next sitting (~60–90 days), localized down for price-sensitive IMGs.

One SKU keeps the message clean (guardrail: simplicity). Split into C only if Phase 1 data shows demand for both scopes. Price is a PayPal config change, so treat the first number as a starting point to A/B, not a commitment.

Guardrails
  • No fake scarcity / countdown around the exam date (guardrail #2).
  • Price well below the Empower US anchors (guardrail #5).
  • One SKU to start. Don't split the message on day one.
Reversibility
Fully reversible — price and window are PayPal config

Price and window are PayPal config; adjust or A/B at any time. This is the low-risk decision of the two — the constraint is picking a defensible first number, not a permanent one.
