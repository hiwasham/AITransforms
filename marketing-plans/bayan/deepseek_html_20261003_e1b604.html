Training Levels, Settings & Personalization

Captured live 2026-09-26 from the nurse test account's in-app Settings, Overview, History, and Achievements screens. The account's Training Level is a single field that drives the whole experience — exam scope, question pool, and which dashboard route the app lands on.

Captured: 2026-09-26 · read-only Track: restored to intern after capture All 12 levels: live-confirmed by switching the field

Two notable live findings

Pharmacist and Family Medicine Resident both resolve to the postgrad dashboard — not a dedicated pharmacy or family-medicine screen.

⚠
Per-track Target-Exam sets still write-gated

Switching training_level does not auto-populate a default exam_targets. That value is set per-user during onboarding and is not surfaced by a track switch.

So the exam scope per track is derivable from doc 08's catalog by profession / education level, but the exact default selection remains [TBD — confirm with team]. The nurse-track Target Exams above are the only set captured directly from the UI.

04 Settings · personalization

The personalization controls available to the learner.

Setting 01
Lab Unit Preference

Three options: SI Units, US-Conventional, or Both. Affects how lab values display throughout the app.

Setting 02
Review Preferences

16 selectable subspecialties filter the screening queue. Unchecked = see everything.

16
Subspecialties available for filtering

Cardiovascular · Pulmonary · Gastroenterology · Nephrology · Endocrinology · Hematology · Infectious Disease · Rheumatology · Neurology · Oncology · Critical Care / ICU · Perioperative Medicine · Geriatric Medicine · Research & Statistics · General Medicine · Ethics & Professionalism.

08 Marketing takeaways

Four reads for the marketing work.

01
Multi-exam Gulf coverage is a concrete differentiator

Five nursing licensure exams across five Gulf countries — matches the approved "Gulf Licensing Exam Preparation" positioning. Usable as a proof point, not a claim.

02
Adaptive engine + subspecialty filtering

The 16 subspecialty filters + adaptive quiz type = a real personalization story. Concrete, demonstrable, and visible from Settings.

03
Milestone / certificate ladder

A gamified retention mechanic — 100 → 5,000 questions. Maps to the EMPOWER Retention stage; usable as a product feature in lifecycle messaging.

04
Physician-track exam sets are still a gap

Only the nurse-track Target Exams were captured. The rest are derivable from doc 08's catalog but the exact per-track defaults remain [TBD — confirm per track].
