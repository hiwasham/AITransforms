## 1. The one-paragraph version

Bayan is already strong where people know it: branded and nursing-exam content scores 55–59 in AI answers. It is invisible where new buyers actually are: un-branded category questions ("MRCP", "SMLE vs OMSB", "ABG", "Gulf licensing") score 4–7. Every one of those zero scores is a page we have not published yet. The plan is to publish the direct answer pages, ship the six GEO fixes that lift AI + Google + Website at once, and own an uncontested long-tail keyword cluster — all with **zero paid spend and zero added headcount**, using the existing content depth (5,589 clinician-reviewed questions, 55+ countries). Expected health lift over 90 days: **25 → ~54**.

> **People who already know Bayan find it. New category buyers don't.**

| Prompt type | Example | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| Branded / nursing | OEN prep, OMSB nursing | 55–59 |
