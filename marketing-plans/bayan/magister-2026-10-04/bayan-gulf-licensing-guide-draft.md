## Introduction

If you are preparing for a medical licensing exam in the Gulf, the first thing to understand is that "the Gulf exam" is not one exam. It is a family of assessments run by different authorities, delivered through different channels, with different formats. Confusing them is the most common preparation mistake. This guide walks through each pathway and how to study for it.

## 1. OMSB (Oman Medical Specialty Board)

OMSB is Oman's specialty board. It is delivered through **Pearson VUE** test centres. Candidates are postgraduate physicians seeking specialist recognition. Preparation emphasizes specialty-specific knowledge and clinical reasoning.

*Study implication:* treat OMSB as a specialty exam, not a general recall test — practise applications, not just facts.

## 2. Prometric-delivered exams

**Prometric is a test-delivery company, not an exam.** Several Gulf and international licensing exams are delivered through Prometric centres. When people say "the Prometric exam," they usually mean a specific licensing exam being delivered via Prometric.

*Study implication:* identify your actual exam syllabus first; the delivery vendor tells you where you sit, not what you study.

## 3. Arab Board

The Arab Board of Health Specializations runs regional specialty exams for postgraduate physicians across member countries. It is recognized across the region and emphasizes specialist-level depth.

*Study implication:* align your study to the specialty, and use question banks that mirror the real format.

## 4. OEN (Oman Nurse) and SNLE (Saudi Nurse Licensure Exam)

Nursing licensure exams (OEN in Oman, SNLE in Saudi Arabia) assess registered-nurse competence. Bayan serves nursing candidates alongside physicians and students.

*Study implication:* nursing exams reward breadth and speed — timed question practice matters.

## 5. How to prepare (the method that works)

1. **Identify your exam** — authority, format, domain.
2. **Use structured questions** — clinician-reviewed items with explanations, not a raw dump.
3. **Practise timed** — build speed for the real sitting.
4. **Review by topic** — fix weak domains systematically.
