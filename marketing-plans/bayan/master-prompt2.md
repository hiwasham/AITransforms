Based on the Master Prompt we just created, here is a strategic analysis of Bayan’s current position and the path forward:

### 1. The "Beachhead" Strategy is High-Potency
Your identification of **internationally-trained doctors (IMGs)** as the ideal customer is a brilliant "beachhead" strategy. 
*   **Why it works:** You aren't just selling "education"; you are selling a **visa and a career**. Because these users are self-funding and under a hard deadline, their "willingness to pay" is significantly higher than a general medical student. 
*   **The Challenge:** You are currently pre-revenue. The transition from 6,000 free members to paid users is the "Valley of Death" for many SaaS startups. Your marketing needs to shift from "utility" (we have questions) to "outcome" (we guarantee you pass).

### 2. The "Quality vs. Scale" Tension
You have a "Seven-Gate" quality process, which is a major strength. However, your **Weakness/Blind Spot** section notes that you cover 20+ exam tracks.
*   **Analysis:** Spreading a small core team across 20 tracks while maintaining a 7-gate review process is mathematically difficult. There is a risk that by trying to be everything to everyone (Doctors, Nurses, Students across 5 products), you might dilute the quality that makes you better than the regional competitors.
*   **Recommendation:** For the next 3 months, consider "tiering" your exam tracks. Apply the full 7-gate rigor to the top 3 revenue-driving tracks and use a leaner (but still safe) process for the others until revenue scales.

### 3. Trust as the Primary Currency
Your competitors (UWorld, AMBOSS) have decades of brand equity. As an Omani startup, your biggest hurdle isn't your technology; it's **trust**.
*   **Analysis:** Your differentiator—clinician-led review in an AI-saturated market—is your strongest weapon. 
*   **Marketing Angle:** You should lean heavily into the "Human Accountability" value. While competitors might use AI to churn out questions, Bayan should market itself as the "Physician-Verified" choice.

### 4. The Data Gap
Your uncertainty regarding **pricing and acquisition channels** is typical for this stage, but it's your most urgent "Key Decision."
*   **Analysis:** With 6,000 free members, you have a goldmine of data. You don't need to guess what they will pay for. 
*   **Strategic Move:** Run a "Founder's Offer" or a limited-time "Beta Transition" discount to a segment of those 6,000 users. This will provide the "measurable growth engine" data you currently lack (conversion rates, price sensitivity).

### Summary for Nas (Marketer)
Your role is critical right now. Since the CEO is focused on clinical direction and strategy, your priority should be **proving the commercial model**. 

**Focus for the next 90 days:**
1.  **Test the Price:** Launch a paid offer to the international IMG segment.
2.  **Gather Social Proof:** Collect testimonials from those who passed their exams using Bayan to combat the "new brand" trust issue.
3.  **Narrow the Funnel:** Don't market all 20 tracks; pick the one with the highest "desperate pain" (likely the SCFHS or OMSB licensing) and win there first.
