## 3. Monthly goal projection model (fillable the day Insights arrive)

Nasim correctly refused to invent numbers before seeing analytics. This gives the CEO a **model** now,
with ranges anchored to the ~775 baseline, and the **formula** so exact targets drop in instantly.

### The funnel (this is the real KPI, not followers)

```
Reach ──► Engaged (saves+shares) ──► Profile visits ──► Bio-link taps ──► Sample-quiz starts ──► Sign-ups
```

The CEO's concern is **conversion**, so the north-star is **sample-quiz starts** from Instagram, not
follower count. Followers are a lagging reach proxy.

### Projection ranges (test volume, not promises)

| Metric | Baseline (pull from Insights) | Month 1 — Foundation | Month 2 — Adjust | Month 3 — Convert |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Followers | ~775 | +5–10% (growth is *not* the goal yet) | +10–20% | +15–25% |
| Avg accounts reached / post | `____` | establish baseline | +25–50% vs M1 | hold reach, lift intent |
| Saves + shares / reach | `____` | measure per format | beat M1 median | beat M2 median |
| Profile visits / week | `____` | baseline | +30% | +30% |
| Bio-link taps / week | `____` | add tracked link | 2× M1 | 2× M2 |
| **Sample-quiz starts / month (from IG)** | `0 tracked today` | **install tracking + first signal** | **first real cohort** | **the number that matters** |
| Attributable sign-ups | `0 tracked` | n/a (no path yet) | first few | the Month-3 goal |

**Formula to set exact targets (fill after baseline):**
- Target reach/post = baseline median × (1 + lift). Start lift at **+25%** M1→M2 (realistic for a page posting 3–4×/week with fixed templates).
- Target quiz-starts = profile-visits × bio-tap-rate × quiz-start-rate. Measure each rate in M1; set M2–M3 as the achievable product of improving each by one step.

### What to tell the CEO about numbers
> "We will not promise a follower number. In Month 1 we install the baseline and the tracked path
> from post → sample quiz. By end of Month 1 you'll have **real rates**, and Month 2–3 targets become
> arithmetic, not hope. Success in Month 3 is **sample-quiz starts and sign-ups traced to Instagram** —
> the conversion you asked about — not likes."

## 4. Content system (enriches the pillars)

Keeps Nasim's Month-1 mix: **~60% fun/relatable · ~25% exam education · ~15% product.**
Keeps **Bissan** (wise — explains *why* the correct answer is correct) and **Ghassan** (funny — makes
the candidate's mistake). Adds the production templates below.

### 4.1 Character bible (so any designer stays on-model)

| | Ghassan | Bissan |
|---|---|---|
| Role | The candidate who guesses | The one who knows |
| Beat | Picks the tempting wrong option, confidently | Explains why it's wrong, why the right one is right |
| Tone | Funny, relatable, a little chaotic | Calm, warm, authoritative |
| Catch-structure | "I was SO sure it was B..." | "Here's the one line that makes it A." |
| Never | mocks the learner; is mean | lectures; uses jargon without unpacking |

### 4.2 Hook library (first line / first 3 seconds — med-exam flavored)

Curiosity: "Everyone picks B. B is wrong. Here's why." · "This one word in the question changes the answer." · "90% of candidates miss this on OMSB."
Story: "Ghassan failed this question. Twice." · "The night before my DHA exam, I learned this trick."
Value: "3 ECG patterns that show up on every Gulf licensing exam." · "Stop memorizing. Do this instead:"
Contrarian: "US exam tools won't pass you in the Gulf. Here's the gap." · "Flashcards are overrated for the Arab Board."

### 4.3 Carousel template — "Question → Answer" (Problem-Proof framework)

The saveable workhorse (what AMBOSS/UWorld win on), localized and character-led:

```
Slide 1  HOOK      The exam question + Ghassan picking the tempting wrong option
Slide 2  TENSION   "Why most candidates choose that…" (the trap)
Slide 3  TURN      Bissan: the one distinguishing fact
Slide 4  ANSWER    Correct option + why each distractor is wrong (the UWorld discipline)
Slide 5  RETAIN    1-line memory hook / mnemonic
Slide 6  CTA       "Try 1 real question → link in bio" (tracked)
```
Clinical reviewer signs off slides 3–4 before scheduling. Fixed brand template = instant recognition.

### 4.4 Reel scripts (3-second rule: visual + verbal + text hook all hit in second 1)

**Reel A — "Ghassan's Mistake" (20–30s, Problem-Solution):**
```
0–3s   Ghassan on screen, bold text: "I picked B. Confidently."  (visual + verbal + overlay hook)
3–10s  The question + why B is tempting
10–22s Bissan enters: the distinguishing fact → correct answer
22–30s "Which would YOU pick? 1 free question in bio." (tracked CTA)
```
**Reel B — "In 30 seconds" product demo (real app footage only):**
```
0–3s   Show the end: a solved question screen. "Pass the Gulf exam. Watch."
3–8s   "Here's how Bayan does it"
8–25s  Screen-record the real app: pick → explain → next
25–30s "Start free → bio" (tracked)
```
Subtitles mandatory (3–5 words/line, key word highlighted) — most views are muted.

### 4.5 "Every piece works twice" (formalizing Nasim's SEO↔IG loop)
For each Bayan SEO article → make its Reel. IG sends people to the article; the article embeds the Reel.
One production, two placements, one tracked path to the sample quiz. This is the model's core efficiency
and the strongest argument for the "work with Dr. Hiva's team" option in Nasim's proposal.

## 5. Four growth loops (zero ad budget — from marketing-ideas)

1. **The 7-Day Gulf-Licensing Streak (challenge, #115-type).** One question/day in Stories for 7 days;
   candidates reply to keep a streak; day 7 → sample quiz. Drives daily DMs + saves, builds the habit.
2. **Candidate referral loop (#93/#137).** "Tag the study partner who always picks the wrong option."
   Exam candidates study in groups — tagging is native and free reach.
3. **UGC proof.** Repost candidates who pass ("Passed DHA with Bayan") — social proof + the exact
   conversion story the CEO wants, with consent.
4. **Comparison content (#11).** One honest carousel: "US-exam tools vs. Gulf-exam reality" — captures
   the search/decision moment where Bayan is the only right answer. (No naming competitors negatively;
   frame as audience-fit.)

## 6. Measurement & operating rhythm (conversion-first)

- **Before launch:** pull 30-day Insights baseline (date range, timezone, follower base); install one
  **tracked bio link / UTM** so every post→quiz path is countable.
- **Weekly 20-min review:** top/bottom posts by **reach and saves+shares**, bio-link taps, quiz-starts,
  one change for next week. Report **counts and rates**, never likes alone.
- **Monthly report:** median performance by **pillar × format** vs. baseline; the decision for next month.
- **Honesty rule:** a rise in reach is **not** a conversion win without downstream quiz-start/sign-up evidence.
