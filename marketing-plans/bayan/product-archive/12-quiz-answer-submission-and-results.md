## Per-question surface (in-quiz)

Each question renders with a rich clinical vignette and A–E options. Header
strip: running score (`0✓ / 0✗`), `Question N of 5`, `Labs` panel (🔬), flag
(⚑), daily goal counter (`7/10`), streak (`2d streak`), **Save & Exit**.

- Item id shown (e.g. **Q3583**), specialty tag, difficulty
  (**Easy/Medium/Hard/Complex**), and a Bloom-level tag (**Application**,
  **Diagnosis**).
- **Elimination:** "Right-click or long-press to eliminate options" (strike
  through distractors).
- Keyboard-answerable: "press 1-5 / A-E".
- Select an option → **Confirm Answer** button appears → confirming grades it.

### Post-answer feedback (per question)

On Confirm, the item grades instantly (**CORRECT / INCORRECT** verdict) and
expands a structured explanation block:

- **✔ Explanation** — "WHY THIS IS CORRECT", full pathophysiology + rationale,
  option-by-option reasoning.
- **📚 Deep Dive** — extended teaching content.
- **📋 References (N)** — cited sources (counted; e.g. "References 5").
- **Copy explanation** button.
- **👍 / 👎** quality rating on the explanation.
- **▶ Discussion** — per-question discussion thread.
- **Difficulty feedback:** Too Easy / Just Right / Too Hard (feeds adaptivity).
- **Flag This Question** (routes to the reviewer/quality queue).
- **★★★★★** item difficulty rating + **SI UNITS** lab-unit toggle (honors the
  Settings lab-unit preference from doc 07).

## Results summary screen (end of quiz)

The capstone screen — the single best marketing artifact of the study loop:

- **Score band:** `0%` · `0 / 5 correct` · verdict label ("More practice
  needed").
- **Time Analysis:** Total (55s) · Avg/Q (11s) · Fastest (3s) · Slowest (43s),
  plus a per-question Q1…Q5 strip.
- **📈 Accuracy Trend** ("Improving!") — Start → End sparkline.
- **🧠 Adaptive Learning Update:** Total Qs · Session Streak (🔥) ·
  **Level X/5** (the adaptive engine's live difficulty level for the learner).
- **📅 Spaced review:** "5 wrong answers scheduled for spaced review" — wrong
  items auto-enrolled into spaced repetition (SRS integration).
- **By Specialty:** per-specialty correct/total (Internal Medicine 0/1,
  Endocrinology 0/1, Rheumatology 0/1, Critical Care/ICU 0/1, Neurology 0/1).
- **Recommended Learning:** targeted article reads (e.g. "Osteoporosis: DEXA
  Screening, Bisphosphonates, FRAX", "Inflammatory Myopathies") + **Explore
  <specialty>** hub links — closes the loop back into the content graph (doc 11).
- **Share Your Score:** WhatsApp · X/Twitter · Copy (built-in social/viral loop).
- **Telegram CTA:** "Get daily MCQs on Telegram — Join @BayanMedEd".
- Navigation: **Home** · **Review Answers**.

## Marketing takeaways

1. **The results screen is the product's dopamine engine** — score + time
   analysis + adaptive level + spaced-review scheduling + specialty breakdown +
   recommended reading, all on one screen. This is the demo-able "smart study
   loop," not a flat score.
2. **Adaptive + spaced repetition are real, not slideware** — every wrong answer
   is auto-scheduled for spaced review and the learner's Level (X/5) updates per
   session. Concrete proof for the adaptive-engine story (docs 07/08/10).
3. **The loop closes back into the content graph** — results recommend specific
   articles and Explore hubs (doc 11), so a quiz drives the next study action.
4. **Built-in social loop** — Share Your Score (WhatsApp/X) + the @BayanMedEd
   Telegram CTA are organic-growth surfaces (EMPOWER "Word of mouth" / referral).
5. **Clinician-grade explanations with cited references** — "WHY THIS IS
   CORRECT" + Deep Dive + References(N) + 👍/👎, consistent with the
   human-reviewed-content brand rule; never "AI-powered" without that caveat.
