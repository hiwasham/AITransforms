03 Areas for improvement

Three highlighted areas — chosen for immediate revenue impact. Each carries its own colour accent and structured improvement opportunities.

3
Customer Onboarding
1/7
Current state

B2B pilots have no documented sequence; orientation and handoff are reactive via ad-hoc emails / calls.

Impact on business

High friction for institutional admins and clinicians, potentially leading to low engagement during the free trial — which jeopardizes the conversion to paid seats.

Improvement opportunities
  • Create a Pilot Welcome Kit PDF or Notion page.
  • Standardize a 30-minute Orientation Webinar recording to replace live calls.
  • Implement a simple 3-email automated sequence for new B2B admins.
4
Asset & Information Collection
1/7
Current state

No standardized forms. Clinician lists and logos are requested ad-hoc via email.

Impact on business

Significant time waste in manual follow-ups and increased risk of data entry errors when provisioning seats.

Improvement opportunities
  • Implement a single Institutional Setup Form (Tally or Typeform).
  • Require a CSV upload for clinician emails to eliminate manual list handling.
  • Automate a "Missing Info" reminder for pilot participants.
6
Expectation Setting & Success Metrics
1/7
Current state

Informal communication of engagement targets. No shared dashboard or milestone document.

Impact on business

Without clear, documented success criteria, the institution may not realize the value of the pilot — making the "conversion" conversation difficult at the 1-year mark.

Improvement opportunities
  • Define 3 key "Success Signals" (e.g., 50 questions/week per user).
  • Create a Pilot Roadmap one-pager showing key dates and review points.
  • Send a monthly Engagement Snapshot to the institutional admin.

04 Owner dependency analysis

All areas · 0/5 Critical dependency

The CEO is the primary operator for every B2B delivery task.

From invoicing to manual seat provisioning in Supabase. Scalability is capped by the CEO's available 10 hours/week. If three large institutions sign pilots at once, delivery will fail — or B2C growth will stall.

Mitigation strategies
  • Productize the onboarding — turn the manual orientation into a self-serve video / guide.
  • Delegate provisioning — train a VA or team member (like Nasim) to handle seat setup using a documented SOP.
  • Automate reporting — use a basic dashboard tool to pull the Supabase queries currently run manually.

05 Strategic recommendations

Two horizons. High-priority items are immediate-impact; medium-priority items protect retention and reduce future drag.

High priority · immediate impact
Standardized Intake & Provisioning

Move from email requests to a structured form. This prevents "chasing" information and allows you to delegate the actual setup.

Medium priority · retention & efficiency
B2B Success Roadmap + Automated Cadence
  • B2B Success Roadmap — document what "success" looks like for these pilots. It turns a "free trial" into a professional "partnership."
  • Automated Communication Cadence — set up a simple monthly check-in email to admins to keep the pilot top-of-mind without manual effort.
