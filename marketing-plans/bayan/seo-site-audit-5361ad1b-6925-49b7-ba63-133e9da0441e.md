# Bayan SEO Audit — Complete Finding Report

**Website:** https://bayan.edu.om/  
**Audit date:** 2026-09-27  
**Scope:** Site structure, 10 priority pages, on-page SEO, crawlability, canonical signals, sitemap/robots, mobile viewport, and homepage performance indicators.

> **Evidence note:** Findings are based on JavaScript-rendered Firecrawl crawls and targeted page reads, plus PageSpeed Insights and CrUX checks. This is a focused priority-page audit, not a complete crawl-level duplicate-title, broken-link, image, or canonical inventory. Where tool responses conflicted, the report labels the item for source-level verification rather than treating it as confirmed.

---

## 1. Executive summary

### Overall assessment

**Overall SEO health: Good foundations with material cleanup opportunities.**

Bayan has a strong, logically organized medical-education site with clear audience paths, Gulf licensing pages, structured clinical courses, calculators, a library, blog content, and conversion routes. The site is crawlable over HTTPS, exposes `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`, returns HTTP 200 on the sampled priority pages, and uses responsive viewport tags.

The largest near-term SEO risks are metadata duplication, heading hierarchy, indexation-policy misalignment, and relevance mismatches in internal linking. Mobile performance is also an opportunity: the homepage scored **63/100** for mobile Performance in the PageSpeed lab run. Real-user Core Web Vitals were unavailable from CrUX during this audit, so field performance remains unverified.

### Severity legend

- **Critical:** Can materially prevent indexing, suppress the wrong page, misdirect search intent, or create a significant technical/crawl failure. Validate and fix first.
- **Warning:** Likely to reduce visibility, click-through rate, topical relevance, or conversion efficiency, but does not appear to block the site broadly.
- **Info:** Improvement opportunity, hygiene issue, or item requiring confirmation rather than a confirmed defect.

### Highest-priority actions

1. **[Critical — verify immediately] Confirm the `/courses` canonical.** One rendered structured-head read returned the homepage as the canonical for `/courses`; if that is present in deployed HTML, make `/courses` self-canonical.
2. **[Critical] Align sitemap contents with robots/indexation policy.** Remove or exclude auth/account URLs and any URLs that are blocked or intentionally non-indexable.
3. **[Warning] Give `/pricing` unique title and meta description.** It appears to reuse homepage metadata despite serving a distinct pricing and institutional-access intent.
4. **[Warning] Correct heading structure.** Add an H1 to `/sample-quiz`; reduce multiple H1s on the homepage and `/prometric` to one primary H1 with H2/H3 sections.
5. **[Warning] Correct Nursing exam-card destinations.** Multiple visible exam cards route to the same OEN quiz URL, creating a clear intent/destination mismatch.
6. **[Warning] Improve mobile performance.** Homepage PageSpeed mobile Performance is 63/100; obtain the full Lighthouse opportunity breakdown and prioritize the highest measured savings.

## 2. Site structure and information architecture

### What was found

The Firecrawl site map identified a broad platform architecture:

- **Audience hubs:** `/prometric`, `/student`, `/postgrad`, `/nursing`
- **Exam pages:** `/omsb-exam`, `/dha-exam`, `/moh-exam`, `/smle-exam`, `/nursing-exam-prep`, `/prep/scfhs`, `/prep/arab-board`, `/prep/mrcp`
- **Product hubs:** `/courses`, `/tools`, `/calculators`, `/osce`, `/virtual-patient`, `/flashcards`, `/pharma`, `/library`, `/quiz`, `/sample-quiz`
- **Content:** `/blog` and many exam/clinical articles; `/library` and numeric knowledge pages
- **Conversion:** `/pricing`, `/login`, `/signup`, `/register`, and Prometric activation/checkout routes
- **Trust and institutional pages:** `/about`, `/global-access`, `/pilot`, `/careers`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/disclaimer`

The homepage provides two strong discovery models: **Choose Your Path** and **Structured Courses & Tools**. Commercial paths are reachable from the homepage, Pricing, and the Gulf Licensing hub.

### Top priority pages

1. Homepage — https://www.bayan.edu.om/
2. Gulf Licensing — https://www.bayan.edu.om/prometric
3. Pricing — https://www.bayan.edu.om/pricing
4. Courses — https://www.bayan.edu.om/courses
5. Sample Quiz — https://www.bayan.edu.om/sample-quiz
6. OMSB guide — https://www.bayan.edu.om/omsb-exam
7. Medical Students — https://www.bayan.edu.om/student
8. Postgraduate — https://www.bayan.edu.om/postgrad
9. Nursing — https://www.bayan.edu.om/nursing

### Structural findings

#### [Info] Clear top-level hub structure

**Evidence:** The homepage routes to audience tracks, courses, calculators, OSCE, virtual patients, flashcards, pricing, sample quiz, and signup.

**Recommendation:** Preserve this architecture. Use it as the basis for a consistent internal-linking model: audience page → exam/product pages → supporting articles → conversion page.

#### [Warning] Deep URL inventory needs crawl and indexation controls

**Evidence:** The site map includes many course modules, library records, calculators, and parameterized activation/checkout routes.

**Risk:** Deep pages can produce duplicate metadata, weak canonical signals, thin content, or crawl budget dilution if every URL is treated as a standalone SEO landing page.

**Recommendation:** Classify URLs into:

- Indexable SEO landing pages
- Indexable supporting content
- Authenticated/product routes
- Transactional parameterized routes
- Utility/legal pages

Then enforce canonical, robots, and sitemap rules by class.

#### [Warning] Potential topic overlap and cannibalization

**Evidence:** Exam-specific pages, Prometric pathways, and blog articles cover overlapping OMSB, DHA, nursing, Arab Board, MRCP, and Gulf licensing topics.

**Recommendation:** Build a keyword-to-URL map. Assign one primary intent to each exam landing page, keep the hub as a category page, and link supporting articles to the canonical destination rather than allowing several pages to compete for the same query.

#### [Info] International SEO signals require a dedicated validation pass

**Evidence:** Rendered metadata exposes English content and alternate locale values such as `ar_OM`, `ar_SA`, `ar_AE`, and `en_GB`.

**Recommendation:** Validate actual hreflang tags, reciprocal return links, locale URLs, self-referencing canonicals, and sitemap alternates. Metadata fields alone do not confirm a valid hreflang implementation.

## 3. On-page SEO findings

### 3.1 Metadata

#### [Warning] Title tags are broadly too long

**Affected:** All 10 priority pages reviewed.

**Evidence:** Observed titles range from approximately 65 to 131 characters; common SERP display guidance is often around 50–60 characters, though Google truncation is pixel-based rather than a strict character limit.

**Impact:** Search snippets may truncate the primary value proposition or place important query terms beyond the visible area.

**Recommendation:** Shorten titles, remove redundant brand suffixes, and keep the primary intent near the beginning. Preserve uniqueness across pages.

Examples:

- Homepage: `Medical Exam Prep for Doctors, Nurses & Students | Bayan`
- Gulf Licensing: `Gulf Licensing Exam Prep: DHA, OMSB & SCFHS | Bayan`
- Courses: `Clinical Medical Courses & Certificates | Bayan`
- Student: `Medical Student Exam Prep: MCQs & Courses | Bayan`
- Postgraduate: `Internal Medicine Board Prep: ABIM, OMSB & MRCP | Bayan`
- Nursing: `Nursing Exam Prep: OEN, DHA, SNLE & Prometric | Bayan`

These are working examples, not approved production copy; validate against current keyword priorities and actual page claims.

#### [Warning] Pricing duplicates homepage metadata

**Affected:** `/pricing` and `/`.

**Evidence:** Pricing returned the same broad homepage title and approximately 297-character homepage meta description in the rendered metadata read.

**Impact:** Weakens the Pricing page’s relevance for plan, trial, price, and institutional-access searches and creates duplicate snippet signals.

**Recommendation:** Give Pricing unique metadata focused on plans, free access/trial terms, audience tracks, and institutional pricing. Example:

- Title: `Bayan Medical Exam Prep Plans & Pricing`
- Description: `Compare Bayan plans for physicians, nurses, and medical students, including free access, premium features, and institutional options.`

Verify all pricing/trial claims before publishing.

#### [Warning] Homepage and OMSB meta descriptions are too long

**Affected:** `/` and `/omsb-exam`.

**Evidence:** Homepage description is approximately 297 characters; OMSB description approximately 241 characters.

**Impact:** Likely truncation and diluted click-through message.

**Recommendation:** Rewrite around 150–160 characters, emphasizing one audience, one core benefit, and one clear next step. Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “definitive” or “most comprehensive” unless documented.

#### [Info] Repeated broad keyword metadata

**Affected:** Multiple pages.

**Evidence:** Several templates expose broad repeated `keywords` lists spanning many exams and products.

**Impact:** The `keywords` meta tag is not a meaningful Google ranking signal; repeated lists add maintenance overhead and can signal unfocused page targeting.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize unique title, description, visible content, headings, structured data, and internal links. Keep keyword metadata only if required by another system.

### 3.2 Heading hierarchy

#### [Warning] Sample Quiz has no detected H1

**Affected:** `/sample-quiz`.

**Evidence:** Structured extraction returned an empty H1 array on a page with indexable question content.

**Impact:** Weakens the document’s primary topic signal and accessibility/semantic structure.

**Recommendation:** Add one visible H1 such as `Try a Free Board-Style Medical Quiz`. Keep the question prompt and category as supporting content.

#### [Warning] Homepage and Gulf Licensing page have multiple H1s

**Affected:** `/` and `/prometric`.

**Evidence:** Homepage returned seven H1s; Prometric returned five H1s.

**Impact:** Makes primary page topic and section hierarchy less clear to search engines and assistive technologies.

**Recommendation:** Keep one primary H1 per page. Convert section headings to H2s and nested headings to H3s.

#### [Info] Several H1s are generic or motivational

**Affected:** Pricing, Courses, Student, Postgraduate, and Prometric.

**Evidence:** Examples include `Choose Your Track`, `Courses`, `Medical Student Track`, and `Master Internal Medicine Board Exams.`

**Recommendation:** Use descriptive H1s that combine audience/topic and intent, while retaining motivational copy as a subheading:

- Pricing: `Medical Exam Preparation Plans for Physicians, Nurses & Students`
- Courses: `Clinical Medical Courses, Cases & Certificates`
- Student: `Medical Student Exam Preparation`
- Postgraduate: `Internal Medicine Board Exam Preparation`
- Prometric: `Gulf Healthcare Licensing Exam Preparation`

### 3.3 Images and alt text

#### [Info] Empty alt attributes need intentional review

**Affected:** Homepage hero background, homepage feature-network image, Pricing sample image.

**Evidence:** Structured extraction returned empty alt values for these images.

**Impact:** None if the images are purely decorative; accessibility and image search impact if they communicate meaningful content.

**Recommendation:** Confirm each image’s role. Keep `alt=""` for decorative assets; add concise descriptive alt for meaningful imagery. Avoid keyword stuffing.

#### [Info] Nursing logo alt is compressed

**Affected:** `/nursing`.

**Evidence:** Logo alt returned as `BayanNursing`.

**Recommendation:** Use natural brand text such as `Bayan Nursing` if that matches the visual identity; do not add exam keywords to a logo alt.

#### [Info] Good alt coverage on key visual pages

**Evidence:** Prometric returned descriptive alt text for hero, process, and feature images; homepage audience cards also had descriptive labels.

**Recommendation:** Preserve this standard on new course, exam, and campaign pages.

### 3.4 Internal linking

#### [Warning] Nursing exam cards have mismatched destinations

**Affected:** `/nursing`.

**Evidence:** Seven visible cards for OEN, DHA, SNLE, QCHP, DOH, Prometric, and NCLEX-RN all point to the same OEN parameterized quiz URL.

**Impact:** Users can land on the wrong experience; search engines receive weak relevance signals; exam-specific link equity is not distributed to matching pages.

**Recommendation:** Create or use distinct exam-matched destinations. Each card should link to a URL whose title, H1, content, and CTA match the visible exam. If all cards intentionally use one shared question pool, explain that clearly while still providing exam-specific informational pages.

#### [Warning] Audience pages are under-linked to core products and content

**Affected:** `/student`, `/postgrad`, `/omsb-exam`, `/sample-quiz`.

**Evidence:** Extracted links show core account and library routes but limited direct links to Courses, OSCE, Virtual Patients, Flashcards, calculators, exam guides, and relevant supporting articles.

**Recommendation:** Use contextual internal links, not just navigation links:

- Audience page → relevant products
- Exam guide → sample quiz, relevant course, pathway access, related articles
- Blog article → exam hub, course, and Pricing where appropriate

#### [Info] Prometric uses many parameterized conversion links

**Affected:** `/prometric`.

**Evidence:** Exam and plan links use `/prometric/activate?...` and `/prometric/checkout?...` URLs.

**Recommendation:** Keep these links for conversion, but establish clean crawlable exam information pages and define canonical/noindex handling for transaction variants.

#### [Info] Courses contains a placeholder link

**Affected:** `/courses`.

**Evidence:** `Radiology Basics Coming Soon` links to `https://www.bayan.edu.om/courses#`.

**Recommendation:** Use a non-link “Coming soon” card or a valid waitlist/detail page.

#### [Info] Blog is a strong content hub but library URLs are numeric

**Affected:** `/blog` and `/library/{id}`.

**Evidence:** Blog links extensively to library records with numeric URLs.

**Recommendation:** Add breadcrumbs, topic clusters, related-content links, and clear contextual links to exam/course pages. Consider descriptive slugs for future library content; do not migrate existing URLs without a redirect and canonical plan.

## 4. Technical SEO findings

### 4.1 robots.txt

#### [Info] robots.txt is present and broadly sensible

**URL:** https://www.bayan.edu.om/robots.txt  
**Status:** HTTP 200, `text/plain; charset=utf-8`.

Observed features:

- `User-agent: *`
- `Allow: /`
- Private/authenticated route disallows
- Sitemap declaration: `Sitemap: https://www.bayan.edu.om/sitemap.xml`

#### [Warning] Robots and public URL patterns may be misaligned

**Evidence:** Robots blocks slash-prefixed patterns such as `/osce/`, `/flashcards/`, `/challenge/`, `/quiz/`, `/register/`, and `/signup/`, while the crawl map/sitemap expose corresponding non-slash URLs.

**Impact:** Redirect or trailing-slash behavior could unintentionally block intended public pages, or the sitemap may advertise pages search engines should not crawl.

**Recommendation:** Test each final URL after redirects and choose one of two clear policies:

- Public product page: allow crawl and include in sitemap
- Authenticated/utility page: block or noindex, and exclude from sitemap

### 4.2 sitemap.xml

#### [Pass with warning] Sitemap exists and is broadly comprehensive

**URL:** https://www.bayan.edu.om/sitemap.xml  
**Status:** HTTP 200, `application/xml`.

It includes major public hubs, exam pages, blog articles, course pages/modules, tools, calculators, and other routes.

#### [Warning] Sitemap includes auth and likely non-indexable URLs

**Evidence:** Entries observed include `/login`, `/signup`, `/register`, `/reset-password`, and `/quiz`; some correspond to robots-disallowed route patterns.

**Impact:** Wastes crawl attention and creates inconsistent indexation signals.

**Recommendation:** Remove non-indexable account, authentication, checkout, and utility URLs unless there is a documented SEO reason to keep them. Ensure every remaining URL returns 200, is indexable, and has a matching canonical.

#### [Info] Sitemaps appear to use a common last-modified timestamp

**Evidence:** Many visible entries shared `2026-09-27T03:32:03.423Z`.

**Recommendation:** Generate `lastmod` from actual content or template changes. A global generation timestamp should not be used as a proxy for page freshness.

### 4.3 Canonicals

#### [Critical — verify] `/courses` may canonicalize to the homepage

**Evidence:** Sampled canonical results:

- `/`: self-canonical — pass
- `/prometric`: self-canonical — pass
- `/pricing`: self-canonical — pass
- `/sample-quiz`: self-canonical — pass
- `/omsb-exam`: self-canonical — pass
- `/courses`: one structured rendered-head read returned `https://www.bayan.edu.om/`

Other Firecrawl metadata identified the source URL as `/courses`, so this may be an extraction/cache inconsistency. It is not safe to call it a confirmed production defect without literal source verification.

**Recommendation:** Inspect deployed HTML and require:

```html
<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.bayan.edu.om/courses" />
```

Then sample course details, modules, blog articles, library records, calculators, and parameterized activation/checkout pages. Clean pages should self-canonicalize; parameterized transaction variants should canonicalize to the clean parent or be noindexed according to purpose.

### 4.4 Mobile viewport and indexability basics

#### [Pass] Responsive viewport is present

Rendered head evidence confirmed responsive viewport tags on the sampled pages:

- Homepage: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`
- Prometric: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`
- Pricing: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`
- Courses: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` in structured head extraction
- Sample Quiz: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`
- OMSB: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`

Sampled pages returned `<html lang="en">` where extracted. The site is served over HTTPS and sampled priority pages returned HTTP 200.

**Recommendation:** Retain the responsive viewport and confirm no template introduces horizontal overflow, intrusive interstitials, or materially different mobile content.

### 4.5 Page load indicators

#### [Warning] Mobile homepage performance is an opportunity

**PageSpeed Insights mobile lab run:**

- Performance: **63/100**
- SEO: **92/100**
- Best Practices: **88/100**
- Run: `2026-09-27T08:32:56.497Z`

**Impact:** A 63 mobile Performance score suggests that loading and interaction costs may affect mobile experience and conversion, even though the score alone does not identify the cause.

**Recommendation:** Run a full Lighthouse opportunity report and prioritize measured savings in this order:

1. Largest above-the-fold image and responsive image delivery
2. JavaScript execution and unused JavaScript
3. Render-blocking CSS/fonts and font loading
4. Third-party scripts
5. Server response and caching
6. Layout stability and interaction responsiveness

Do not claim specific LCP/FCP/CLS/TBT causes until the detailed audit values are captured.

#### [Info] No CrUX field data was available

**Evidence:** Mobile origin query for `https://www.bayan.edu.om` returned `404 — chrome ux report data not found`.

**Interpretation:** This is not evidence of poor performance; it means field data was unavailable from the queried dataset at audit time.

**Recommendation:** Keep collecting real-user Web Vitals through site instrumentation and repeat the CrUX check later. Use field data over lab data for judging whether changes improved real user experience.

## 6. Prioritized action plan

### Priority 1 — Critical validation and indexation controls

1. **Verify the literal `/courses` canonical in deployed HTML.**
   - Owner: engineering/SEO
   - Done when: `/courses` returns a self-referencing canonical and the value matches the sitemap URL.
2. **Reconcile robots.txt and sitemap.xml.**
   - Remove login, registration, password reset, authenticated quiz, checkout, or other non-indexable routes from the sitemap unless explicitly intended.
   - Confirm final trailing-slash behavior for public product pages.
   - Done when: every sitemap URL is crawlable, returns 200, is indexable, and has the intended canonical.
3. **Create a URL classification table.**
   - Classify pages as SEO landing, supporting content, product/authenticated, transaction, utility, or legal.
   - Apply consistent canonical, robots, and sitemap rules.

### Priority 2 — High-impact on-page fixes

4. **Rewrite unique titles and meta descriptions for the 10 priority pages.**
   - Start with Homepage, Pricing, Prometric, OMSB, Nursing, and the audience hubs.
   - Remove redundant brand repetition and keep the primary intent near the front.
5. **Fix H1 hierarchy.**
   - Add one H1 to Sample Quiz.
   - Retain one H1 on Homepage and Prometric; demote section headings.
   - Make generic H1s more descriptive on Pricing, Courses, Student, and Postgrad.
6. **Fix Nursing pathway relevance.**
   - Map each exam card to its correct informational and/or quiz destination.
   - Ensure destination title, H1, copy, canonical, and CTA match the exam.
7. **Strengthen contextual internal links.**
   - Connect audience pages to products.
   - Connect exam guides to sample quiz, relevant courses, supporting articles, and the appropriate access page.
   - Add breadcrumbs and related-content modules to blog/library content.

### Priority 3 — Performance and accessibility hygiene

8. **Run a complete mobile Lighthouse opportunity review.**
   - Capture LCP, FCP, CLS, INP/TBT, TTFB, and opportunity savings.
   - Optimize based on measured evidence rather than generic assumptions.
9. **Review image alt attributes.**
   - Confirm empty values are decorative.
   - Add descriptive alt to meaningful imagery.
10. **Replace the `/courses#` placeholder link.**
    - Use a non-link “Coming soon” state or a valid future course/waitlist URL.
11. **Improve library information scent over time.**
    - Use descriptive slugs for new library content.
    - Preserve redirects and canonicals if existing numeric URLs are migrated.

### Priority 4 — Measurement and ongoing governance

12. **Establish a recurring technical SEO check.**
    - Monitor sitemap/robots consistency, canonical drift, status codes, duplicate titles/descriptions, and redirect behavior.
13. **Track real-user Web Vitals.**
    - Recheck CrUX when data becomes available and compare against RUM measurements.
14. **Create a keyword-to-URL map.**
    - Prevent cannibalization among Prometric, exam pages, blog posts, and library content.
15. **Review date-sensitive exam content.**
    - Update OMSB year references and factual exam details on a defined schedule, with citations or source verification for high-stakes claims.

## 7. Recommended success criteria

The audit can be considered materially improved when:

- `/courses` and all important public pages self-canonicalize correctly.
- The sitemap contains only intended indexable URLs and has no robots conflicts.
- Each priority page has a unique, focused title and meta description.
- Every indexable priority page has exactly one primary H1.
- Nursing exam cards route to matching destinations.
- The homepage and audience pages link contextually to core products and exam content.
- The `/courses#` placeholder is removed.
- Mobile PageSpeed Performance improves from the observed **63/100** baseline, with detailed LCP/CLS/interaction evidence captured.
- Real-user Web Vitals are measured through RUM or become available in CrUX.
