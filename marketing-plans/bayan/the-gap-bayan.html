◆ The shape of the fix

This is not a product problem. It's a signal problem.

Bayan already has the substance — 5,589 clinician-reviewed questions across 55+ countries, on a site that AI crawlers can read today. What's missing is the layer that translates that substance into direct answers, structured guidance, and keyword-matched pages the market can find.

The tracked levers alone move health from 25 → 54 and GEO from 71 → ~90, targeting question-bank keywords where difficulty is 5–18 and one term is already at #28. Nothing on that path requires building something new — only publishing what Bayan is uniquely able to say.
