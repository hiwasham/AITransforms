Problems & Pain Points

Core problem

“I stopped guessing what to study.”

Learners waste time on generic prep not aligned to the specific Gulf / international exam gating their career.

Why alternatives fall short

  • Foreign question banks aren't aligned to Gulf licensing exams (OMSB, Prometric, DHA).
  • Generic tools don't adapt to the learner's weak areas.
  • AI-only content is not clinically trustworthy for high-stakes medicine.

What it costs them

  • Failed exams delay or block a Gulf medical career.
  • Wasted study months.
  • Money spent on ill-fitting tools.

Emotional tension

  • Fear of failing a career-gating exam.
  • Doubt about whether prep is actually working.
  • Distrust of AI in medicine.

Differentiation

Prep that matches the actual exam and the actual patients you'll treat → higher first-attempt pass confidence.
