# How To Create Magnetic Names For Your Product, Services, and Ideas

The name that you give your product or service is the highest-leverage Marketing you can do. Choose a name that’s appealing to the ear – not to the eye – because that’s how brains remember. Focus on repetitive sounds, rhythmic syllables and powerful associations.
