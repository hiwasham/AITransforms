# The Effective Email Template

Email is one of the most powerful forms of marketing available to us today and it is something you definitely want to use as part of your marketing campaigns and customer follow up sequences. However, in order for your emails to be effective, it must be able to cut through the noise and relay your message to your customer in a clear and effective way. Use this checklist to organize your ideas and come up with emails that work.
