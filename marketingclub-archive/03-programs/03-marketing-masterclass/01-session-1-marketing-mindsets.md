# Session 1: Marketing Mindsets

In this video, you'll learn about creating effective marketing strategies to grow your business.

You'll gain insights into implementing tactics rather than just understanding them, by tuning up your marketing mindsets to be more proactive. The concept of testing and applying different marketing aspects to evaluate what works best for your business will be introduced.

Plus, you'll learn about the importance of customer interaction and understanding their needs to create viable and persuasive marketing strategies.
