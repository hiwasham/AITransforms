# Session 2: First Impressions

In this video, you're going to learn about the key principles of creating impactful first impressions in marketing. You're going to discover how to communicate with your customers in a way that resonates with their needs and wants.

You'll also learn the importance of using the customer's language in your marketing materials and putting their desired results front and center. Additionally, we'll deep dive into various examples and analyze them to provide you with practical marketing strategies that you can immediately use in your own business for improved performance.

By the end of this video, you'll gain an understanding of how to create clear and direct first impressions that immediately communicate the value you provide and resonates with your audience.
