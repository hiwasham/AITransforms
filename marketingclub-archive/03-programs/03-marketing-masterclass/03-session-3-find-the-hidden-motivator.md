# Session 3: Find The Hidden Motivator

In this video, you will learn about identifying hidden emotional motivators in your clients.

You'll find out how to dig deeper beyond superficial appearances to discover your customer's secret fears, desires, and dreams. By understanding the irrational human patterns of decision-making, you will be able to create powerful marketing strategies that resonate deeply with your audience.

You'll discover how to use specific words that motivate the most, how to address your customer's subconscious worries, and how fear might be holding your clients back from making a decision.

And, you'll be provided with practical examples and case studies and gain insights on how to create marketing campaigns that directly speak to your customers' irrational motivators.
