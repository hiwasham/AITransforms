# Session 8: Write Copy that Motivates

In this video, I'll be sharing expert tips on persuasive copywriting. We'll delve into the importance of using the right language - the language of your customers - to evoke emotional responses and motivate people to take action.

Also, I'll explore the distinction between features, advantages and benefits, and why it's crucial to focus on benefits when marketing, and I'll guide you in writing powerful, motivating content using easy to understand language and efficient delivery methods for maximum effectiveness.

Explore the concept of benefits-driven copy with short words and sentences, yet rich in detail. On this copywriting journey, we'll also touch on persuasive language, the significance of 'long copy', and the tested techniques to draw in your reader.
