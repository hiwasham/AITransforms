# Session 10: Design For Understanding

In this video, we're going to explore common design mistakes, such as designing for appearance over understanding, making your design overly complex, or underutilizing established design principles.

We'll discuss simple and effective design rules, including creating clear visual hierarchy, using natural reading gravity, and keeping alignments, colors, and fonts to a minimum. We'll also explore the power of contrast and repetition in design, and dive into some real-world examples from successful brands.

By the end, you'll have a better understanding of how to make your designs communicate effectively and stand out in the right way. So, come on, let's dive in together and enhance your understanding of design!
