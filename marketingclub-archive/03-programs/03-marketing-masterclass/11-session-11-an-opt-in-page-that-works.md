# Session 11: An Opt-In Page That Works

In this video, you'll learn about the importance of opt in pages for your website.

These are crucial for generating and maintaining leads -- potential customers who may not purchase your product or service immediately but might do so after some effective follow-ups. You'll be guided on how to build an opt in page that grabs people's attention and increases the chances for them to eventually make a purchase.

In addition to an informative video and copy, simple designs and relevant opt-in incentives are also explained. This informative video is ideal for individuals and businesses aiming to maximize their online presence through an effective opt in page strategy.
