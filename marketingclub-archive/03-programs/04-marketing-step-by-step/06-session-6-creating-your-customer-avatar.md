# Creating Your Customer Avatar

Most business owners think of their customers as a group of people, and communicate to them as a group. This is a deadly mistake.

In this session you’ll learn what your “Customer Avatar” is, why it’s the key to your marketing success, and how to speak in the “voice” of your customer so they feel an instant connection with you…
