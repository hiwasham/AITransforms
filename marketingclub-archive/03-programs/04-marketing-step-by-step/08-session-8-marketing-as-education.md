# Marketing as Education

There’s a shift happening in Marketing. More savvy business owners are seeing that Education is actually the best Marketing.

In this session you’ll learn why educating your customer in your marketing can boost your sales, how to use this education to build trust, and why giving your marketing an “Editorial” design can dramatically increase response.
