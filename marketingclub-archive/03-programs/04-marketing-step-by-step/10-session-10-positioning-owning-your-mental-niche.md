# Positioning – Owning Your Mental Niche

One of the most overlooked – and powerful – things you can do to grow your business is to “position” it powerfully.

In this session you’ll learn what positioning is and isn’t, how to use position so you don’t have to “compete” with ANYONE, and how to make your product stand out as the #1 product in its category (even if you’re just starting out).
