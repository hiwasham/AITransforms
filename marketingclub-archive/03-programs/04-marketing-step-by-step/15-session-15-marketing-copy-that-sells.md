# Marketing Copy that Sells

The words you use in your marketing (known as “copy”) are critical to your marketing success.

In this session you’ll learn a simple tip to make your copy better than 90% of marketers out there, how to use “Speak-Write” to connect with your Customers, and how to identify the language of your Customers and use it to make more sales.
