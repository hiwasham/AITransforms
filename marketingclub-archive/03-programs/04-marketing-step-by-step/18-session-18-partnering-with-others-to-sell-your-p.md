# Partnering with Others to Sell Your Products

One of the highest leverage marketing actions you can take is to partner with others to sell your products.

In this session you’ll learn how to find partners who have already spent a lot of money to get a list of YOUR customers, how to get inside the heads of your partners so they are eager to work with you, and 4 key things you must do so partners see you as professional.
