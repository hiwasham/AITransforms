# How to Generate Referrals

A free way to get more business is to have your existing customers refer their friends and other people they know to your business. Sadly, most businesses don’t take advantage of this key marketing technique.

In this session, you’ll learn the deadly referral mistake most business owners make, exactly when you should start planting the referral seed in the minds of your customers, and what to do when you get a referral to motivate customers to get you even MORE.
