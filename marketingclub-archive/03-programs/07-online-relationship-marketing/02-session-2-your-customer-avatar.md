# Your Customer Avatar

The Customer Avatar is an individual that you create in your mind to “stand in” for your individual customer.

In Session 2, you’ll learn all about creating your Customer Avatar and really getting inside the mind of your prospective customers. Imagine taking all of your best customers and combining them into a single person that has all the key traits of people who buy from you.
