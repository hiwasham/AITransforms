# Move the Free Line

In this session you’re going to learn how giving away your best ideas can attract high quality prospects and build strong relationships with your new and current customers.

Free is one of the most powerful words in marketing. “Moving The Free Line” is putting your best knowledge and insights into a product that you can GIVE AWAY to potential customers.
