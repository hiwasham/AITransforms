# Planning The Prospect’s Experience

[http://streaming_alt.s3.amazonaws.com/onlinerelationshipmarketing/onlinerelationshipmarketing_session07.mp3](http://streaming_alt.s3.amazonaws.com/onlinerelationshipmarketing/onlinerelationshipmarketing_session07.mp3)

Typically, when two people meet they start figuring out where they have something in common. When you have a substantial connection with someone you tend to open up to having a relationship with them. As you’re connecting with new customers, try to connect on something substantial.

In this last session you’ll learn how to script out your business friendships. You’ll also get a step-by-step formula for doing this and steps to take action once you’re done.
