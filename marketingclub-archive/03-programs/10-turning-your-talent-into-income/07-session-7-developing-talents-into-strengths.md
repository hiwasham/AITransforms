# Session # 7: Developing Talents into Strengths

A talent or gift is what you got when you were born. A strength is what you do with it – in other words, it’s about developing your talent into skills and expertise that you can use in the world to create value. In this session we’re going to learn how to develop our natural talents into strengths. I reveal several key mistakes most people make when they try to do this that wastes their energy and robs them of important years they could be using in much more productive ways. Check it out…
