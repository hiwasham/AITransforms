🎫 Intake & Triage
Phase 1
01
AI Automated
Ticket Submitted
School admin submits a support request via the Hamkelasi portal, email, or WhatsApp. System auto-creates a ticket record with timestamp and school ID.
R: School Admin | A: Support Lead | C: N/A
02
AI Automated
Auto-Classification
AI reads the ticket content and classifies it by type (Technical, Billing, Training, Feature Request) and assigns an initial severity score (Low / Medium / High / Critical).
R: AI Agent | A: Support Lead | C: N/A
03
AI Automated
Priority Assignment
Ticket is prioritized based on school tier (number of students, contract value) and issue severity. High-priority schools skip the queue and receive instant acknowledgment.
R: AI Agent | A: Support Lead | C: Account Manager
04
Agent Assignment & ACK
Ticket is routed to the next available Level 1 agent based on workload and specialization. Auto-acknowledgment message is sent to the school within 5 minutes of submission.
R: Support Lead | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
🔍 Level 1 — Self-Service Resolution
Phase 2
05
AI Automated
L3 Guide Suggested
AI checks the ticket against the Hamkelasi Level 3 Guide (FAQ library). If a documented answer exists, the system sends the matching guide link to the school before the agent engages.
R: AI Agent | A: Support Agent | C: School Admin
06
School Self-Resolution Check
School is given 30 minutes to confirm if the guide resolved their issue. If confirmed resolved, ticket is auto-closed. If no response or marked unresolved, it advances to agent handling.
R: School Admin | A: Support Agent | C: N/A
07
Agent First Response
Support agent reviews the ticket, verifies school identity, and opens a live conversation (chat or call). Agent uses the Hamkelasi Voice guidelines — no improvised scripts.
R: Support Agent | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
08
L1 Troubleshooting
Agent works through the documented L1 checklist for the ticket category. All steps are logged in real time inside the ticket record in Vtiger. SLA target: resolved within 2 hours.
R: Support Agent | A: Support Lead | C: N/A
🛠 Level 2 — Technical Investigation
Phase 3
09
Escalation Trigger
L2 Escalation Decision
If L1 troubleshooting does not resolve the issue within 2 hours, the agent marks the ticket for L2. Escalation reason is documented. School is notified of the handoff and new ETA.
R: Support Agent | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
10
Technical Specialist Review
L2 specialist picks up the ticket in Mizito. They access server logs, platform configs, or LMS integration data relevant to the school's environment. Diagnosis is documented in the ticket.
R: Technical Specialist | A: Support Lead | C: Support Agent
11
Root Cause Analysis
Specialist identifies whether the issue is a user error, platform bug, integration failure, or configuration gap. Root cause is tagged in the ticket for knowledge base use. SLA target: diagnosed within 4 hours.
R: Technical Specialist | A: Support Lead | C: Product Team
12
Fix Implementation
Specialist applies the fix or workaround. If a platform-level fix is needed, a dev task is opened in Mizito and linked to the ticket. School is kept informed every 60 minutes until resolved.
R: Technical Specialist | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
🚨 Escalation — Level 3 & Founder Exception
Phase 4
13
Critical Only
L3 Escalation Trigger
Escalation to L3 is triggered only if: issue affects 10+ schools simultaneously, involves data loss or security risk, or remains unresolved after 8 hours. Omid receives a Slack alert with full ticket context.
R: Support Lead | A: Omid (CEO) | C: Technical Specialist
14
CEO Exception Review
Omid reviews the escalation summary (auto-generated by AI from ticket history). He approves a resolution path — he does not troubleshoot directly. Decision is logged and becomes a new documented standard.
R: Omid (CEO) | A: Omid (CEO) | C: Technical Specialist
15
Emergency Fix Deployment
Approved fix is deployed by Technical Specialist. If a platform patch is required, it follows the expedited deployment checklist. All affected schools are contacted directly by the Support Lead.
R: Technical Specialist | A: Omid (CEO) | C: Support Lead
16
Post-Incident Brief
Within 24 hours of resolution, a brief incident report is drafted (timeline, root cause, fix applied, prevention measure). Report is added to the knowledge base and shared with all school admins if relevant.
R: Support Lead | A: Omid (CEO) | C: Technical Specialist
✅ Closure & Quality Audit
Phase 5
17
Resolution Confirmation
Agent sends a structured closure message to the school confirming the fix, the root cause (if shareable), and any preventive steps for the school to follow. School has 24 hours to reopen if unresolved.
R: Support Agent | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
18
CSAT Survey Sent
Automated satisfaction survey (3 questions, 1-5 scale) is sent to the school admin 1 hour after ticket closure. Results are logged in Vtiger and linked to the agent and ticket record.
R: AI Agent | A: Support Lead | C: School Admin
19
AI Automated
AI Quality Audit
AI scores the full ticket interaction against the Hamkelasi Voice rubric and the L3 Guide routing standard. Scores ≥7/10 are filed silently. Scores below 7 trigger a Slack alert to Support Lead for coaching review.
R: AI Agent | A: Support Lead | C: Omid (CEO)
20
Knowledge Base Update
If the ticket revealed a gap in the Level 3 Guide, the Support Lead drafts a new FAQ entry and submits it for review. Approved entries are published within 48 hours and tagged to the ticket that originated them.
R: Support Lead | A: Omid (CEO) | C: Technical Specialist

⚡ Process Insights & Bottleneck Analysis

  • Primary Bottleneck — L2 Escalation Volume: If the Level 3 Guide is not kept current, agents will skip self-service and escalate directly — inflating L2 workload. The knowledge base update loop (Step 20) is the most important preventive mechanism in this system.
  • Owner Dependency Risk: Omid is only in the critical path at Steps 14–16. This is correct for exception handling but the Slack alert in Step 13 must include a full AI-generated summary — Omid should never have to read the full ticket thread to make a decision.
  • AI Leverage Point: Steps 02, 03, 05, 18, and 19 are fully AI-automated. Ensuring these five steps are running reliably removes the equivalent of 1 full-time support agent from routine work.
  • SLA Targets Embedded: L1 resolution target is 2 hours. L2 diagnosis is 4 hours. Any ticket exceeding 8 hours without closure automatically triggers the L3 escalation path — no manual judgment required.
  • Scale Readiness: This system handles 700 schools without linear headcount growth. Quality is enforced by AI at Step 19 — meaning Omid leads by exception, not by review of every interaction.
