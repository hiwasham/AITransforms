The Training

What the four days cover

The IFS model creates real-time interplay between Parts in ways individual therapy can't — groups surface both internal and relational dynamics at once. Drawing on Chris's book Creating Healing Circles®, you'll move through three layers of learning:

1

Foundations

Formats and themes for IFS groups, how group dynamics work, and the stages groups move through as they develop.

2

Facilitation Skills

The key facilitator skills for IFS in a group setting, and how to build a container that supports deeper healing.

3

Live Practice

Live demonstrations of IFS sculpting and other experiential exercises, plus breakout groups to practice facilitating.
