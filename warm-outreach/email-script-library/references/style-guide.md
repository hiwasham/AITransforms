# Hiwa Email Style Guide

Status: seed rules. Update after importing the 50 scripts.

## Default voice
- Human, warm, ambitious, curious, and direct.
- Natural English, not corporate or AI-polished.
- Specific proof beats generic praise.
- Short is usually better than impressive.
- A small honest imperfection is okay; carelessness is not.

## Avoid
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I am reaching out to"
- "I deeply admire your work" unless backed by a specific reason
- needy energy, guilt, fake urgency, false familiarity
- generic compliments

## Preferred structure
1. Specific hook: something real about recipient/work/context.
2. Personal reason: why it matters to Hiwa.
3. Clear low-friction ask.
4. Respectful exit: no pressure.
