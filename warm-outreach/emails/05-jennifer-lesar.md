Subject: made you something for the LeSar Holdings homepage

Hi Jennifer,

I came across you in Hayden's webinar, then read up on your work — LeSar
Development, GPLA, LSS, and the San Diego County Housing Blueprint. Three
decades turning "intractable societal challenges" into actual policy is
not something you need advice on, so this isn't advice. It's a gift.

I spent an evening rebuilding your lesarholdings.com homepage. One HTML
file, attached — whoever runs your site can open it in a browser next to
the live page and compare. No strings, use it or ignore it.

What I changed and why:

  1. Added a real navigation. The current site is one long page with no
     way to jump to About, Team, or Contact — so anyone landing mid-scroll
     from a Google search on your name has no path back to context. There's
     now a proper nav.

  2. Gave each firm its own reason to exist. LDC, GPLA, and LSS all had
     identical "Visit Site" buttons, so a visitor couldn't tell which firm
     does what before clicking away. Each now has distinct copy — housing
     policy and the Housing Blueprint for LDC, and so on.

  3. Pulled the executive bios out of the JS lightbox. The `×` popup hides
     your team from search engines and adds a click on mobile. The bios are
     now inline expandable cards — readable by Google, easier on a phone.

  4. Built the visual signature from your actual work. Instead of a generic
     palette, I used navy straight from your wordmark, brick as the one
     accent (a literal nod to housing), and a thin architectural blueprint
     grid — tied to the Blueprint being your flagship deliverable, not
     decoration for its own sake.

  5. Added a newsletter capture. LDC already publishes real policy thought
     leadership, but there's nowhere on the homepage to subscribe. There's
     now a "get the monthly newsletter" block tied to that content.

One honest flag: the stat strip has a bracketed placeholder — I couldn't
find your portfolio-wide numbers (communities served, units financed), so
I left `[ ]` for whoever owns that data to fill in rather than invent a
figure. Everything else on the page is your real copy and mission language.

Two more things, separate from the redesign. Your headshot on the site is
the amateur `Jennifer-LeSar.jpg` — I ran it through an image model twice:

  - one lightly cleaned-up version (attached, e123709a…), close to the
    original, just sharper and better lit;
  - one more heavily edited version (attached, b169db6f…) that's stronger
    but clearly retouched — treat it as a "here's what a proper portrait
    could look like" reference, not a drop-in.

Keep whichever fits, or neither.

No ask here. I build these because I enjoy it, and a mission like yours
deserved a homepage as considered as the policy work behind it.
