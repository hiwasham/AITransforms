Leadership

Executive team

Decades of combined experience across community development, public policy, and systems change.

Jennifer LeSar

Founder & CEO

Jennifer LeSar combines more than three decades in community development, real estate development, and investment banking with deep knowledge of ecosystem change management and organizational strategy.

She received her M.B.A. in real estate and finance and M.A. in urban planning from UCLA, and her B.A. in political science and economics from Bryn Mawr College. She is a Fellow at The Billions Institute and a Lecturer at Stanford University on the California housing crisis.

Rich Valdez

Chief Operating Officer

Rich manages the portfolio's governance, contracting and procurement, marketing and communications, policies and procedures, and risk management. He spent over 30 years as a civil litigation attorney and 25+ years teaching at the University of San Diego School of Law.

He served on the City of San Diego's Ethics Commission and holds a J.D. from the University of San Diego School of Law and a B.A. in political science from UC San Diego.

Zack Olmstead

Executive Vice President, Operations

Zack brings 20+ years across the housing policy continuum, including nine years in executive leadership at the California Department of Housing and Community Development, six as Chief Deputy Director overseeing 1,300+ staff.

He played formative roles in creating California's Homekey and No Place Like Home programs, and holds a Master's in Public Policy and Administration from CSU Sacramento.
