Meet your grower

WG

Walter Groux

Grower & founder, Nature's Edge Farm

My microgreen journey began when a friend who'd started growing them to support her new vegan lifestyle sent me a big box of seeds after I kept asking questions about her growing pics — she knew I was a big nutrition fan. I started growing for my wife and me with no thought of a business, just nutrition.

Now I have two main microgreens passions: spreading the word on the incredible nutritional value in microgreens, and helping people learn to grow energy-packed, nutrient-dense microgreens with amazing flavor, indoors, in just 1–2 weeks — even with a tight budget, a tiny space, and no yard.

I'll be happy to help you with YOUR microgreen journey. — Walter
