What the brain is made of

Five parts, one job: keep every Bayan fact in one place

Facts

A versioned, approved record per product — figures, pricing, positioning, taglines, brand entity, live or in-development status.

6 product records · 1 brand record

Team

You, Nasim, and booth or clinical staff each ask the same brain and get the same answer. Approval stays with you.

~2 calls a week of your time

Surfaces

The sites, app store listings, brochures and booth material the brain watches for mismatches against the approved record.

Public surfaces only at Stage 0

Checks

Scheduled jobs: weekly drift check, pre-exhibition audit, pre-print fact pull.

One weekly report, not a dashboard to tend

Trust

Content locks enforced in the record itself: human-owned, clinician-reviewed, verified counts only, no outcome guarantees.

Unknowns stay marked, never guessed

Not in this

  • No learner-facing tutor over the question bank
  • No published copy without Nasim
  • No access to clinical or learner data

Where this goes

Stage 0 ships first. The rest is named, not sold.

You asked for your time back, not a platform. So the first stage is narrow enough to finish in a week, and each later stage only starts if the one before it earned it.

  1. Stage 0 — Product Brain ships first

    Canonical approved facts, ask-the-brain in English and Arabic, and the drift check. $500 one-time. Your cost: about two calls, one approval pass, one weekly report.

    The brain drafts internal and reference material from approved facts, always sourced. Anything publishable routes to Nasim. Priced on request.

    Approved facts feed your surfaces directly, so new material is born consistent instead of being reconciled afterwards. The brain stops being a checker and becomes the supply.
