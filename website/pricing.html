Drift-guard — $200/mo

A record fixes today's contradictions. A retainer stops tomorrow's. These are two separate decisions and the second one can wait.

What the retainer does

  • Re-reads the watched surfaces on a schedule
  • Flags a figure that drifts back — 4,000+ reappearing, for example
  • One weekly report: new conflicts, decisions waiting, conflicts closed
  • Keeps the Arabic and English records locked to each other
  • Adds new surfaces and new products to the record as they appear

What happens if you don't take it

Nothing breaks. The approved records are yours, in plain files, and they keep working as the answer of record. What you lose is the watching — a value that drifts back onto a live surface in three months would go unnoticed until someone spots it.

You can start it, stop it, or restart it later. Stopping it does not touch a single approved fact.
