Weekly drift check — three conflicts right now

Every Sunday at 9am Oman time, the brain checks every surface it knows about and reports which facts are drifting. The report lands in Nasim's and Dr. Abdullah's inboxes.

What the drift check does

  1. Reads every live surface — bayan.edu.om, bayanai.tech, medad.om, the App Store listing, the Google Play listing, and any brochure PDFs Hiwa uploaded.
  2. Compares what each surface shows against the approved record — question count, country coverage, company name, product roster, taglines.
  3. Flags every conflict — when a surface shows 4,000+ and the approved value is 5,589, that is drift.
  4. Sends the report — one table naming every conflict, where it appears, and what the approved value is.

Pre-exhibition audit — Oman Health 2026 is in two weeks

Before Oman Health 2026 (Oct 15–18), the brain runs a pre-exhibition audit so Nasim can reprint anything that drifted. Offline surfaces are harder to update — once a brochure is printed, it stays printed.

What it checks

  • Exhibition booth brochure (EN and AR)
  • Product brochures handed out at the booth
  • Deck slides shown on the booth screen
  • Taglines on banners and signage

What it reports

  • Which facts are drifting
  • Which brochures need reprinting
  • Which slides need updating
  • How many days until the event

The audit runs one week before the event (Oct 8). That gives Nasim seven days to reprint, ship, and verify. See offline surfaces.
