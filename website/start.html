Week one — kickoff to first drift report

Day 1 — Kickoff

Hiwa interviews Dr. Abdullah and Nasim. Three questions:

  1. Which surfaces matter? (websites, app stores, brochures, decks)
  2. What are the facts we need to track? (question count, country coverage, product roster, taglines, company name)
  3. Who approves what? (Dr. Abdullah approves facts, Nasim owns publishing)

Deliverable: List of surfaces the brain will watch, list of facts it will track.

Days 2-4 — Build the brain

Hiwa reads every surface, records every fact, and flags every conflict. Each fact is written as a Markdown file with the source and verification date attached.

Example: bayan.edu.om shows "4,000+" questions, medad.om shows "3,550", the App Store shows "5,500+", and the verified count is 5,589 — that is four conflicts, and the brain flags all four.

Deliverable: Draft fact record sent to Dr. Abdullah for approval.

Day 5 — Approval

Dr. Abdullah reviews the draft record and approves or corrects each fact. His approval is attached to every record — who approved it, when, and what the exact wording is.

Deliverable: Approved fact record, locked in as the brain's source of truth.

Day 6 — First drift report

The brain runs the first drift check across every surface and sends the report to Dr. Abdullah and Nasim. The report is a plain table: which facts are drifting, where they appear, and what the approved value is.

Deliverable: First drift report, sent by email.

Day 7 — Handoff

Hiwa hands off the brain to Dr. Abdullah and Nasim. You decide whether to continue with the optional retainer (weekly drift checks, $200/mo) or stop here and keep everything you paid for.

Deliverable: The brain, the first drift report, and the handoff call.

What we need from you

From Dr. Abdullah

  • One hour on day 1 (kickoff interview)
  • One hour on day 5 (approve the draft record)
  • Which facts you want the brain to track
  • Which surfaces matter (we can discover most of them, but you know which ones are critical)

From Nasim

  • One hour on day 1 (kickoff interview)
  • List of surfaces the brain should watch
  • Access to brochure PDFs and deck files (we read them, we do not edit them)
  • Confirmation: which email address should receive the weekly drift report

No access to private systems. The brain reads only public surfaces — bayan.edu.om, medad.om, bayanai.tech, the App Store, Google Play, and any brochure PDFs you share. It does not access learner data, exam results, or internal systems. See what it can and can't see.

Handoff — what you get, what you decide

What you get at handoff

  • The brain — every approved fact in plain Markdown files, yours to keep
  • The first drift report — which surfaces are conflicting right now
  • Bilingual ask interface — ask the brain questions in English or Arabic
  • Time-saved report — how many hours the brain saved this week by checking surfaces automatically
  • Pre-exhibition audit scheduled — one week before Oman Health 2026 (Oct 15-18)

Option 1: Continue with the retainer

$200/mo, cancel anytime. Weekly drift reports keep coming every Sunday at 9am Oman time. Adding new surfaces costs nothing extra. Pre-exhibition audits before every event.

When this makes sense: You are going to Oman Health 2026, you plan to add more surfaces, or Nasim is spending more than 3 hours per week checking surfaces manually.

Option 2: Stop here and keep everything

No ongoing cost. You keep the brain, the first drift report, and every file Hiwa built. The weekly drift check stops, but the facts stay in the files and you can ask Hiwa to run a one-off check before any event.

When this makes sense: You want the brain for Oman Health 2026 but do not need weekly checks after that, or you are not sure yet and want to wait.

You decide at handoff. There is no pressure, no minimum term, and no penalty for stopping. See pricing details.
