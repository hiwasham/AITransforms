App stores

The surfaces you can't quietly correct

Store listings are reviewed, cached and scraped. They are the hardest surface to fix after the fact, which makes them the best argument for getting the record right first.

What store listings carry

Question counts, country counts, product taglines, pricing tiers, and the publisher name — every one of which currently has more than one value somewhere in Bayan's material.

What changes at Stage 0

Before a listing is updated, the figures come from the approved record and the Arabic wording comes from the same record. Nasim still writes and submits the listing — the brain only supplies the numbers and the approved phrasing.

Store listings are checked as published text. The brain does not hold store credentials and cannot submit a listing change — see access.

Mechanics

How a surface gets checked

  1. Read the surface as a visitor sees it

    Public pages, public listings, the PDF as it ships. No logins, no scraping behind authentication.

  2. Pull out every claimable figure

    Counts, prices, country numbers, product status, entity and company names, taglines.

  3. Compare against the approved record

    Match, mismatch, or not-in-record. A not-in-record figure is reported as unverified rather than assumed correct.

  4. Report the mismatch with the surface named

    "This page says X, the record says Y" — specific enough for Nasim to fix without investigating.

The brain never edits a surface. It produces a list of mismatches; a person makes the change. That boundary is deliberate — see Nasim's lane.
