Adding a new surface

When Bayan launches a new product site, updates the brochure, or adds a landing page, the brain starts watching it. No extra charge — surfaces are not priced per-unit.

How it works

  1. Nasim tells Hiwa the new surface exists — the URL or a PDF of the brochure.
  2. Hiwa adds it to the brain's watch list and runs the first drift check.
  3. From that point forward, the brain checks it weekly alongside every other surface.

Adding a surface takes less than 10 minutes. It is covered under the $200/mo drift-guard retainer at Stage 2 — no extra setup fee, no per-surface charge. See optional retainer.
