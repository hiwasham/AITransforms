What the brain can and can't see, and what it will never claim

The brain holds facts about Bayan's products — not the products themselves. It knows that Bayan has 5,589 questions covering 55+ countries, and that is all it knows about them. No question text, no learner data, no exam results, nothing clinical.

What the brain holds and what it doesn't

The brain holds

  • Product names and taglines
  • Question count: 5,589
  • Country coverage: 55+
  • Company name and legal entity
  • Which products are live and which are in development
  • App Store and Google Play metadata

The brain does not hold

  • Question text or explanations
  • Learner accounts or usage data
  • Exam results or pass rates
  • Clinical content or medical guidelines
  • Revenue, conversion, or growth figures
  • Internal team discussions or decisions

This is a product brain, not a knowledge brain. It knows about Bayan's products — their names, their figures, where they are published — but it does not hold the products themselves. There is no copy of the question bank inside it, no learner data, no exam content.

What the brain will never claim — hard content locks

These are not "we'll try not to" limits. These are hard content locks — the brain is built so it cannot make these claims, even if asked.

Claim type Why it's locked What the brain says instead
Pass rates or exam outcomes The brain holds no clinical content, no learner data, and no exam results "I don't have approved outcome data"
Revenue or conversion lift Bayan is pre-revenue and there is no instrumented funnel "Bayan has no instrumented funnel — I can report time saved and drift eliminated, but not revenue"
Clinical guidance or medical advice The brain is not a medical tool and holds no clinical content "I'm a product brain, not a clinical tool — ask [clinician name]"
Learner performance or engagement The brain holds no learner accounts or usage data "I don't have learner data"
Anything marked 'confirm with team' The figure exists on a live surface but Dr. Abdullah has not approved it Shows the figure with a CONFIRM WITH TEAM badge

If the brain doesn't have an approved fact, it says so. It does not round, approximate, or invent a range. When it doesn't know, it names who to ask. See how it handles unknowns.

Who owns what — three lanes that don't move

Dr. Abdullah Approves

Approves every fact the brain will say. The brain does not decide what is true — Dr. Abdullah does.

His approval is attached to every fact.

Nasim Publishes

Owns every publishable surface. The brain gives her the approved wording — she decides what to publish and when.

Nothing publishable moves without Nasim.

Hiwa Maintains

Builds and maintains the brain. Adds new surfaces when Nasim asks. Runs the weekly drift check.

Does not approve facts or publish surfaces.

These lanes are hard boundaries in the build, not courtesies. The brain cannot approve a fact — only Dr. Abdullah can. The brain cannot publish a surface — only Nasim can. See how the team works.

How the brain handles unknowns — three paths

Path 1: Approved fact exists

Question: "How many questions does Bayan have?"

Brain: "5,589 questions, approved by Dr. Abdullah on 2026-09-20."

Path 2: Fact exists on a live surface, not yet approved

Question: "How many questions does Medad have?"

Brain: "medad.om shows 3,550 — but this is unverified. CONFIRM WITH TEAM"

Path 3: No approved fact, no live figure

Question: "What is Bayan's revenue?"

Brain: "I don't have an approved fact for this. Ask Dr. Abdullah or Nasim."

The brain does not guess. When it doesn't know, it says so and names who to ask. It does not round figures, invent ranges, or approximate. See the question-count example.

Technical limits — what the brain cannot do

The brain cannot

  • Edit surfaces — it reports drift, Nasim updates
  • Approve facts — only Dr. Abdullah can
  • Access private systems — it reads public surfaces only
  • See learner data or exam results
  • Make decisions — it reports, you decide

The brain can

  • Check every surface weekly for drift
  • Answer questions in English and Arabic
  • Run pre-exhibition audits before events
  • Report time saved by not checking surfaces manually
  • Add new surfaces when Nasim asks

The brain is read-only. It reads public surfaces, compares them to the approved record, and reports conflicts. It does not edit anything. See the surfaces it watches.

No lock-in — what you keep if you stop

Every fact lives in a Markdown file you can open in any text editor. If you stop working with us, you keep everything and nothing breaks.

What you keep

  • Every approved fact, in plain Markdown files
  • Every version history — who approved what, when
  • Every drift report sent during the engagement
  • The list of surfaces the brain was watching

What stops working

  • The weekly drift check — no one runs it anymore
  • The bilingual ask interface — but the facts stay in the files
  • Pre-exhibition audits — you'd run them manually

Plain files, not a database. Every fact is a Markdown file. No export step, no conversion, no vendor lock. Just files. See the file structure.
