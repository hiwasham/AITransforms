The loop

How a value becomes an approved fact

  1. A conflict is found

    Either the weekly check spots two surfaces disagreeing, or someone asks a question the record can't answer confidently.

  2. It arrives as a decision, not a task

    You see the values in circulation, where each one appears, and a recommendation. The JournalReady price move from $19 / $144 to $4.99 / $29.99 is a live example — confirm with team until you say which is current.

  3. You approve one value

    One line. The record now carries that figure, your name, and the date.

  4. Everyone answers from it

    Booth staff, Nasim's copy, the brochure draft, the Arabic wording — all from the same approved record, in both languages.

  5. Drift that reappears is flagged, not re-litigated

    If a surface goes back to 4,000+ next quarter, the brain names the surface. You don't re-decide the fact.

Boundary

Nothing publishable moves without Nasim

This is a hard line in the build, not a courtesy.

The brain does

  • Answer a question with the approved figure and its source
  • Hand Nasim approved EN and AR wording she can use as-is
  • Tell her which live surfaces currently contradict the record
  • Say plainly when a fact is unverified instead of guessing

The brain does not

  • Publish, post, schedule or send anything
  • Edit a live site, an app-store listing or a brochure
  • Write public copy that skips her review
  • Replace clinician review on anything clinical
